# 9. Legal Red Lines Ordinary People Often Cross

This section does two sums: your personal freedom, and your money. The things below are ones ordinary people often think are "no big deal," but they can lead to detention and fines, and in serious cases to prison. Each entry gives the specific figures from the statute, along with cases published through official channels. The Public Security Administration Punishments Law cited here is the 2025 revision, which took effect on January 1, 2026, and its article numbers differ from the old law. The Criminal Law cited is the consolidated text reprinted by the Beijing Public Security Traffic Management Bureau, which states that it is "amended under Criminal Law Amendment (XI)." The body text notes the relevant amendment for each article.

Entries in this section are grouped by theme below. Item numbers are in parentheses.

**Posting and forwarding online**: do not forward unverified disaster news (item 1), do not forward content that insults heroes and martyrs (item 2), close overseas websites after viewing and do not pass their content on (item 3), and do not post explicit videos in groups (item 4).

**Helping others collect money, loan fraud, and insurance fraud**: do not rent out your bank card to collect money for others (item 5), do not "package" documents to get loans (item 6), do not withdraw money from a bank card you found (item 7), claim offers only with your real identity (item 8), do not lend out your ID card (item 16), and do not fake accidents to claim insurance (item 21).

**Everyday small matters**: do not throw anything out of windows or balconies (item 9), do not buy replica guns online (item 10), register drones before flying them (item 11), do not take a cut from card games or set them up (item 13), do not eat wild animals (item 14), never electro-fish (item 27), and do not light fires for picnics at graves in forests or grasslands (item 28).

**Conflict and police**: do not detain a person to collect a debt (item 15), cooperate when police stop you (item 17), count the cost before you hit someone (item 19), and do not carry batons or stun guns for self-defense (item 26).

**Sex, children, and the body**: do not secretly film other people (item 12), do not have sex with anyone under 14 (item 18), if you cannot raise a child, go through civil affairs registration (item 20), do not sell your organs (item 22), and do not buy sex, which by default leads to detention (item 23).

**Copyright and fan works**: translating and reposting pirated e-books (item 24), and selling fan fiction for money without first obtaining permission (item 25).

**Exams**: do not hire someone to take an exam for you, and do not buy or sell answers (item 29).

### 1. Don't forward unverified disaster, epidemic, or police-alert messages in groups, don't photoshop images, and don't use AI to fake scene photos
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=freedom -->
- Cost: No money cost. Before you forward a message, check whether an official agency issued it. If you are not sure, don't forward it, and don't edit the image or add "reportedly." The hard part is resisting the urge to be the first person to raise the alarm.
- Plain language: Spreading a disaster, epidemic, or police-alert rumor is punished the same as making it up, if you know it is false. Forwarding one you did not invent still brings 5 to 10 days' detention and a fine of up to 1,000 yuan. Serious disruption can mean up to 3 years in prison, or 3 to 7 years if it causes serious harm.
- Benefit: Deliberately spreading rumors that falsely report danger, epidemics, disasters, or police matters, and so disturbing public order, is punished with 5 to 10 days' administrative detention. A fine of up to 1,000 yuan can be added. The lighter tier is detention of under 5 days, or a fine of up to 1,000 yuan. Making up a false report of danger, epidemic, disaster, or police matters and spreading it online can bring up to 3 years in prison. Knowingly passing on a known fake also falls in this tier. The precondition is that it seriously disrupts social order, which is the statute's wording. Serious consequences bring 3 to 7 years (nationwide).
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2025). 治安管理处罚法（2025 年修订，第二十九条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2015). 刑法修正案（九）（第三十二条，即刑法第二百九十一条之一第二款）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180205_364562.shtml>；全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百九十一条之一）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>（北京市公安交通管理局转载）
- Notes: China only: Knowingly forwarding a rumor is treated the same as making it up, and police use the same article for both. "I only forwarded it" is not a defense. Photoshopped and AI-generated fake scene photos count as making things up too. The Ministry of Public Security published several batches of typical online rumor cases involving disasters and dangers in 2025. The ministry's website could not be opened while this section was being written, so no specific cases are cited.

### 2. Don't post or forward images, memes, or comments that insult heroes and martyrs, and don't joke about the national flag or anthem
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=freedom -->
- Cost: No money cost. Don't comment on, edit, or forward content involving heroes and martyrs, the national flag, the national emblem, or the national anthem. The hard part is holding back when someone in a group jokes about it and you feel like adding a comment.
- Plain language: Mocking heroes and martyrs, the flag, or the national anthem can bring 5 to 10 days' detention or a fine of 1,000 to 3,000 yuan. Worse cases bring 10 to 15 days. Serious cases are crimes, punishable by up to 3 years in prison. A group chat of a few hundred people counts as a public place.
- Benefit: Using insults or fabricated facts to infringe the name, portrait, reputation, or honor of heroes and martyrs, where this harms the public interest, is punished with 5 to 10 days' detention or a fine of 1,000 to 3,000 yuan. Desecrating or denying the deeds and spirit of heroes, or making and spreading material that glorifies wars of aggression and disturbs public order, also falls in this tier. The heavier tier is 10 to 15 days' detention and a fine of up to 5,000 yuan. Reaching "serious circumstances" makes it a crime, punishable by up to 3 years in prison. Publicly insulting the national flag or emblem, or altering the anthem's lyrics or music, or singing it in a distorted or derogatory way, also carries up to 3 years for serious cases. Official case: Xiao X posted two messages in a 499-member village WeChat group, which is a group chat app used across China, insulting agricultural scientist Yuan Longping. He was sentenced to 6 months of control. Control is a penalty that keeps you out of prison but restricts your movements. Qiu X used a Weibo account with over 2.5 million followers, a Chinese microblogging platform, to post two items distorting the deeds of border guards. He was sentenced to 8 months and ordered to publicly apologize through a national media outlet (nationwide).
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2025). 治安管理处罚法（2025 年修订，第三十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2018). 英雄烈士保护法（第二十二、二十六条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/gyssshmhsh/201912/t20191202_440079.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2020). 刑法修正案（十一）（第三十五条，即刑法第二百九十九条之一）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/202012/t20201227_503682.shtml>；全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第二百九十九条、第二百九十九条之一）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院 (2022). 涉英烈权益保护十大典型案例（案例二、案例三）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/382301.html>
- Notes: China only: This article of the Public Security Administration Punishments Law was added in the 2025 revision. Before that, such acts could only be handled under the Criminal Law, or through civil public-interest lawsuits brought by the procuratorate. In the courts' view, a group chat of a few hundred people counts as a public setting.

### 3. After viewing content on overseas websites, close the page. Don't take screenshots, forward it, move it into groups, or pile on in the comments
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=freedom -->
- Cost: No money cost. Once the page is closed, the matter is over. Don't save it, forward it, or move it to domestic platforms. Don't use an account tied to your real identity to post political content on overseas platforms over time. Don't accept fees or funding from overseas bodies, organizations, or individuals. The hard part is resisting the urge to screenshot or share something that catches your eye.
- Plain language: These laws punish spreading material, not viewing it. But forwarding it, moving it into a group, or joining in the comments all count as spreading when a case is handled. Inciting subversion or separatism carries up to 5 years, and more than 5 years for ringleaders. Keeping terrorist or extremist material, even without sharing it, can also be a crime.
- Benefit: Using rumors, defamation, or other means to incite others to overthrow state power or the socialist system carries up to 5 years in prison. Ringleaders, or those whose crimes are grave, face more than 5 years, up to 15 years. Inciting secession or undermining national unity carries the same range. Committing either act in collusion with overseas institutions, organizations, or individuals is punished more severely. Funding others to do these things, whether the funding comes from domestic or overseas bodies or individuals, carries up to 5 years for the person directly responsible, and more than 5 years in serious cases. Stealing, spying on, buying, or illegally providing state secrets or intelligence for overseas institutions, organizations, or people carries 5 to 10 years. In the especially serious cases of that item, the sentence is over 10 years or life imprisonment. In lighter cases, it is up to 5 years. Crimes that endanger national security always carry an added 1 to 5 years of deprivation of political rights, which removes the right to vote and to hold public office during that time. Confiscation of personal property may also be imposed. There are administrative penalties too. No individual or organization may use the internet to engage in activities that endanger national security, honor, or interests. No one may incite subversion of state power or the socialist system, or incite splitting the country or undermining national unity. Those who publish or transmit such information are punished under the relevant laws and regulations (nationwide). Another category is a crime even without spreading. Knowingly and illegally possessing material that promotes terrorism or extremism can be enough for a conviction if the case is serious. This covers books, audio and video materials, or other items. The penalty is up to 3 years' fixed-term imprisonment, criminal detention (short-term custody), or control (no custody, but restricted movement). A fine may be imposed alongside the sentence or instead of it. The basis is Article 120(6) of the Criminal Law, added by Criminal Law Amendment (IX).
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第四十五、五十五、五十六、一百零三、一百零五、一百零六、一百零七、一百一十一、一百一十三条，这几条未经修正案修改）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2025). 网络安全法（2025 年修正，2026 年 1 月 1 日施行，第十三条第二款、第七十一条第一项）. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-12/29/c_1768735112911946.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2015). 刑法修正案（九）（新增第一百二十条之六）. 国家信访局转载全文 <https://www.gjxfj.gov.cn/gjxfj/fgwj/flfg/webinfo/2017/03/1601761496614423.htm>
- Notes: China only: The laws cited here punish inciting others, spreading material outward, and providing it to others. Merely viewing it is not covered. Terrorist and extremist material is the exception the statute itself names: keeping it is enough for a conviction (Article 120(6)). So never save it. Once you have seen it, stop there: don't take screenshots, save it, forward it, comment on it, or tag others to share it. Forwarding and joining in the comments both count as spreading in a case, and "I only forwarded it" is no defense. Collusion with overseas institutions, organizations, or individuals is punished more severely. If you are unsure whether something is true, do not pass it on at all; see item 1 of this section (do not forward unverified messages). For how the penalty for circumvention tools works, see section 11, item 11. This kind of content brings no real benefit when shared, but the cost is prison time, deprivation of political rights, and confiscation of property. This book did not obtain official judgments that can be checked, so it cites only the statutes and no cases.

### 4. Keep explicit videos to yourself. Don't post them in groups, don't sell "resource" packs, and don't create groups for them
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=high scope=freedom -->
- Cost: No money cost. Don't forward them, don't save them in group files, and don't become the owner or an admin of such a group.
- Plain language: Watching them privately is not covered by the penalties. Posting them in a group is spreading them online, and the law does not require repeated posts. The penalty is 10 to 15 days' detention and a fine of up to 5,000 yuan, and up to 2 years in prison for serious cases. Selling "resources" for profit carries up to 3 years, and in the most serious cases, life imprisonment. Creating a group of 30 or more people for spreading such material makes the creator, admins, and main posters liable for a crime.
- Benefit: Spreading obscene information online carries 10 to 15 days' detention and a fine of up to 5,000 yuan. The law does not say how many times it must be sent. Lighter cases carry detention of under 5 days, or a fine of 1,000 to 3,000 yuan. If the content involves minors, the penalty is heavier. Spreading obscene material to the level of "serious circumstances" carries up to 2 years in prison. Spreading it for profit carries up to 3 years plus a fine. Serious cases within that item carry 3 to 10 years, and especially serious cases carry over 10 years or life imprisonment. Creating a group mainly used to spread obscene electronic information, with 30 or more members, is treated as a crime, and so is a case with serious consequences. The creator of such a group, its administrators, and the people who mainly post to it are all convicted of spreading obscene material (nationwide).
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2025). 治安管理处罚法（2025 年修订，第五十条第一款第五项、第八十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>；全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第三百六十三、三百六十四条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2010). 关于办理利用互联网、移动通讯终端、声讯台制作、复制、出版、贩卖、传播淫秽电子信息刑事案件具体应用法律若干问题的解释（二）（第三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201002/t20100204_25001.shtml>
- Notes: China only: Both laws punish "spreading." Private viewing is not covered. Posting in a group is spreading online. The rules set no number of posts, so a single post falls within the wording; this is an inference from the text. Elsewhere in the same law, the item that penalizes sending harassing messages specifically requires "repeated sending," but this item does not. The threshold for "serious" circumstances is set by judicial interpretation, and this section has not verified the specific figures.

### 5. Refuse "part-time jobs" that ask you to use your own card to receive, withdraw, or transfer money, however much they offer
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=high scope=freedom -->
- Cost: No money cost. Give up this kind of work: "a few hundred yuan a day, just bring your card to the bank."
- Plain language: This kind of job pays a few hundred yuan but leaves you with a criminal record. One man brought his ID and a bank card to a designated place, helped with transfers and withdrawals, and earned over 500 yuan. Prosecutors did not charge him, but police gave him 10 days' detention and a 1,000 yuan fine. Another handed his own and others' bank and SIM cards to a gang for collecting and withdrawing money. He was sentenced to 2 years and 3 months and fined 22,000 yuan.
- Benefit: Knowingly helping to move, buy, sell, or otherwise conceal money or goods that come from crime carries up to 3 years in prison, plus a fine (a fine may be imposed along with the sentence, or on its own). Official case: Wang X, following instructions, brought his ID and one bank card to a designated place, helped with transfers and withdrawals, and earned over 500 yuan. Prosecutors did not charge him. Police detained him for 10 days under the Anti-Telecom and Online Fraud Law and fined him 1,000 yuan. Man X handed bank cards and SIM cards to a gang for collecting, transferring, and withdrawing money. He was sentenced to 2 years and 3 months and fined 22,000 yuan (nationwide). Converting the money into virtual currency such as Tether (a stablecoin) and transferring it out is the same offense. An X and two others, following instructions from a telecom-fraud ring, used their own bank cards to receive money. They bought virtual currency on trading platforms and transferred it to accounts the ring named, keeping a commission. Investigators found that over 500,000 yuan of victims' money had been defrauded. The three were each sentenced for concealing criminal proceeds to between 2 years and 8 months and 3 years and 3 months, and each fined between 30,000 and 40,000 yuan (Tongzhou District, Beijing).
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第三百一十二条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民检察院 (2023). 检察机关依法惩治电信网络诈骗及其关联犯罪典型案例（案例九）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202311/t20231130_635188.shtml>；最高人民法院、最高人民检察院 (2025). 依法惩治掩饰、隐瞒犯罪所得、犯罪所得收益犯罪典型案例（案例一、五）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202508/t20250825_704514.shtml>
- Notes: China only: For the consequences of lending your bank card or doing "running scores" (passing other people's money through your account for a commission), see section 8, item 8 (lending your bank card). This item covers doing the withdrawals and transfers yourself. Converting the money into virtual currency and moving it out is the same matter. For the losses from buying crypto yourself, see section 5, item 45 (don't buy virtual currency). "I didn't know where the money came from" is not always accepted. When commenting on Man X's case, the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate wrote that courts look at what information you had, how the money was converted and moved, whether the trades and accounts were abnormal, and then weigh this together with your experience and your relationship to upstream parties to judge what you knew. The person in the case above who was not charged was still detained for 10 days.

### 6. If someone invites you to "package" documents for a loan in exchange for a cut of the loan amount, refuse every time
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=high scope=freedom -->
- Cost: No money cost. Refuse outright. Don't lend your ID or your personal credit record to anyone, don't sign a loan contract you haven't read, and don't go to a bank in someone else's place to sign in person.
- Plain language: Helping someone "package" a loan application and taking a cut is treated as fraud. One ring of 126 people passed off unemployed people as "good customers" and fraudulently obtained over 30 million yuan from more than 80 bank branches. Those who signed the loan papers received 30 percent of each loan as a commission. Of the 80 people sentenced at first instance, 76 received 1 year and 4 months to 6 years and 6 months. Whoever's name the loan is in owns the debt.
- Benefit: Obtaining loans, bill acceptances, letters of credit, guarantees, and similar credit from banks or other financial institutions by deception, and causing major losses to them, carries up to 3 years in prison, plus a fine (a fine may be imposed along with the sentence, or on its own). Losses that are especially major, or other especially serious circumstances, carry 3 to 7 years plus a fine. Police open a case when direct economic losses reach 500,000 yuan. Where someone never intended to repay and obtains a loan by using a fabricated investment project, fake economic contracts, fake supporting documents, or fake property certificates, it is loan fraud. Under that item, a case is opened at 50,000 yuan. The penalty is up to 5 years plus a fine of 20,000 to 200,000 yuan. Large amounts or other serious circumstances carry 5 to 10 years, and especially large amounts carry over 10 years up to life imprisonment. Forging, altering, or trading in official documents, certificates, or seals of state organs carries up to 3 years, and 3 to 10 years in serious cases. Official case: a ring of 126 people across 16 provinces and municipalities passed off unemployed people as "good customers" and obtained over 30 million yuan in loans from more than 80 bank branches. The "white-card" borrowers (people with no credit history) who took out the loans received 30 percent of the loan amount as commission. Prosecutors charged 105 white-card borrowers. Of the 80 people already sentenced at first instance in 2026, 76 were convicted of loan fraud and received 1 year and 4 months to 6 years and 6 months, plus fines. Four others, who also withdrew cash for the ring, were convicted of both loan fraud and money laundering, with the sentences combined. These four received 2 years and 4 months to 7 years and 6 months (nationwide). The loan-fraud article cited here follows the text of Amendment (XI), in effect since March 2021.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2020). 刑法修正案（十一）（第十一条，修改刑法第一百七十五条之一）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202012/t20201227_503700.shtml>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第八十八、一百九十三、二百八十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；最高人民检察院、公安部 (2022). 关于公安机关管辖的刑事案件立案追诉标准的规定（二）（第二十二、四十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/xwfbh/wsfbt/202204/t20220429_555906.shtml>；最高人民检察院 (2026). 骗贷流水线背后藏洗钱黑手（检察日报）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202604/t20260428_726494.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2012). 出境入境管理法（第十二条）. <https://www.nia.gov.cn/n741440/n741547/c1013311/content.html>（国家移民管理局）；全国人大常委会 (2023 修正). 民事诉讼法（第二百六十六条）. <https://amr.guizhou.gov.cn/zwgk/xxgkml/jcxxgk/zcfg/fl/202401/t20240129_83645867.html>（贵州省市场监管局转载）
- Notes: China only: "I meant to repay" was not accepted in this case. Prosecutors established that these people had no jobs and no fixed assets, so they could not repay, and they spent the money they received. From that they concluded that they never intended to repay. Those who withdrew cash for the ring may also face a separate money-laundering charge. Whoever's name the loan is in, and whoever signed the contract, is the borrower. Even where no crime is involved, the debt and the entry on your credit record are yours. Fleeing abroad does not end it: once a case is filed, you can be pursued for years. Anyone serving a sentence, or who is a suspect or defendant in a criminal case, may not leave the country. Courts can bar exit where a civil case is unresolved, and can restrict exit for judgment debtors who fail to comply. For cash withdrawals and transfers for others, see item 5 of this section ("part-time jobs" that ask you to use your own card). For how to discover that someone has taken a loan in your name, see section 8, item 9.

### 7. Don't take money from a bank card you found. If you find a phone or wallet, contact the owner or hand it to the police. Don't pocket it
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=high scope=freedom -->
- Cost: No money cost, and only a few minutes. Make a phone call to find the owner, or take the item to a police station.
- Plain language: Keeping a found item for yourself, and refusing to hand it back once its value is "relatively large," can bring up to 2 years in prison, or 2 to 5 years for a large amount. Using a found bank card at an ATM is treated more severely, as misusing someone else's credit card. For larger amounts that carries up to 5 years plus a fine. Police open a case for cash withdrawals without waiting for the owner to file a complaint.
- Benefit: Found property must be returned to its owner. You must notify the owner promptly so they can collect it, or hand it over to the public security authorities or another department. Keeping someone's lost property for yourself, when the amount is "relatively large" and you refuse to give it back, is embezzlement, punishable by up to 2 years in prison. A large amount carries 2 to 5 years. Using someone else's found credit card at an ATM counts as "misusing another person's credit card." Under the Criminal Law, a "credit card" is defined by a 2004 Standing Committee interpretation as an electronic payment card issued by a bank or other financial institution that has some or all of the following functions: consumer payments, credit loans, transfer settlement, and cash withdrawal. So an ordinary bank card that can withdraw cash is included. Where the conduct reaches the level of a crime, it is treated as credit-card fraud: a larger amount carries up to 5 years and a fine of 20,000 to 200,000 yuan. Large amounts, or other serious circumstances, carry 5 to 10 years and a fine of 50,000 to 500,000 yuan (nationwide).
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第三百一十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；全国人大 (2020). 刑法（根据刑法修正案（十一）修正，第一百九十六、二百七十条）. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/11033925/index.html>；最高人民检察院 (2008). 关于拾得他人信用卡并在自动柜员机（ATM 机）上使用的行为如何定性问题的批复. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/flfg/sfjs/201208/t20120830_47871.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2004). 关于《中华人民共和国刑法》有关信用卡规定的解释. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/zgrdw/npc/xinwen/lfgz/zxfl/2004-12/30/content_334756.htm>
- Notes: China only: Embezzlement is an offense that proceeds only on complaint. The owner must sue in court, and the police do not act on their own. Withdrawing cash with a found card is different: the owner does not need to file a complaint, and the police open a case directly. What counts as "relatively large" is set by judicial interpretation, which this section did not verify. After an item has been handed to the authorities, the owner who comes to collect it must pay storage costs (Civil Code, Article 317).

### 8. Claim offers with your real identity, one account for one claim. Don't buy virtual numbers to bulk-register extra accounts, and don't claim repeatedly by exploiting loopholes
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=freedom -->
- Cost: No money cost. Give up the work in "free-stuff task groups." If you find a loophole in a platform, leave it alone. The hard part is resisting the urge to treat a discount as income you can copy in bulk.
- Plain language: Claiming one offer per account, with your real identity and used on yourself, is not punishable. Registering fake users in bulk, or placing and taking your own orders to claim more, is fraud. At the provincial threshold of 3,000 to 10,000 yuan, fraud carries up to 3 years. At 30,000 to 100,000 yuan, it carries 3 to 10 years. One rider used virtual numbers costing 0.1 yuan each to claim coupons and resold the goods, cashing out over 90,000 yuan. Twelve co-defendants were convicted.

For electrofishing, poisoning or dynamite fishing: confiscation of the gear, catch and illegal gains, and a fine of up to 200,000 yuan. In serious cases, a fine of 200,000 to 2 million yuan. Fishing in violation of closed areas or closed seasons, or using banned gear: a fine of up to 200,000 yuan.
