# 15. Renting and buying a home

Scope: money. The easiest place to lose money when renting is the deposit and the agency. The biggest cost of buying a home is mortgage interest, plus things the seller knows and you don't. The last entries of this section cover buying: which account the money should go into, when the deposit and purchase payments can be recovered, and what to do if the handover is late. They also cover how to guard against a seller selling the same flat twice, and how to negotiate the mortgage rate down. The last two cover after you move in: a property management company may not cut off water or power to pressure you into paying the property fee, and a leak in a new home within the warranty period should be fixed by the developer.

### 1. Write the deposit amount, refund timing and deduction conditions into the rental contract
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. Ten extra minutes when signing. The hard part is raising it in front of the landlord.
- Plain language: Write the deposit amount, when it is refunded, and when it can be deducted into the contract before you sign. A deduction reason that is not in the contract cannot be used by the landlord to keep your deposit when you move out.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation says it clearly: "A lessor who collects a deposit shall agree in the housing lease contract on the deposit amount, the refund time, and the circumstances under which the deposit may be deducted. Apart from the circumstances agreed in the housing lease contract, a lessor may not deduct the deposit without good reason." Three things must be in the contract: how much deposit you pay, when it is returned, and when it can be deducted. A reason not written in the contract cannot be used by the landlord to keep your money.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: China only: On the day you move out, photograph and video the flat together with the landlord. Record the water, electricity and gas meter readings, and photograph the walls and floor too. If your deposit is deducted without reason, first complain to the local housing rental administration. Small amounts can go to the small-claims court, see section 8. For renting a shop front to trade, whether you can open it and whether catering can be licensed, see section 12, item 27 (renting a shop to open a business).

### 2. If the landlord cuts off water or power, changes the locks, or threatens you at home, call the police first and keep evidence: the rules ban using these methods to force you out
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. A phone call to the police, which can be done on the spot.
- Plain language: A landlord may not force you to end the contract or move out by cutting off water or power, changing the locks, or threatening you at home. The rules ban this. If it happens, call the police first and keep the evidence. Do not confront them yourself.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation says it clearly: "A lessor may not use violence, threats or other illegal means to force a lessee to terminate the housing lease contract or to vacate the leased housing." Vacate means move out. If this happens, call the police first and keep the evidence. Do not fight back yourself.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: China only: Calling the police creates a record of the police visit. If you later claim compensation for losses, that record is evidence. Also notify the local housing rental administration.

### 3. Agencies may not collect and pay out rent and deposits on your behalf; pay the landlord directly
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. Check the payee before paying, which takes a few minutes. The hard part is holding firm when an agent tells you to transfer the money to it.
- Plain language: By rule, rent and deposits may not be collected and passed on by an agency. Pay the landlord directly. Every time money passes through an agency's hands, it gets one more chance to run off with it. Before paying, check that the payee is the name on the property ownership certificate.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation lists collecting and paying out housing rent and deposits on someone else's behalf as a prohibited act for real estate brokerage firms. An agency may not collect your rent and deposit on the landlord's behalf. Each time money passes through an agency, it gets one more chance to run off with it.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: China only: Before paying, check that the payee is the name on the property ownership certificate. If it is not the owner, ask why and get a written authorization. An agency may only charge a brokerage fee for introducing the parties.

### 4. Before renting a long-term apartment, check its funds supervision account; do not pay a full year up front to get a small discount
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=some gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. Checking takes about fifteen minutes.
- Plain language: Paying a full year up front gets you only a small discount. If the company goes bust, your deposit and the rent for the remaining months may be lost together. If you also took out a rent loan, it is worse: the apartment is gone but you still repay the loan every month. Before signing, check whether the company has set up a publicly disclosed funds supervision account.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation requires rental enterprises that run sublease operations to "set up a housing rental funds supervision account and make it public." Sublease operation means the company leases the whole property from the landlord and sublets it to you. The small discount for paying a year up front does not make up for the risk that, if the company fails, your deposit and remaining rent are lost together. If you also took out a rent loan, the risk is greater.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Notes: China only: When signing, check whether the contract ties you to a loan. If an installment app paid the apartment a year's rent in one go and you repay the loan monthly, you must keep repaying even after the apartment company fails. Do not sign this kind of contract.

### 5. If the flat is sold during your lease, the lease still holds and you do not have to move
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: No money.
- Plain language: If the flat is sold during your lease, your lease still stands. The new owner must let you stay until the lease ends on its original terms. You do not have to move, and the rent does not need renegotiating. This applies only if you already live there lawfully, so keep your contract, transfer records and move-in date as evidence.
- Benefit: The Civil Code has a rule called "sale does not break the lease." Its text: "If ownership of leased property changes during the lease term, this does not affect the validity of the lease contract." If the house changes owner during the lease, the lease you signed still holds. The new owner must let you stay until the end of the term under the original contract.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第七百二十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: China only: This applies only if you have already moved in lawfully; the Civil Code's wording is lawful possession and use. Keep your contract, transfer records and move-in date as evidence.

### 6. Before renting, check the ownership certificate and any mortgage on the flat; pay every sum by bank transfer and note the purpose
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. Taking your ID to the property registry to check takes about half an hour. The hard part is getting the landlord to show the ownership certificate in person.
- Plain language: Before signing, ask the landlord to show their ID and the property ownership certificate. Then take your own ID to the real estate registry to check whether the flat is mortgaged or has been seized by a court. You can check even before signing a contract. A flat that was mortgaged or seized before you rented it may be auctioned and change owner, and then you cannot insist on staying. Pay everything by bank transfer and note the purpose, which leaves you a record.
- Benefit: Before signing, the landlord must show identity documents and the real estate ownership certificate. If there is no ownership certificate, the landlord must produce other documents proving the right to rent the property. The landlord must also cooperate with your checks on the flat's information; the regulation places this duty on the lessor. Before you sign, take your ID to the real estate registry in the city or county where the flat is. There you can check the basic details of the property, whether it has co-owners, whether there is a mortgage, prenotation or residence-right registration, and whether it has been seized by a court. After signing, you can check the registration results with the lease contract. This step matters because "sale does not break the lease" has two exceptions. If the flat was mortgaged to a bank before it was rented, and is later sold to enforce that mortgage, you cannot require the new owner to let you stay under the original contract. The same applies if a court had already seized the flat before it was rented. When a court auctions a flat and the lease would obstruct a mortgage registered earlier, the court first removes the lease and then auctions the flat. Conversely, if the flat was rented to you first and you moved in, and the landlord mortgaged it later, the mortgage does not affect your lease. If the flat is seized or its ownership is disputed so that you cannot live there, you may terminate the contract. If someone else asserts a right that stops you living there, you may ask to pay less rent or none. Paying every sum by transfer with a note such as "rent for [flat], [month]" is the author's experience: if a dispute arises, it shows that you paid and what the money was for.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2025). 住房租赁条例（国令第 812 号，2025 年 9 月 15 日施行）（第九条第一项：「向承租人出示身份证明材料、拟出租住房的不动产权属证书或者其他证明其具有合法出租权利的材料，并配合承租人依法查询、核实拟出租住房有关信息」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>；自然资源部 (2024 修正). 不动产登记资料查询暂行办法（第八、十九、二十、二十一条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11506/202408/content_6966482.html>；自然资源部 (2025). 关于修改《不动产登记资料查询暂行办法》的决定（自然资源部令第 18 号，第二十一条第三项改为「不动产是否存在抵押权登记、预告登记、异议登记或者居住权登记等情形」）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12546/202602/content_7057456.html>；全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第四百零五、七百二十三、七百二十四、七百二十五条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于修改《最高人民法院关于在民事审判工作中适用〈中华人民共和国工会法〉若干问题的解释》等二十七件民事类司法解释的决定（法释〔2020〕17 号，第二十一项第 6 点修改城镇房屋租赁合同解释原第二十条，条文顺序调整后为第十四条：「（一）房屋在出租前已设立抵押权，因抵押权人实现抵押权发生所有权变动的；（二）房屋在出租前已被人民法院依法查封的」）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282621.html>；最高人民法院 (2004). 关于人民法院民事执行中拍卖、变卖财产的规定（法释〔2004〕16 号，第三十一条第二款；法释〔2020〕21 号删去第五至七条后为第二十八条第二款）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/shenpan/xiangqing/713.html>；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于修改《最高人民法院关于人民法院扣押铁路运输货物若干问题的规定》等十八件执行类司法解释的决定（法释〔2020〕21 号）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282661.html>；证据留存与转账备注同理见第 8 节关于彩礼和借条的两条
- Notes: China only: Among the items you can check before signing, the owner's name is not included. So check the name on the ownership certificate against the landlord's ID in person. Have the owner present at signing; if they cannot attend, get a written power of attorney. If a secondary landlord sublets to you, you need the original landlord's written consent. For rights such as terminating the contract or paying less rent, if the landlord does not agree you must sue in court. A first-instance ordinary procedure is required to finish within six months, which can be extended; lawyer fees are generally borne by each party. The rule on transfer notes has no direct legal basis; it is the author's experience. The same checks can be made before buying a resale home, but after signing you should also register a preliminary registration; see item 14 (preliminary registration).

### 7. If an agency collects the sale price for a resale home, it must go through the dedicated trust account it holds at a bank
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is insisting on the dedicated account, which may mean arguing with the agency.
- Plain language: If an agency collects the sale money for you, it must go through its dedicated account at a bank. That account is supervised. The money must not go into the agency's own account or be transferred to an individual agent.
- Benefit: The rules state it clearly: if the parties agree that a real estate brokerage firm will collect and pay out transaction funds on their behalf, the funds must be transferred through the client transaction settlement dedicated deposit account that the firm opened at a bank. The purchase money goes into a supervised dedicated account, not the agency's own account and not any agent's private account.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 住房城乡建设部、国家发展改革委、人力资源社会保障部 (2011). 房地产经纪管理办法（令第 8 号，2016 年第 29 号令修改）第二十四条：「房地产交易当事人约定由房地产经纪机构代收代付交易资金的，应当通过房地产经纪机构在银行开设的客户交易结算资金专用存款账户划转交易资金。」第十八条「房地产经纪服务实行明码标价制度……在经营场所醒目位置标明房地产经纪服务项目、服务内容、收费标准」；第十九条「两家或者两家以上房地产经纪机构合作开展同一宗房地产经纪业务的，只能按照一宗业务收取佣金，不得向委托人增加收费」；第十七条代办贷款、代办房地产登记等其他服务「应当向委托人说明服务内容、收费标准等情况，经委托人同意后，另行签订合同」. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1918920.htm>
- Notes: China only: The purchase money is often hundreds of thousands to millions of yuan (RMB), the largest single sum in this book, so never send it to an agent through WeChat (a Chinese messaging and payment app). There are two other common ways to be overcharged. One is two agencies jointly handling the same deal; only one commission may be charged. The other is agencies arranging a mortgage or property registration for you; these are separate services that need a separate contract, and the fee must be disclosed in advance. Pay all sums by bank transfer with a note of purpose; see item 6 of this section. Where to pay for an off-plan purchase, see item 10 (presale funds supervision account).

### 8. Do not rent a partitioned flat: the smallest rental unit is the room as originally designed, and kitchens, bathrooms and balconies may not be lived in
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. The cost is that, on the same budget, you have to live farther away or in a smaller space.
- Plain language: By rule, the smallest rental unit is the room as originally designed. Kitchens, bathrooms, balconies and basement storage rooms may not be lived in. Also, during the lease the landlord may not raise the rent on their own whim.
- Benefit: The rules state clearly that the smallest rental unit is the room as originally designed; one room may not be partitioned into several rentals. The floor area per person must not fall below the local minimum standard. Kitchens, bathrooms, balconies and basement storage rooms may not be rented out for people to live in. During the lease, the lessor may not unilaterally raise the rent. A lessor who violates Article 8 may be ordered by the competent authority to correct it within a set time; if it does not, it may be fined 5,000 to 30,000 yuan (RMB).
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 住房和城乡建设部 (2010). 商品房屋租赁管理办法（令第 6 号）第八条：「出租住房的，应当以原设计的房间为最小出租单位，人均租住建筑面积不得低于当地人民政府规定的最低标准。厨房、卫生间、阳台和地下储藏室不得出租供人员居住。」第九条：「出租人应当按照合同约定履行房屋的维修义务并确保房屋和室内设施安全……房屋租赁合同期内，出租人不得单方面随意提高租金水平。」第二十二条：「违反本办法第八条规定的，由直辖市、市、县人民政府建设（房地产）主管部门责令限期改正，逾期不改正的，可处以5000元以上3万元以下罚款。」<http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1845070.htm>
- Notes: China only: Penalties for illegal partitions fall on the landlord. But correcting the violation means removing the partition, so tenants living inside can only move out; this is inferred from the rule, not stated in it. Deposits and rent already paid often cannot be recovered; this is the author's experience. Partitions are also unsafe: they often block escape routes, and many people share one room and one electricity line. When viewing a flat, check three things: whether the walls were built later, whether the room has a window, and whether the electricity supply can carry this many people.

### 9. City residents who want to live in the countryside should rent a rural house only, and not buy rural homestead land or houses on it
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. The trade-off is that you do not own the house, the lease term is at most 20 years at a time, and you must renegotiate with the landlord when it ends.
- Plain language: City residents may not buy rural homestead land, farmers' houses or so-called "small property rights" housing in the countryside; national documents ban it. A villager saying "just sign a contract" does not make it legal either. To live in the countryside long-term, sign a rental contract. A single lease may run up to 20 years, and you can renew it at the end.
- Benefit: A State Council General Office document states clearly: "Urban residents may not buy homestead land, farmers' houses or so-called 'small property rights' housing in rural areas." The same document says that once farmers have sold or rented out their houses, applications for new homestead land will not be approved. A 2019 document from the Central Leading Group Office for Rural Work and the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs repeated it: "Urban residents are strictly prohibited from buying homestead land in rural areas." The same document names the lawful route: urban residents may rent a farmhouse to live in or to run a business. The lease term may not exceed twenty years, and the parties may agree on renewal when it ends.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2007). 关于严格执行有关农村集体建设用地法律和政策的通知（国办发〔2007〕71 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_2395.htm>；中央农村工作领导小组办公室、农业农村部 (2019). 关于进一步加强农村宅基地管理的通知（中农发〔2019〕11 号）第五、六部分. <https://www.moa.gov.cn/govpublic/NCJJTZ/201909/t20190920_6328397.htm>
- Notes: China only: Homestead land is the land a village allocates to its own villagers to build houses on; it can only be allocated to people of that village. When renting a farmhouse, sign a written contract with the farm household stating the lease term, the rent, whether you may renovate or alter the house, and how renovation costs are settled at the end. The documents cap the lease term at 20 years, so avoid contracts for "50 years" or "70 years", and never pay several years' rent in one go. The documents also prohibit using homestead land for villas, courtyard estates or private clubhouses in the countryside, so do not pay villagers to "co-build" them.

### 10. Before buying a home off the plan, check the presale permit, and pay the deposit, down payment and loan only into the presale escrow account named in the contract
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: No money cost. Look at the presale permit before signing and check the account before paying. It takes a few minutes. The hard part is refusing when a salesperson asks you to transfer money to a different account.
- Plain language: An off-plan home is one sold before it is finished. Without a presale permit, the developer may not sell it or take a deposit. By rule, your down payment and loan must all go into an escrow account. The escrow is released only after the home is ready for delivery. Money paid to any other account is not covered by this protection.
- Benefit: In Chinese law, an off-plan home is called presale of commercial housing: the developer sells a home while it is still being built. In August 2026, the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, the Ministry of Natural Resources and the National Financial Regulatory Administration issued a notice stating: "All purchase funds paid by the buyer, including the down payment and personal housing loans, shall be deposited in full into the fund escrow account." The escrow is released only after the home passes completion acceptance and water, electricity, gas and heating are ready for delivery. The developer must state in the purchase contract the buyer's rights and duties regarding the escrow account. If the developer breaches the contract or cannot deliver on time, you may terminate the contract as agreed. The developer must refund the money under the contract and bear liability for breach. The People's Bank of China and the National Financial Regulatory Administration also require that personal housing loans for presale homes be paid by the bank directly into the project's presale escrow account. A 2022 notice from three agencies already said that deposits, down payments and mortgage loans "shall all be deposited directly into the escrow account." A developer without a presale permit may not presell, and may not take deposits through subscriptions, reservations, queue numbers or VIP cards. A presale contract signed without a presale permit is void by court ruling. If the permit is obtained before you sue, the contract may be found valid. Money paid outside the escrow account is not covered by this supervision. The same 2026 document also says that for projects sold as ready-built homes, after the developer obtains a construction permit, it may first take "a small deposit." That deposit is supervised by the housing fund supervision agency and held in a "deposit escrow account" opened at the project's main lending bank. The supervision ends once the home is ready for delivery.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 住房城乡建设部、自然资源部、金融监管总局 (2026). 关于完善商品住房销售制度的通知（建房规〔2026〕3 号，第一部分第（二）（三）项，第二部分第（三）项）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_13026/202610/content_7082773.html>；中国人民银行、金融监管总局 (2026). 关于改革完善房地产信贷管理推动加快构建房地产发展新模式的意见（银发〔2026〕171 号，第二十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_13026/202610/content_7082770.html>；银保监会办公厅、住房城乡建设部办公厅、人民银行办公厅 (2022). 关于商业银行出具保函置换预售监管资金有关工作的通知（银保监办发〔2022〕104 号，第二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-11/14/content_5726954.htm>；住房城乡建设部 (2010). 关于进一步加强房地产市场监管完善商品住房预售制度有关问题的通知（建房〔2010〕53 号，第一部分第（一）项，第二部分第（九）项）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2010/content_1629136.htm>；最高人民法院 (2020 修正). 关于审理商品房买卖合同纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释（法释〔2020〕17 号修正后文本，第二条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282621.html>
- Notes: China only: Projects that obtained a construction planning permit before August 28, 2026 follow their local presale conditions and fund supervision rules. Requiring down payments and loans to go into escrow appeared in both the 2010 and 2022 notices. For projects sold as ready-built homes, where the developer takes a small deposit during construction, the new rule requires the deposit to go into the deposit escrow account. The name and number of the escrow account are those written in the purchase contract, so check them before paying. If a contract is ruled void and the developer does not refund, sue in court. Under the ordinary first-instance procedure, a case should be decided within 6 months, which may be extended. Lawyers' fees are generally paid by each side. For how to pay for a resale home, see item 7 (the dedicated deposit account for transaction funds).

### 11. If you paid a subscription deposit or down payment on a new home but no formal contract was signed, or the loan was not approved, for a reason that is not your fault (such as a tighter policy after signing or a bank halting loans), demand a refund from the developer
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: No money cost. Read two more clauses when you sign the subscription agreement and the formal contract. If the developer will not refund, you must sue in court.
- Plain language: If you paid a subscription deposit but no formal contract was signed, or the loan was not approved, first find out whose fault it was. If neither you nor the developer is at fault, the developer must refund the deposit, or refund the purchase money with interest. A tighter local loan policy after signing, or a bank halting loans, is generally treated as neither party's fault. If your credit record shows late payments or your income is too low, it is your fault, and the deposit may not come back.
- Benefit: The Supreme People's Court's judicial interpretation states: if a developer took a deposit through subscription, order or reservation, and the formal sales contract was not signed for a reason on one side, the matter is handled under the deposit rules. Under the deposit rules, a buyer who breaches forfeits the deposit. A seller who receives a deposit and breaches must refund twice the amount (see section 12, item 15, on a payment labelled as a deposit). If the contract was not signed for a reason that is neither party's fault, "the seller shall return the deposit to the buyer." A separate rule covers loans. If the sales contract provides for payment by loan, and the loan contract is not signed for a reason on one side, so the sales contract cannot be performed, the other party may terminate the contract and claim damages. If the loan contract is not signed for a reason that is neither party's fault, both parties may terminate the contract. The developer must return the principal of the purchase money received, with interest, or return the deposit. The law gives only a principle for what counts as neither party's fault. The examples below come from the author. A tighter local mortgage policy after signing, with banks stopping loans, is generally neither party's fault. Late payments on your credit record, or insufficient income statements, are your fault. If the project lacks complete papers or the bank will not lend for this project, it is the developer's fault. These two rules apply only to commercial homes bought from a developer. They do not apply to resale homes.

- Sources: 最高人民法院 (2020 修正). 关于审理商品房买卖合同纠纷案件适用法律若干问题的解释（第一、四、五、十九条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282621.html>；全国人大 (2020). 民法典（第五百八十六、五百八十七条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Notes: China only: What counts as neither party's fault is disputed, and the court decides it case by case. If the subscription agreement already sets out the main terms of the sales contract, and the developer has received the purchase money, the court will treat it as a formal sales contract. Under the ordinary first-instance procedure, a case should be decided within 6 months, which may be extended. Under the simplified procedure, within 3 months. Lawyers' fees are generally paid by each side.

### 12. When buying a new home, check how area discrepancies are handled before signing, and photograph or screenshot the promises in brochures, ads and scale models of the development
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: No money cost. Read two more clauses before signing, and take photos or screenshots while you do.
- Plain language: If the contract says nothing else, a difference of less than 3% between the registered area and the contract area is settled on the actual area, and you pay or get back the difference. If the difference is over 3%, you may cancel the purchase, and the developer must refund the money with interest within 30 days. A specific promise about the homes and facilities in the complex, made in ads, is binding even if it is not written into the contract, if it affects whether you buy.
- Benefit: A Ministry of Construction regulation states that where a home is priced by area, the contract must say how area errors are handled. If the contract does not say, the following rules apply. "If the area error, as an absolute value, is within 3% (inclusive), the price is settled on the actual area." If the error exceeds 3%, you have the right to cancel the purchase. If you cancel, the developer must refund the money already paid within 30 days from the day you ask, with interest. If you do not cancel and the registered area is larger than the contract area, you pay for the part within 3%, and the developer bears the part beyond 3%, with ownership going to you. If the registered area is smaller than the contract area, the developer refunds the part within 3%, and refunds double the part beyond 3%. On advertising, the Supreme People's Court's interpretation states that sales ads and promotional material are generally only invitations to negotiate, not contracts. But if the developer makes specific, definite statements about homes and facilities within the planned development scope, and these have a major effect on whether you sign and at what price, they count as contract terms. "Such statements and promises count as contract content even if they are not written into the commercial housing sales contract," and breaking them creates liability for breach.

### 13. If a developer delivers a new home late or delays the property title, send a written demand first to keep evidence, then claim the penalty under the contract

- Cost: No money cost. Write a formal demand, send it by courier and keep a copy. If you really want to cancel or claim compensation, it will most likely take a lawsuit.
- Plain language: If the developer delivers your new home late and still has not done so three months after your written demand, you may cancel the purchase. If the contract sets no penalty, you are paid the rent for a similar home in the same area for the period of delay. The same compensation applies if the developer's fault stops you from getting the property title, and you may cancel if it goes more than a year past the deadline. Your right to cancel expires, so do not leave the decision open for long.
- Benefit: The Supreme People's Court's interpretation states: if the developer delivers late and still does not deliver within three months after your demand, you may cancel the contract, unless the contract provides otherwise. If the developer has not asked, you must exercise the right within one year from the day you learn you can cancel, or it is lost. ### 14. Before buying a home, check whether it is mortgaged or seized; after signing, apply for an advance registration notice at the property registration office

### 15. If you carry a floating-rate mortgage and its rate is well above the rate on new mortgages, ask the bank to negotiate a lower rate or apply to refinance
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: No money cost. Call the bank's customer service or apply in its mobile banking app. Each attempt takes several dozen minutes. The bank may not agree.
- Plain language: A mortgage rate is a base rate plus a fixed spread. That spread was fixed for the whole loan, but since 2024 you may negotiate with the bank to change it. If your rate is well above the national rate on new mortgages, you can ask the bank to lower it, or apply for a new loan to replace the old one. On a RMB 1,000,000, 30-year loan, a rate half a point lower saves about RMB 100,000 in interest.
- Benefit: The People's Bank of China's 2024 notice allows borrowers and banks to negotiate changes to the spread added on top of the LPR, the loan prime rate. The LPR is the quoted loan market rate, and mortgage rates are set by adding or subtracting a spread from it. A PBOC official explained that the spread in the original contract was fixed for the whole loan term, and this change ends that. In August 2026, the PBOC and the National Financial Regulatory Administration added in a document: for issued floating-rate mortgages, when the rate deviates from the rate on new national mortgages by a certain margin, "the borrower may negotiate with the bank to change the contractual rate, or apply for a new floating-rate personal housing loan to refinance the existing loan. The choice of method is up to the borrower." The amount of a new loan may not exceed the principal remaining on the old loan. The savings below were calculated by the author using the equal-installment formula. On RMB 1,000,000 over 30 years, the monthly payment at a 4.0% annual rate is about RMB 4,774, and at 3.5% about RMB 4,490. That is about RMB 284 less per month, and about RMB 100,000 less interest over 30 years.

### 17. If a property company cuts water, electricity, heating or gas to force you to pay property fees, complain to the local housing authority; if you owe property fees, the company may only demand payment, sue or apply for arbitration

### 18. When taking over a new home, inspect it room by room on handover day and write the defects into the handover record; have leaks and water and electrical faults repaired by the developer within the warranty period in the Housing Quality Warranty
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=no gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: No money cost. Spend two or three extra hours on handover day checking walls, floors, doors, windows, and water and electrics room by room, and take photos. Hiring someone to inspect costs extra.
- Plain language: On handover, the developer must give you a Housing Quality Warranty. The law sets a minimum warranty of 5 years for leaks in bathrooms, roofs and exterior walls, and 2 years for water and electrical pipes and decoration, counted from completion acceptance. The developer's warranty may not be shorter than the builder's remaining warranty. If the developer refuses to repair, or delays repair within the warranty period, you may hire someone to repair it, and the developer pays.
- Benefit: A Ministry of Construction regulation states that when selling a commercial home, the developer must provide the buyer with a Housing Quality Warranty and a Housing Use Manual. The developer bears quality warranty liability for the home it sold, counted from the delivery date. The warranty period for a commercial home may not be shorter than the remaining period of the builder's quality warranty to the developer. If a quality problem within the warranty scope appears during the warranty period, the developer must repair it and compensate for the losses caused. Damage caused by force majeure or improper use is not the developer's responsibility. The minimum warranty periods for builders are set by State Council regulation, counted from the date of qualified completion acceptance. Roof waterproofing, and leak prevention in bathrooms, rooms with waterproofing requirements and exterior walls, is 5 years. Heating and cooling systems: 2 heating or cooling seasons. Electrical wiring, water and drainage pipes, equipment installation and decoration works: 2 years. Foundations and main structure: the reasonable service life specified in the design documents. The Supreme People's Court's interpretation says that during the warranty period the developer must bear the repair duty: "If the seller refuses to repair, or delays repair beyond a reasonable time, the buyer may repair it itself or entrust someone else. The repair costs and other losses during the repair period are borne by the seller." If a quality problem seriously affects normal living or use, you may cancel the contract and claim damages. If the main structure is confirmed to be substandard, you may also cancel and claim damages. Checking each room on handover day, writing defects into the handover record and taking photos is the author's practice.
