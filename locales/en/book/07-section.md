# 7. Getting by when money is tight

This section covers money and safety nets: how much you can receive, what you can be exempted from, what services you can get, and what conditions apply. It is not converted into years of life. Policies change every year and differ by place, so amounts and thresholds follow what your local government publishes for the current year.

The entries in this section are grouped by theme below. Item numbers are in brackets.

**Lost your job**: apply for unemployment insurance online (item 1), get certified as having employment difficulties (item 12), claim vocational training subsidies (item 13).

**Owed wages**: first file a complaint with labor inspection (item 2), apply for legal aid (item 3), and if you are owed wages, first work out who your employer is (item 22).

**Emergency and last-resort help**: go to a rescue station (item 4), apply for temporary assistance (item 6), apply for minimum living allowance (item 7), disability two-allowance subsidies (item 8), replace a lost ID card immediately (item 11), when you have nowhere to sleep (item 21), when you cannot repay credit cards or online loans (item 23).

**Medical and social insurance**: do not let resident medical insurance lapse (item 9), for a serious illness use medical insurance and medical assistance first (item 10), if social insurance contributions lapse, do not panic (item 18), get a medical or critical illness insurance policy (item 20).

**Finding work and starting over**: public employment services and gig-work markets (item 5), a systematic approach to job hunting (item 14), do not pay deposits or hand over your ID (item 15), prison or bankruptcy does not close off the way back (item 19).

**Housing and food**: get into public rental housing (item 16), and cut housing and food costs to the minimum (item 17).

### 1. After losing your job, apply for unemployment insurance benefits online first
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: Free. You can apply on your phone through any of three channels: the "Palm 12333" app (the official labor and social security app), the e-social-security card mini-program inside WeChat (a messaging app) or Alipay (a payment app), or the National Social Insurance Public Service Platform. You need a social security card or your ID card. In most areas you do not have to visit an office. You must first register as unemployed.
- Plain language: If you and your employer paid unemployment insurance for at least one year, and you did not quit by your own choice, you can claim benefits. You must also register as unemployed and show you are willing to work. How long you can receive benefits depends on how long you paid in. During this period, unemployment insurance also pays your employee medical insurance for you.
- Benefit: You must meet three conditions together. First, you and your employer have paid unemployment insurance for at least one year in total. Second, you did not quit by your own choice (the law calls this "unemployment not caused by one's own will"). Third, you have registered as unemployed and are willing to work. How long you can receive benefits depends on how long you paid in. With at least one but under five years of payments, the maximum is 12 months. With at least five but under 10 years, the maximum is 18 months. With 10 years or more, the maximum is 24 months. The monthly amount is set by provincial governments, so it differs by province. The amount is below the local minimum wage and above the urban minimum living allowance. The national goal is to raise it gradually to 90% of the minimum wage standard. While you receive unemployment benefits, you stay enrolled in and keep your basic employee medical insurance benefits. The basic medical insurance premium for this period is paid from the unemployment insurance fund, so you do not pay it yourself (national; regulation in force since 1999).

- Notes: China only: If you quit by your own choice, that does not count as "not caused by one's own will," so you cannot claim. So do not sign a "resigned for personal reasons" form when you leave; see item 7 of section 19. Benefits are counted from the day you register as unemployed, so the later you register, the later you get paid. Register right after you leave. Registration requires the separation certificate your employer issues; see item 17 of section 19 (separation certificate). Whether you must honor a non-compete agreement, and what to do if the company gives no compensation, is covered in item 12 of section 11 (signed a non-compete agreement). Employee medical insurance during the benefit period is paid by unemployment insurance, so you do not need to enroll again. Amounts differ a lot between provinces; follow what the place where you are insured publishes. The "90% of minimum wage" figure comes from the 2017 guidance of the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security and the Ministry of Finance (Ren She Bu Fa [2017] No. 71). The original wording is "gradually raise the unemployment insurance benefit standard to 90% of the minimum wage standard." This is a target, not the amount paid today. ### 2. If you are owed wages, file a complaint with labor inspection first, then apply for labor arbitration; both are free, and most cases get an outcome within a few months
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=some gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: Free. Both the complaint and arbitration cost nothing. You need to gather your labor contract, pay slips, attendance records, bank transfer records and chat logs, and make one to three trips to the labor bureau or the arbitration commission. Arbitration has a one-year time limit (the legal term is arbitration limitation period). If you are still working at the company and your wages are unpaid, this one-year limit does not apply. If you have already left, you must file within one year from the day you left. The hard part is making repeated trips and gathering evidence one piece at a time, which can wear people down.
- Plain language: Start by calling 12333 (the national labor and social security hotline) or going to the local labor inspection office to file a complaint. Once it is accepted, the inspection must be finished within 60 working days. If they find wages are owed, they can order the company to pay by a deadline. If it still does not pay on time, it must add 50% to 100% in compensation. If that fails, apply for labor arbitration, which is decided within 45 days. But a decided case does not mean the money has arrived. If the company has no assets or the owner has fled, you may win and still get nothing.
- Benefit: The first step is a complaint. Call 12333 or go to the local labor inspection office. The law says that once a complaint is accepted, the inspection must be finished within 60 working days, and a complex case can be extended by another 30 working days. If the inspection finds wages are owed, it can order the company to pay by a set deadline. If the company still does not pay by then, it must pay an extra compensation of 50% to 100% of the amount owed. In 2024, national labor inspection bodies handled 137,000 cases and recovered wages of 8.65 billion yuan for 654,000 people. If you have an IOU or a wage list and the facts are clear, you can also apply directly to a basic people's court for a payment order. A payment order is a court order telling the company to pay, and the court issues it within 15 days after accepting the application. The second step is arbitration. The law says a case must be concluded within 45 days of acceptance, and this can be extended by 15 days. For wage claims you can ask for advance enforcement, meaning part of the award is enforced before the whole case is decided. If the amount owed is no more than 12 times the local monthly minimum wage, it is final arbitration: once the tribunal rules, the decision is settled and the company cannot sue over it. In 2024, the national arbitration bodies handled 4.257 million cases and concluded 4.156 million. The amount concluded was 93.47 billion yuan. The mediation success rate was 79.6%, the arbitration conclusion rate was 98.2%, and the arbitration termination rate was 73.7%. Some people say this takes "six months to a year." That refers to the cases that go to court after arbitration. Those court cases take 6 months at first instance (3 months under simplified procedure), plus 3 months on appeal. In 2024, courts concluded 88,000 cases to recover labor remuneration and recovered 28.54 billion yuan. 998 people were sentenced for refusing to pay labor remuneration (national).

If the company has no assets or the owner has fled, you may win and still get nothing. These two routes require an employer. Unlicensed workshops and shops count and can be complained against and taken to arbitration. Private work for a household or an individual, such as a domestic helper, is not an employment relationship. That goes to court; see item 22 (work out who employs you first). Migrant workers applying for legal aid to recover wages are not means-tested. ### 3. If you cannot afford a lawsuit, apply for legal aid; wage claims, alimony and work injury cases are already within its scope
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: Free. Legal aid does not charge the person receiving it a single cent.
- Plain language: You can apply for free legal aid for wage claims, claims for alimony, social insurance or minimum living allowance, and losses suffered while doing a good deed. Call 12348 (the national legal aid hotline) or go to your local legal aid center. You no longer have to go back to your home region to get a proof of financial hardship; just state your situation honestly. For defendants who may face the death penalty, and for blind, deaf, mute or underage defendants, a lawyer is assigned regardless of income.
- Benefit: The State Council regulation sets out six types of civil cases that legal aid covers. The regulation says: "claims for state compensation according to law; claims for social insurance benefits or minimum living guarantee benefits; claims for pensions or relief payments; claims for alimony, child support or maintenance; claims for payment of labor remuneration; asserting civil rights arising from acts of righteousness." In plain terms, that means: state compensation, social insurance or minimum living allowance, pensions and relief payments, alimony and child support, unpaid wages, and losses from a good deed. In criminal cases, a lawyer is assigned regardless of financial situation to defendants who may face the death penalty, and to blind, deaf, mute and underage defendants.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2003). 法律援助条例（国令第 385 号，第十条、第十一条、第十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6944.htm>；司法部有关负责人 (2021). 就贯彻实施法律援助法答记者问. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2021-12/29/content_5665114.htm>
- Notes: China only: The Legal Aid Law took effect on 1 January 2022, with one change. The old rule required a proof of financial hardship. Now you simply state your financial situation truthfully, and you do not need to go back to your home region for a certificate. Entry points are the local legal aid centers and public legal service centers. You can also call 12348.

### 4. When you have nowhere to go, go to a rescue station for food, shelter and a travel ticket
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: Free. Go to the local rescue management station yourself, or tell a police officer or the civil affairs department and ask them to take you there. When you check in, you register your name and personal belongings honestly. The hard part is admitting out loud that you have no money and nowhere to go.
- Plain language: The station provides meals and a bed, takes you to hospital if you fall ill, helps contact family, and gives you a ticket home if you cannot afford one. Stays are usually no longer than 10 days. Coming and leaving are both voluntary. It is not detention, and no cash is handed out. Tickets are issued to your household registration place or your current residence, so decide where you want to go back to before you go.
- Benefit: The station provides food and shelter that meet hygiene standards. If you fall ill suddenly there, you are sent to hospital for treatment. The station will also help you contact relatives and your employer. If you cannot afford a ticket back to your household registration place or your residence, you are given a travel voucher (a bus, train or boat ticket). Your household registration place is your official home; your residence is where you live now. Stays are generally no longer than 10 days. They can be extended while you wait for family to pick you up, for treatment, or for a placement. When police and other administrative staff come across a vagrant in the course of their duties, they must tell that person they can seek help here. For disabled people, minors, older people and people with limited mobility, staff must guide them there and escort them (national, since 2003).
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2003). 城市生活无着的流浪乞讨人员救助管理办法（国务院令第 381 号，第五、六、七条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2003/content_62246.htm>；民政部 (2003). 城市生活无着的流浪乞讨人员救助管理办法实施细则（民政部令第 24 号，第十一、十二条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2003/content_62510.htm>
- Notes: China only: Asking for help and leaving are both voluntary; this is not detention. The station provides basic meals and shelter, not cash. Travel vouchers are issued by household registration place or residence, so before you ask for help, decide where you want to go. For night and winter arrangements, see item 21 (when you have nowhere to sleep).

### 5. Look for work first through the free public employment services and gig-work markets, not fee-charging agencies
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: Free. Register at a public employment service agency at county level or above, such as an employment service center or a human resources market. Local gig-work markets also let you register. If you do not know the address, call 12333 and ask.
- Plain language: Public agencies such as employment service centers and human resources markets help you find jobs, give career guidance, answer policy questions, and handle employment and unemployment registration, all at no charge. The law also forbids these agencies from profiting from these services. Registration and job postings at gig-work markets are free too. While you are there, register as unemployed as well, since unemployment benefits and the certification of employment difficulty both need that registration.
- Benefit: The law says the following are free of charge: consultation on employment policy; information on where jobs are available and where workers are short; career guidance and job placement; employment and unemployment registration; and employment assistance for people with employment difficulties. These agencies may not run commercial activities and may not make money from these services. Gig workers' job-seeking and recruitment information, both registration and posting, is also free to the public and falls within the public employment information service. Support is to be strengthened for gig workers who have been without work for a long time, and for low-income households and other gig workers in difficulty (national).
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2007, 2015 修正). 就业促进法（第三十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2021-10/29/content_5647636.htm>；人力资源社会保障部等五部门 (2022). 关于加强零工市场建设 完善求职招聘服务的意见（人社部发〔2022〕38 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-07/09/content_5700177.htm>
- Notes: China only: Unemployment registration is the required first step for unemployment benefits and for certification of employment difficulty, so do it at the same time. If you do gig work paid by the day, check the pay on the spot, and keep the other party's contact details and a record of the work you did.

### 6. After a sudden crisis, apply for temporary assistance first
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=no gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: Free. Apply to the township government or street office where the emergency happened, and explain what went wrong, such as a fire, a traffic accident, a family member's sudden serious illness, or a sudden rise in essential costs. Urgent, small-amount cases go through "small-amount fast relief," with no check of household finances and no public notice, and usually finished within 3 days. If medical or school costs have suddenly risen, the application goes through public notice before approval. Prepare the documents on your local list. Usually one to two trips.
- Plain language: If something sudden happens at home, such as a fire, a car crash, or a serious illness in the family, and it has knocked basic living out of balance for a while, you can apply for temporary assistance from the township or street office where it happened. You do not need to be on minimum living allowance. Emergency cases are usually finished within 3 days, and the top amount is one month of the local minimum living allowance standard. Other cases get at most six months of the local minimum living allowance standard.
- Benefit: Two kinds of households can receive temporary assistance. One is a household that, because of an accident or a serious illness of a family member, cannot cope even with basic costs such as food, housing and medical care. The other is a minimum living allowance household whose unavoidable costs have suddenly risen beyond what it can bear. Which events are covered, and how much is paid, is set and published by county-level and higher governments (national system, amounts set locally, since 2014). The Social Assistance Law, in force from 1 July 2026, describes the target as "people who, because of sudden, urgent or catastrophic difficulties, are temporarily in serious difficulty meeting their basic living needs," and says assistance can be given as cash or in kind. The application is made to the township government or street office where the emergency happened. In urgent cases with small amounts that need immediate action, assistance must be given first and the paperwork completed afterwards. The Ministry of Civil Affairs and the Ministry of Finance issued guidance at the end of 2025 on how payment works. For sudden crises, accidental injuries and serious illnesses, "small-amount fast relief" applies. It does not check household finances again and does not require public notice. Processing is generally no more than 3 days, and the top level is no more than one month of the local minimum living allowance standard. Households on the edge of minimum living allowance, or households with rigid expense difficulties whose medical or school costs have suddenly risen, get assistance after public notice. In principle it is "approved once, paid in stages." Temporary assistance beyond small-amount fast relief has a top level of no more than six times the local monthly minimum living allowance standard (national, from 2026; specific standards set locally).

### 7. If your income is below the local minimum living allowance line, apply for minimum living allowance; if you are slightly above it, or medical or school costs are high, apply for medical, education, housing or employment assistance
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=some gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: Free. A family member who lives with you submits a written application to the township government or street office where your household registration is. If you cannot do it yourself, the village committee or residents' committee can file it for you. A check of household income and assets is required. Usually two to three trips, bringing your ID, household registration book, and proof of income and assets. The hard part is opening your household's finances to be checked, and then waiting for public notice. If you do not qualify for minimum living allowance, you can still apply for medical, housing or employment assistance through the same township or street office. Education assistance is applied for at the school your child attends.
- Plain language: If average income per person in your household is below the local minimum living allowance line, and your assets meet the rules, you can apply. At the end of 2024, the national average urban minimum living allowance was 798.1 yuan per person per month, and the rural average was 593.9 yuan. The payment covers the gap, or can be a fixed amount by tier. If your income is somewhat above the line, or medical or school costs take up too much of it, you can apply for medical, education and housing assistance even without qualifying for minimum living allowance.
- Benefit: If household income per person, after dividing total household income by the number of people, is below the local minimum living allowance standard, and household assets are within the set limits, the household can receive minimum living allowance. At the end of 2024, the national average minimum living allowance standard was 798.1 yuan per person per month in cities and 593.9 yuan per person per month in rural areas. The urban allowance covered 6.25 million people, and the rural allowance covered 33.615 million people. The allowance is paid monthly as the gap: local standard minus household income per person. For example, if the local standard is 798.1 yuan and each person in the household earns 500 yuan a month, you receive 298.1 yuan per person per month, not 798.1 yuan. The Social Assistance Law, in force from 1 July 2026, also allows payment as fixed amounts by tier: "the minimum living allowance may be paid as the actual gap between household income per person and the local minimum living allowance standard, or as fixed amounts by tier." The standard is set by taking local per-capita consumer spending from the previous year, multiplying it by a set ratio, and adjusting it again after some time. Put simply, it is the average amount each person in the area spent last year, worked out as a fraction (national average; it varies by place). Households just above the line fall into two tiers. The first is the minimum living allowance edge household: assets meet the rules, and household income per person is below 1.5 times the local minimum living allowance standard (2023 national document). From 1 July 2026, under the Social Assistance Law, provinces set this line by adding a fixed percentage above the local standard. The second is the rigid-expense difficulty household: household income per person is below the local previous-year per-capita disposable income, assets meet the rules, and the share of household income taken by unavoidable spending on medical care or education is above the local threshold. Both kinds of household can receive medical, education and housing assistance. Education assistance covers fee reductions and exemptions, scholarships and living subsidies, and student loans. Housing assistance in cities means priority allocation of public rental housing or a rental subsidy. Unemployed people who are able to work in these two tiers can also receive employment assistance. Severely disabled people and seriously ill people among the minimum living allowance edge households can be included individually in minimum living allowance and receive it monthly (national, since 2023; ratios and assessment methods are set locally).

If the debt exceeds your ability to repay and you are still willing to repay, the bank "may" negotiate with you on equal terms and sign an individualized installment agreement. The agreement lasts no more than 5 years. It must state how much is owed, how much is due each period, and whether annual fees and interest continue during repayment. Once both sides sign, the bank and the collection agency it entrusts must stop collection. If you do not repay under the agreement, this restriction does not apply to you. If the agreement is oral, the bank must keep a recording, at least until the day the debt is fully repaid. Debts already placed on an installment plan cannot be installed again; individualized installment is the only exception (2022 regulatory notice). When the People's Bank of China repealed the old bank card measures in 2025, it stated that credit cards continue to be governed by these rules. For online loans there is no uniform rule for individualized installments or extensions; whether you can get one depends on the contract and the lender. If the lender is a consumer finance company, its rules prohibit collection by violence, threats, intimidation or harassment. Banks and consumer finance companies may not have collectors contact people unrelated to the debt. As for paid agencies, the National Financial Regulatory Administration and four other departments issued a risk warning in 2026. It states that claims such as "the loan or credit card does not need to be repaid", "debt negotiation", "debt swap" and "credit whitewashing" are false information. These "agency rights-protection" services charge high fees, and if you want to quit midway they may sue you for breach of contract. They collect your bank card, phone card, home address and your child's school. If you follow their instructions to submit false materials, maliciously evade debt or pressure through complaints, the case can be serious enough to constitute a crime. In 2022, the Beijing regulator also named advertisements for "interest suspension", "stopping collection" and "full interest waiver" and warned that believing them and refusing to repay or contact the bank will likely leave an overdue bad record on your credit report. In 2022, the National Development and Reform Commission launched a special campaign against "credit repair". Its notice says these operators confuse credit objection with credit repair and defraud people under the names of "credit repair, whitewashing and note removal". Negative credit information is kept for 5 years from the day the bad act or event ends, then deleted. During the retention period, you can add a statement to the record, which the credit bureau must note (nationwide).
