# 25. What to handle after a death

Focus: money. This section covers only the procedures and how to get the money: what to do right after a death, which costs have checklists you can check against, which accounts hold money that can be recovered, and which steps close relatives have the right to handle. The first five items follow the order things happen: the scene, the death certificate, the body and cremation, disputing the cause of death, and deregistering the hukou. Arrangements the person should make while alive, such as voluntary guardianship, a will and accounts, are in section 17. The three benefits for work-related deaths are in section 19.

The funeral rules in this part are based on the new Regulations on Funeral Management, in force since 30 March 2026. For how the funeral subsidy and survivors' pension under the pension insurance are calculated, see item 9 (housing provident fund balance, social insurance benefits).

### 1. If someone dies at home or outside, first tell a normal death from an unnatural one: for accidents, unknown causes, or deaths found after living alone, call the police at once and do not move the body or the scene
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. It takes one phone call.
- Plain language: If someone dies in an accident, of an unknown cause, or after living alone for a long time without hospital treatment, the police issue the death certificate. If the scene has been disturbed, the certificate is harder to get. Without it, cremation, deregistration and insurance claims all stall, so if you are unsure, call 110 (China's police emergency number) first.
- Benefit: Article 8, paragraph 2 of the Household Registration Regulations: "When a citizen dies from an accident or the cause of death is unknown, the household head and the person who discovered the death must immediately report it to the local police station or the township people's government." In other words: after an accident, or when the cause of death is unknown, the household head and the person who found the body must report it at once to the police station or the township government. Article 23 of the Regulations on Funeral Management: "When a natural person dies a natural death, the medical institution shall promptly issue a death certificate. For an unnatural death case (incident) involving the lawful handling of the public security organs, where the deceased was not treated by a medical institution, the public security organ shall issue the death certificate." In other words: a natural death is certified by the medical institution that treated the person. An unnatural death handled by the police, where there was no hospital treatment, is certified by the police. If the scene has been disturbed, the cause is both hard to establish and hard to certify. Cremation, deregistration and insurance claims all depend on this one piece of paper.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (1958). 中华人民共和国户口登记条例（第八条）. <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/mpost_2531969.html>（广东省人民政府门户网站刊登）；国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，自 2026 年 3 月 30 日起施行，第二十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm>
- Notes: China only: Someone who was bedridden for a long time and dies at home counts as a normal death. You can ask the institution that treated them, or the community health service centre or township health centre for the area you live in, to issue the certificate. Who treats and who certifies is in item 2 (death certificate). If you cannot tell which kind of death it was, treat it as unnatural and call 110 first; the police will still issue the certificate after investigating. If you tidy up the scene before calling the police, nobody can help you. Do not move the body, wash or dress it, or remove medicine bottles or notes.

### 2. The death certificate is the key to everything that follows: whoever treated the person issues it; for a normal death at home, a community health service centre issues it within one day
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=time -->
- Cost: Free.
- Plain language: If the person died in hospital, the hospital that treated them issues the certificate. If they died a normal death at home, the local community health service centre or township health centre does. For a normal death with a clear cause, it is issued within one day. You will need it for almost every later step, so get several photocopies.
- Benefit: The Standards for Registration of Population Death Information (Trial) say that a death inside a medical institution is certified by "the medical institution responsible for treatment". Someone who died in a hospital or health centre is certified by the one that treated them. A normal death at home or elsewhere is certified "by the community health service institution or township (street) health centre in the local area", meaning the community health service centre or the township or street health centre where the person lived. Deaths that the public security or judicial authorities judge to be unnatural are handled by them under current rules and procedures. A 2026 notice from five national bodies says the paper death certificate and the electronic certificate "have the same legal effect". For a normal death with a clear cause, the certificate must be issued within one day of death. If the certificate is lost, you may apply once to the original issuing body for a replacement. If basic information is wrong, you may apply once for reissue with supporting documents.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国家卫生计生委办公厅 (2014). 人口死亡信息登记管理规范（试行）（国卫办规划发〔2014〕68 号，第九条）. <https://mzj.sz.gov.cn/szmz/pc/zwgk/jcxxgk/zcfg/byfw/content/post_2952215.html>（深圳市民政局转发）；国家卫生健康委、公安部、民政部、国家中医药局、国家疾控局 (2026). 关于加强居民死亡医学证明信息登记和电子证照管理工作的通知（国卫规划发〔2026〕5 号）. <https://wjw.fujian.gov.cn/xxgk/fgwj/gjwj/202602/t20260228_7102810.htm>（福建省卫生健康委员会转发）
- Notes: China only: Deregistration, cremation, withdrawing housing provident fund money, claiming social insurance benefits, insurance claims and inheritance all need this certificate. Get several photocopies now, and apply for the electronic certificate too if one is available. You can get only one replacement, so keep the original safe. If the name or ID number is wrong, fix it on the spot; otherwise every office has to be redone when you handle the inheritance.

### 3. Moving, storing and cremating the body can only be done by a funeral parlour; a hospital morgue does not run funerals, and storage is generally limited to 3 days
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: Free.
- Plain language: Only a funeral parlour can transfer, store, embalm, restore and cremate a body. A hospital morgue only holds bodies briefly and does not run funerals. Storage at a funeral parlour is generally limited to 3 days, so ask for an extension if you need longer. Keep the cremation certificate, which you will need for inheritance and insurance.
- Benefit: Article 25 of the Regulations on Funeral Management: "Body transfer, storage, embalming, cosmetic restoration and cremation services are provided exclusively by funeral parlours." In other words, other businesses cannot do these. Article 24: "Hospital morgues may not be outsourced and may not provide funeral services; they may provide temporary storage facilities for bodies." In other words, a hospital morgue can only hold a body briefly and cannot run a funeral. Article 27: "Funeral parlours generally store bodies for no more than 3 days. If longer storage is needed, the bereaved family or the party handing over the body must complete the extension procedures at the funeral parlour." In other words, to keep a body longer, apply for an extension at the parlour. Article 28: "The funeral parlour shall cremate a body only on presentation of both the death certificate and the cremation confirmation form, and shall issue a cremation certificate." In other words, both documents are required, and you get a cremation certificate afterwards. Article 29: "In areas that practise cremation, bodies shall be cremated on the spot or nearby at a funeral parlour." In other words, cremate at a funeral parlour near where the person died.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，自 2026 年 3 月 30 日起施行，第八、九、二十四、二十五、二十七、二十八、二十九条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm>
- Notes: China only: The "one-stop" packages offered right after a death in hospital usually do not come from a funeral parlour. Transfer itself is a basic item with a regulated price; see item 6 (basic service list). Keep the cremation certificate, as you will need it for inheritance, insurance and moving ashes later. Whether a locality practises cremation or burial is set by the provincial government. Ethnic minority funeral customs are protected under Article 9 of the regulations. Burial where cremation is required, or burial or tomb-building outside cemeteries and designated ecological burial areas, will be ordered corrected by the civil affairs department within a set deadline.

### 4. If you dispute the cause of death, request an autopsy within 48 hours and seal the medical records
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part is remembering to raise it at that moment.
- Plain language: If you doubt the cause of death, an autopsy must happen within 48 hours; 7 days if the hospital can freeze the body. The family must sign for consent, and refusing or delaying puts responsibility for an unclear cause on that side. Ask for the medical records to be sealed at the same time. Once a body is cremated, no autopsy is possible.
- Benefit: Article 26 of the Regulations on the Prevention and Handling of Medical Disputes: "When a patient dies and the doctor and the patient's side disagree about the cause of death, an autopsy shall be carried out within 48 hours of death; where the body can be frozen, this may be extended to 7 days." So if you want the cause of death clarified, the autopsy must be done within 48 hours. A hospital with freezing facilities can extend this to 7 days. The same article says: "An autopsy shall be carried out with the consent of the deceased's close relatives, who must sign for it; a refusal to sign is deemed a refusal of consent." So if the family will not sign, it counts as not consenting. It also says: "If either side refuses or delays the autopsy beyond the time limit, and this affects the determination of the cause of death, the side that refused or delayed bears the responsibility." So whichever side refused or ran past the deadline is responsible if the cause stays unclear. Article 24: sealing and unsealing medical records "shall be done with both the doctor and the patient's side present." In other words, you and the hospital must both be present when records are sealed or unsealed.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2018). 医疗纠纷预防和处理条例（国务院令第 701 号，第二十四、二十六条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm>
- Notes: China only: Saying "I already thought something was wrong" after the deadline has passed puts the responsibility on the side that delayed. Once a body is cremated, an autopsy is no longer possible, so settle this before contacting a funeral parlour. How to seal the medical records is in section 24.

### 5. Deregistering a deceased person's hukou has a legal deadline: before burial in cities, within one month in rural areas
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=low scope=time -->
- Cost: Free. Bring the death certificate and the hukou booklet, and go to the police station once.
- Plain language: In cities, deregistration must be done before burial; in rural areas, within one month. Take the death certificate and the hukou booklet to the police station. If you delay, inheritance transfers, benefit claims and insurance cancellations will all be blocked, because they check the registration status.
- Benefit: Article 8, paragraph 1 of the Household Registration Regulations: "When a citizen dies, the household head, relatives, dependants or neighbours shall report the death to the household registration authority and have the hukou cancelled: before burial in cities, within one month in rural areas. If a citizen dies while staying at a temporary residence, the household registration authority of the temporary residence shall notify the authority of the permanent residence to cancel the hukou." In other words, the household head, relatives, dependants or neighbours can go to the registration office (the police station) to report the death and cancel the hukou. If the person died while temporarily staying elsewhere, the police station there notifies the police station of their registered home to cancel the hukou.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (1958). 中华人民共和国户口登记条例（第八条）. <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/mpost_2531969.html>（广东省人民政府门户网站刊登）
- Notes: China only: An open hukou blocks inheritance transfers, benefit claims and insurance cancellations, because these offices check the registration status. If the pension keeps being paid, that counts as fraudulent claiming and will have to be repaid sooner or later. When you go to the police station, also ask how the social insurance and medical insurance benefits will stop and how to claim the burial subsidy. Those amounts are in item 9 (housing provident fund balance, social insurance benefits).

### 6. Funeral services are split into basic and non-basic items; basic items are on a national list, and their charges are set by law
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: Free. Spend a few minutes checking the list before you set out.
- Plain language: Body transfer, storage, farewell, cremation, ashes storage and ecological burial are all basic items on the national list. Their prices are set by regulation, and no charges may be added outside the list. Before you agree to anything, ask which items are basic and what the local standard price is.
- Benefit: Body transfer, body storage, farewell, cremation, ashes storage and ecological burial are on the national list of basic items. Niche burial at government-run funeral facilities is on the list too. Charges for these items are set by law, not by the funeral parlour itself. The list of non-basic items is set by the civil affairs departments at the prefecture-level city level or above.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，2025 年 11 月 14 日国务院第 72 次常务会议修订通过，「自2026年3月30日起施行」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> 第五条：「殡葬服务分为基础项目和非基础项目，收费政策由国务院发展改革部门、财政部门会同国务院民政等部门制定。」「国家制定殡葬服务基础项目清单，将遗体接运、遗体存放、遗体告别、遗体火化、骨灰寄存、生态安葬以及政府举办的殡葬服务机构提供的骨灰格位安葬等纳入清单范围」；第五十条「殡葬服务实行清单化管理并动态调整，禁止在清单之外设立项目、收取费用。」
- Notes: China only: Provinces, autonomous regions and centrally administered municipalities may add items to the national list. First ask which items are basic and what the local charge is, then discuss the rest. Prices for basic items are set by regulation; for items outside the basic list, you choose yourself, but still check them against the list and the charge standards. Separating these two categories is what lets you challenge overcharges. Wanting to give a loved one a dignified farewell is nothing to be ashamed of, and this is not advice to skimp. How the money is spent is up to you.

### 7. If you face forced bundling, charges above the posted price, or one charge split into several fees, write it down and complain
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part is staying willing to push back in the moment.
- Plain language: The rules name these price violations: no posted price list, charges above the listed price, forced bundling of funeral goods, and forced use of another company's paid service. Splitting one charge into several fees also counts. On the spot, ask for the price list and keep receipts and chat logs. Price cases go to market regulators; funeral issues go to civil affairs.
- Benefit: The regulation lists five types of price violation. Four are covered below. Not posting a price list, or charging more than the listed price, counts as failing to post prices clearly or adding charges beyond the listed price. Refusing to handle one thing unless you buy another counts as forced sale through bundling or unreasonable conditions. Forcing you to take another company's paid service counts as forcing, or effectively forcing, you to accept third-party paid services. Charging more under a different name counts as a disguised price increase. The rules also ban "splitting items, or widening the scope of charges, as disguised ways to raise fees." Splitting one service into several separately charged items, or widening what gets charged, is banned too.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，2025 年 11 月 14 日国务院第 72 次常务会议修订通过，「自2026年3月30日起施行」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> 第四十二条：「殡葬服务机构不得违反有关规定擅自设立收费项目，或者采取分解项目、扩大范围收费等形式变相提高收费标准。」第四十三条列举的价格违法行为含「（一）不按规定明码标价，或者在标价之外加价或者收取未标明的费用」「（三）采取捆绑、附加不合理条件或者不合理限制等方式，强制或者变相强制销售丧葬用品、提供殡葬相关服务并收取费用」「（四）强制或者变相强制丧属接受第三方的有偿服务，或者利用相关服务网点重复收取费用」；第五十一条要求民政部门与政务服务便民热线建立信息共享监督机制，「对丧属反映的殡葬服务问题，应当会同有关部门及时予以处理并将处理结果告知丧属」
- Notes: China only: Price violations are handled by market supervision authorities, and funeral services themselves by civil affairs departments. On the spot, get a price list and keep receipts and chat records. Complaining afterwards is still possible.

### 8. Funeral agents, goods buyers, and planning or hosting intermediaries must register with the county civil affairs department
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: Free. Ask whether they are registered.
- Plain language: Funeral agents, goods buyers, planners and hosts, and information consultants must register with the county civil affairs department. Unregistered firms are missing from the official funeral service system. Online checks vary by place, so the safest step is to call the county civil affairs office. Those who overcharge by deception or sell the dead's information will be dealt with under the law.
- Benefit: An unregistered agent is not in the civil affairs department's funeral service information system. Anyone who uses deception to charge illegally, pushes lavish funeral spending, forces services on families, or sells the dead's information will be dealt with under the law. The system also marks them and makes this public, so anyone can see it.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 殡葬管理条例（国务院令第 824 号，2025 年 11 月 14 日国务院第 72 次常务会议修订通过，「自2026年3月30日起施行」）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> 第四十一条：「除殡葬服务机构外，从事殡葬服务代理、用品代购、策划主持、信息咨询等殡葬相关服务活动的组织和个人，应当向县级人民政府民政部门备案。」「对存在以欺骗等手段违规收费、诱导大操大办、强迫接受服务、倒卖逝者信息等损害丧属权益行为的依法处置，并在殡葬服务信息系统中进行标注，向社会公开。」
- Notes: China only: Online memorial platforms are regulated the same way. Article 45 of the regulation bars them from inducing consumption and from unlawfully handling the deceased's personal information.

### 9. Claim money held in different places one at a time: housing provident fund balance, social insurance benefits, and work injury benefits
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=no gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: Free, but you have to visit several counters. Before you go, get the death certificate and proof of family relationship.
- Plain language: Heirs can withdraw the housing provident fund balance, which is easy to miss because it is not in a bank account. Leftover pension account money can also be inherited. Illness deaths of company employees bring a funeral allowance and 3 to 24 months of survivor benefits. Work-related deaths bring three more payments.
- Benefit: The housing provident fund balance (money left in the deceased's housing provident fund account) can be withdrawn by heirs and legatees. A legatee is a person named in a will to receive property. For work-related deaths there are three separate payments: a funeral allowance, a dependents' survivor benefit, and a one-time death benefit. Money left in a pension personal account can be inherited, as the Social Insurance Law states. If an employee in the basic pension scheme dies of illness or from a non-work cause, the family can also receive a funeral allowance and survivor benefits, together called "survivor benefits" (遗属待遇 in the original). For company employees the amounts follow a nationwide formula. The funeral allowance equals twice the provincial average monthly disposable income of urban residents in the previous year. The survivor benefit is also based on that monthly average income, paid for 3 to 24 months according to years of contributions. Under 5 years: 3 months. 5 to 10 years: 6 months. 10 to 15 years: 9 months. Over 15 years: one extra month for each additional year, up to 24 months. For retirees, the number of months falls by one for every year of pension already received, with a minimum of 9 months.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 住房公积金管理条例（国务院令第 844 号修改，2026 年 9 月 20 日起施行）第二十四条末款. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12946/202608/content_7079363.html>：「职工死亡或者被宣告死亡的，职工的继承人、受遗赠人可以提取职工住房公积金账户内的存储余额；无继承人也无受遗赠人的，职工住房公积金账户内的存储余额纳入住房公积金的增值收益。」；工伤保险条例第三十九条的三笔待遇见第 19 节. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>；全国人大常委会 (2018 修正). 社会保险法（第十四、十七条）：「个人死亡的，个人账户余额可以继承」「参加基本养老保险的个人，因病或者非因工死亡的，其遗属可以领取丧葬补助金和抚恤金」. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8080816f135f46016f210989b9179a>（国家法律法规数据库）；人力资源社会保障部、财政部 (2021). 企业职工基本养老保险遗属待遇暂行办法（人社部发〔2021〕18 号，2021 年 9 月 1 日起施行，第四、五条），原件未公开挂网，条文按广州市花都区人民政府行政复议决定书（花都府行复〔2022〕226 号）的逐字引用核对：「丧葬补助金的标准，按照参保人员死亡时本省……上一年度城镇居民月人均可支配收入的2倍计算」「缴费年限不满5年的，发放月数为3个月……缴费年限30年以上的，按照30年计算，发放月数最高为24个月」. <https://www.huadu.gov.cn/xxgk/xzfy/content/post_8672730.html>；福建省人力资源和社会保障厅. 常见问题：「每领取1年基本养老金减少1个月，发放月数最低为9个月」「累计缴费年限不足5年的，其遗属待遇（含丧葬补助金和抚恤金）标准不得超过其个人缴费之和」. <http://rst.fujian.gov.cn/wz/cjwt/czzgly/202311/t20231113_6294929.htm>
- Notes: China only: The housing fund money is the one most often forgotten, because it is not in a bank account and is not automatically included in estate division. Money left in the pension personal account and survivor benefits are calculated and paid separately, so handle them together at the social insurance counter where the person was insured. Materials differ by place. If contributions were under 5 years, the combined survivor benefits cannot exceed what the person paid in. This formula comes from the rules for company employee pensions; ask the counter about survivor benefits for public institution employees and for urban and rural residents' pensions. For the funeral allowance and survivor benefit paid into the deceased's bank account, see item 12 of this section (withdrawing deposits directly at the bank). For will and inheritance formalities, see section 17. If you plan to renounce the inheritance to avoid debts, do not withdraw this money first; see item 11 of this section (written renunciation of inheritance).

### 10. Close relatives can ask platforms to view, copy, correct, or delete the deceased's accounts and personal information
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=mid scope=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Only if the platform does not cooperate do you need to complain or sue.
- Plain language: Close relatives can ask a platform to view, copy, correct, or delete the deceased's personal information, if they have a legitimate interest. If the platform refuses, it must explain why, and you can sue. Exception: arrangements the person made while alive, such as naming an account heir, come first.
- Benefit: The law states that when a natural person dies, close relatives may exercise rights to view, copy, correct, or delete the relevant personal information, for their own lawful and legitimate interests. They can ask the platform to show the information, make a copy, correct errors, or delete it. Article 50 requires platforms to set up a convenient mechanism for handling requests, and says that a platform rejecting a request "must explain the reasons." If the platform refuses, "the individual may bring a lawsuit in the people's court in accordance with the law."
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2021). 个人信息保护法. 中国人大网. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202108/t20210820_313088.html> 第四十九条：「自然人死亡的，其近亲属为了自身的合法、正当利益，可以对死者的相关个人信息行使本章规定的查阅、复制、更正、删除等权利；死者生前另有安排的除外。」；第五十条「个人信息处理者应当建立便捷的个人行使权利的申请受理和处理机制。拒绝个人行使权利的请求的，应当说明理由」「个人可以依法向人民法院提起诉讼」
- Notes: China only: The law includes the phrase "unless the deceased made other arrangements while alive." If they named an account heir or left clear wishes, follow what they left. So while people are clear-headed, it is worth writing down where their accounts and passwords are; see section 17. Cancelling an account and deleting personal information are two separate things; see section 14.

### 11. When a relative dies in debt, heirs repay only from the inheritance they receive; to avoid the debt, file a written renunciation of inheritance before the estate is divided
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: Writing a renunciation statement is free. The real cost is giving up the estate too, such as the house or savings. If you accept the inheritance, creditors may sue you, and you must respond and provide material showing what the estate is worth.
- Plain language: Debts do not disappear when someone dies. Heirs, such as children or a spouse, repay only from the inheritance they receive, and need not pay beyond it. To avoid the debt, file a written renunciation before the estate is divided. Silence counts as accepting the inheritance.
- Benefit: Civil Code Article 1161: "Heirs are liable for the deceased's legally due taxes and debts only up to the actual value of the estate they inherit. Heirs may voluntarily repay any excess, and this limit then does not apply." In practice, taxes and debts he owed are repaid only from the inheritance you receive. Any excess need not be repaid, unless you choose to pay it. Paragraph 2 of the same article says: "Heirs who renounce inheritance are not liable for the deceased's legally due taxes and debts." Civil Code Article 1159 says taxes and debts must be paid before the estate is divided. Article 1124 says renunciation must be made "in writing before the estate is processed; without such a statement, the heir is deemed to have accepted." Article 33 of the Supreme People's Court's first interpretation on inheritance says the written statement goes to the estate administrator or the other heirs. Article 35 of the same interpretation says that renouncing after the estate has been divided does not renounce the inheritance right; it renounces property already received. Article 1145: heirs may elect an estate administrator, and if they do not, they all serve. Article 1147: the estate administrator must inventory the estate, make an estate list, and handle the deceased's claims and debts. In a case from Yuzhou, Henan, a borrower died in March 2023 and his children did not make a written renunciation. In May 2023 the court ruled that the children must repay 95,000 yuan of principal and interest, up to the actual value of the inheritance. The children said it was unclear how much the company they inherited was worth, so the judgment could not be enforced. Their accounts and car were frozen and sealed, and they were put on the list of people subject to restricted high consumption. The matter was only settled in February 2025, with 90,000 yuan paid. In a case from Chongqing, a borrower died owing principal and interest on a 400,000-yuan loan, and the heirs all declared that they renounced the inheritance. But his spouse and daughter had already withdrawn the basic pension and occupational annuity that counted as his estate. Prosecutors found that the two had not expressly renounced, so they were deemed to have accepted. In November 2025 the spouse settled with the creditor and the debt was repaid. Debts like these range from 10,000 yuan to several hundred thousand yuan, so the benefit is rated large.

### 12. After a relative dies, do not assume notarization is required: deposits of up to 50,000 yuan at one bank can be withdrawn directly, and a house can be inherited at the property registry with all heirs present
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=no gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: Free. Banks holding deposits must be visited one by one. For a house, you must gather all heirs and go to the same registration window on the same day. If someone refuses to come, or heirs cannot agree on the split, this route fails, and you must go to notarization or sue.
- Plain language: A deceased person's deposits at one bank, totaling up to 50,000 yuan, can be withdrawn by a spouse, child, or parent without notarization. A house can also be inherited through the property registry, if all heirs appear together. Notarization costs money, for example up to 10,000 yuan for one home in Fuzhou or Xiamen.
- Benefit: On deposits, a 2021 notice from the China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission and the People's Bank of China states that eligible small deposits of a deceased depositor no longer require notarization. There are three conditions. First, the combined balance at one bank is no more than 10,000 yuan; the bank may raise this limit itself, up to 50,000 yuan. Second, the applicant is a spouse, child, or parent, or an heir or legatee named in a notarized will. Third, the whole balance is withdrawn at once, and the account is then closed. Spouse, children, or parents must provide four items: a death certificate; a household registration book, marriage certificate, birth certificate, or similar proof of family relationship; their own ID; and a handwritten, signed commitment letter. The 2024 notice (Jin Gui [2024] No. 6) from the National Financial Regulatory Administration and the People's Bank of China raised the limit to 50,000 yuan uniformly from June 1, 2024. Rural small and medium-sized banks may complete this by December 31, 2025. Gold savings products, and government bonds and wealth management products sold by banks, can also be withdrawn this way once they mature or are redeemed, and their amounts count toward the 50,000 yuan. Funeral allowances and survivor benefits paid into the deceased's account do not count toward the 50,000 yuan, and the spouse, children, or parents can withdraw them with the same materials. For the house, Article 14 of the Implementing Rules of the Interim Regulations on Real Estate Registration says that inheritance or legacy registration "shall submit death certificate materials, a will or an agreement among all statutory heirs on the distribution of the real estate, and materials on family relationship with the deceased, and may also submit notarized materials or effective legal documents." Notarization is one option, not a requirement. In 2016 the Ministry of Justice issued a notice abolishing the 1991 joint notice that required notarization for property registration. The Fuzhou Real Estate Registration and Transaction Center explained what to do without notarized materials: all statutory heirs or legatees must go to the registry together to have their documents checked and sign a statement confirming the materials are true. Fees, using Fujian's standard: for inheritance notarization not involving property, the portion up to 200,000 yuan is charged at 0.8%, with a minimum of 300 yuan. For property, fees are charged by area: in Fuzhou and Xiamen, no more than 70 yuan per square meter, and in principle no more than 10,000 yuan per home. Working this out with that standard, notarization for a 50,000-yuan deposit costs 400 yuan, and for a 90-square-meter home in Fuzhou the maximum is 6,300 yuan. The savings are several hundred to several thousand yuan, so the benefit is rated medium.
