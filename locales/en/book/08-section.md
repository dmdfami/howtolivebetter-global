# 8. Keeping Yourself Out of Trouble: Legal and Financial Safety

This section puts a price on money and personal freedom, not on lifespan. It covers three things: what the law makes you responsible for, how much you can save in compensation by following it, and what procedural rights you have if something goes wrong. The article numbers have been checked against the official texts.

The entries are grouped by topic below. The numbers in brackets are item numbers within this section.

**Preventing fraud**: stop a payment right away once you find out you have been scammed (item 2); the hard anti-fraud rules (item 3); seeing a face on video is not verification (item 4); do not lend your bank card or phone card to anyone (item 8); check your credit report for free (item 9); scams in online shopping and second-hand trading (item 22); ask for the price before any home repair visit (item 27); do not carry things for strangers (item 29); helping someone pass a face scan is the start of an "AB loan" (item 45).

**Signing, lending and debt**: read the whole document before you sign (item 17); put loans in writing with a loan agreement (item 18); there are deadlines for asserting your rights (item 19); if you are subject to enforcement, report your assets honestly (item 20); being put on the dishonest-debtor list (item 21); do not act as a nominal legal representative of a company (item 28); a family member has gambling debts (item 44).

**Conflicts and grudges**: when a conflict breaks out, call the police first (item 10); you may fight back against harm you cannot avoid (item 11); do not seek revenge after a grudge (item 12); do not attack people who have nothing to do with it (item 13); when a thought of taking everyone down with you comes up (item 14); when someone close to you says no one should come out ahead (item 15); the route of taking out insurance on someone and then harming them is closed off (item 38).

**Love and marriage**: think carefully before giving large gifts (item 23); send bride price by bank transfer (item 24); write down premarital property (item 25); verify marital status before marrying (item 26); if you are subjected to domestic violence, call the police first (item 43).

**Calling the police, being accused, and collecting evidence**: if you are summoned, get a lawyer first (item 5); if something happens, go and explain it yourself (item 6); if someone fabricates facts to report you (item 33); if evidence is insufficient, the court should acquit (item 34); apply for state compensation (item 35); when you report a crime, get a case acceptance receipt (item 39); do not give money to anyone handling your case (item 40); record conversations that may turn hostile (item 41); photograph the whole scene first (item 42).

**Online speech and cyberbullying**: do not insult people or spread rumors online (item 16); if you are targeted by online abuse, switch on protective settings first (item 37).

**Sex and blackmail**: do not have sex with someone who is not in their right mind (item 31); if someone demands money over nude chats, report it to the police immediately (item 32); a victim seeking compensation should not meet the other party alone (item 36).

**Driving, keeping dogs and preventing theft**: after a traffic accident, do not drive away (item 1); do not drive after drinking (item 7); keep dogs on a leash (item 30); fit good locks on doors and windows (item 46).

### 1. After a traffic accident, stop, help the injured and call the police. Do not drive away
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=high scope=freedom -->
- Cost: No money. Stopping, turning on hazard lights, helping the injured and calling the police, then waiting for traffic police, takes an hour or two at most. For a minor accident with no injuries and no dispute over the facts, take photos first. Then record the other driver's name, phone number, license plate, driver's license number, insurance policy number and the damaged parts. Once both sides have signed, you may leave the scene and negotiate afterward.
- Plain language: If you drive away, you are treated as fully at fault. Your license is revoked, and if it counts as a crime you can never reapply. Insurance will not pay, so you must cover hundreds of thousands of yuan yourself. Staying to help and calling the police takes an hour or two at most.
- Benefit: The driver who runs bears all the responsibility for the accident. Only if you can prove the other side was also at fault can your responsibility be reduced a little. Running also means your license is revoked. If the hit-and-run is a crime, you can never apply for a driver's license again. If it counts as the crime of traffic accident, running can bring a sentence of 3 to 7 years. If running leads to a death, the sentence is 7 years or more. Insurance also does not pay: under commercial auto insurance, vehicle damage and third-party liability cover will not pay for a "traffic accident hit-and-run" or for "deliberately damaging, fabricating or destroying the scene or evidence". Hundreds of thousands of yuan in compensation come out of your own pocket (nationwide).

"I bumped something, felt nothing, and drove on" can still later be counted as a hit-and-run, so if you are unsure, call the police. The insurance point cites model clauses, the insurance industry's standard template. What you can actually claim depends on your own policy.

### 2. If you find you have been scammed, call 110 or 96110 at once to request a payment stop. Do not investigate on your own first
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. One phone call. State the time and amount of the transfer and the other party's account number. Keep the transfer screenshot and chat records.
- Plain language: The first minutes after a transfer matter most. Police can urgently stop payment and quickly freeze funds, and banks must cooperate. Once money is moved and split up, recovery becomes much less likely. So call 110 or 96110 first rather than checking or asking around yourself. A call from 96110 is police warning you, so answer it.
- Benefit: Public security organs have the power to make immediate inquiries into funds involved in a case, urgently stop payment, and quickly freeze them. Banks and payment institutions must cooperate. Frozen funds are returned to the victim through the proper procedure. Once money is moved and dispersed, the chance of recovering it drops sharply, so the earlier a payment stop is requested, the more effective it is. 96110 is the nationwide anti-fraud warning hotline. Its calls come from police warning you, so be sure to answer (nationwide, since December 2022).
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2022). 反电信网络诈骗法（第二十、三十四条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml>；福建省公安厅 (2023). 96110 来电，请务必接听. <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202303/t20230306_6126156.htm>（省级公安机关页面）
- Notes: China only: Anyone who claims to be from the courts, prosecutors or police and asks you to transfer money to a "safe account" is running a scam. The law only says a "payment stop and freeze mechanism" should be set up, which in practice means banks hold the money. How much can be recovered depends on whether the money is still in the account when you report. The introduction to the National Anti-Fraud Center app (the government's official anti-fraud app) and to 96110 comes from the Fujian Provincial Public Security Department's page. The original page on the Ministry of Public Security site could not be opened.

### 3. Remember the hard anti-fraud rules: do not trust calls, do not share information, do not click links, and check before transferring. The seven most common scams all follow this pattern
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. Memorize the "three don'ts and one do" (do not trust, do not share, do not click, check before transferring), read the seven categories below, and install the National Anti-Fraud Center app. Always answer calls from 96110. Whenever someone asks you to transfer money, share your screen, download an unknown app, click a link or give a verification code, hang up first and call the official number to verify. The hard part is that the other side often pushes hard, and you must resist that pressure and hang up first.
- Plain language: Hang up if anyone asks you to transfer money, share your screen, install an app, click a link or give a code. Then call the official number. Fake investments, fake customer service, fake police or courts, romance scams and fake orders all make you pay first. Spotting them early beats chasing money later.
- Benefit: The law requires governments and relevant departments to explain the methods of each type of telecom and online fraud to the public. Public security organs, working with financial, telecom and internet regulators, have built an early-warning and intervention system that warns people who are likely to be defrauded. 96110 is that nationwide early-warning hotline, and its calls should be answered. In June 2026 the Ministry of Public Security reported that the ten most common types of fraud made up 85% of all telecom fraud cases. Fake-order rebate fraud has the most cases, at 25%. Fake online investment fraud causes the biggest losses, about 40% of all losses. The Fujian Provincial Public Security Department breaks down the warning signs by type as follows. Fake-order rebate: "anyone who invites you to earn money together is very likely trying to take yours", and if you do not finish the assigned tasks, your principal is not refunded. Fake investment: "water armies" in group chats post profits, the scammers give small returns first and then ask you to invest more, and withdrawals require you to "pay tax". Online loans: "a loan does not require a deposit or bank statement deposits. If you run into one, it must be fake." Fake customer service: they say your membership will be auto-charged or will hurt your credit, and pressure you to transfer money to cancel. These scammers often have you download an app named after a "meeting" and then open screen sharing. Once you share, "everything on your phone, including message pop-ups, SMS verification codes and password entry, can be seen clearly". The Shanghai Anti-Fraud Center's advice is: "anything that mentions a meeting ID, screen sharing or remote control is a scam." Fake police, prosecutors or courts: "public security, procuratorate and court organs have no so-called safe account. Any so-called judicial body that handles cases or takes statements by phone, QQ, WeChat or online is a scam." Abroad, scammers also pose as overseas consulates, saying you are suspected of money laundering, have important documents to collect, or have a problem with your passport or visa, and then transfer the call to "police" in China. The Chinese embassy in the US warned students: "anyone who claims to be an overseas consulate, carrier or customs official and transfers your call to the public security organs on suspicion of illegal activity is a scam!" Pig-butchering scams: "a 'lover' you have never met, only seen in photos or videos, should not be trusted easily", and someone who never meets or video-calls you but always has urgent money needs. Seductive fake-order scams: you are asked to "do tasks" before a meeting, and the money you pay keeps growing. Spotting these early is far cheaper than a payment stop later. How much a payment stop can recover depends on whether the money is still in the account when you report. For how to request a stop, see item 2 (call 110 or 96110 to request a payment stop) (nationwide, since December 2022).

Real loans never require a deposit, and the courts and prosecutors have no "safe accounts". Remember these two points and you can block most of these scams. ### 4. Seeing a face on video or hearing a voice on the phone does not verify anyone. For anything involving money, hang up first and call back on the old number in your contacts
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. One extra phone call and a few more minutes. Share fewer front-facing videos and long voice messages on public accounts. The hard part is that the other side often pushes hard, and you must resist transferring money on the spot.
- Plain language: Seeing a face or hearing a voice does not prove identity. If money is involved, hang up first, then call the old number saved in your contacts or ask a mutual acquaintance. If the other person says "bad signal" or "in a meeting" and refuses a face-to-face video call, treat it as a scam.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Public Security's cybersecurity bureau offers three methods. The first is an action check: ask the person to make a continuous turning motion, or fully cover the face with a palm and quickly move it away. The original text says: "current AI technology, when simulating these complex facial occlusions and changes in light and shadow, is prone to stuttering, blurring or distortion." The second is listening for voice details: "AI-generated speech sometimes lacks natural breathing pauses and emotional variation, or has a slight mechanical quality or unusual background noise." The third is verification through multiple channels. The original calls this "the most core move": "after hanging up, call a phone number of the other person that you often use and are familiar with (do not call back the suspicious number), or verify indirectly through a third person you both know." The slogan is "don't trust video or voice easily; verify with action checks." Under the law, face swapping and voice swapping have long been banned for use in fraud. Services for generating faces, replacing faces, synthesizing voices and imitating voices must carry a clear label, and no organization or person may maliciously delete, alter, forge or hide that label. But it is the compliant platforms that follow these rules, and scammers will not add labels, so "I did not see an AI label" cannot be taken as proof that the other person is real. The Criminal Investigation Bureau of the Ministry of Public Security once disclosed a case: a 14-person gang was caught with more than 50,000 synthetic dynamic face videos and over 500,000 pieces of citizens' personal information. Police also seized 60 cloud servers (nationwide; labeling obligations apply from 1 September 2025).
- Evidence level: B

- Notes: China only: Call back using the old number saved in your contacts. Do not call the number shown on caller ID, and do not use a new number the other person sent in chat. If the other person refuses a face-to-face video call or a specified action, citing "bad signal" or "in a meeting", treat it as a scam. The action-check method is only a supplement, since AI keeps improving. The only reliable method is the third one, verification. If money has already been transferred, see item 2 in this section (call 110 immediately to request a payment stop). For protecting your own face and voice from being collected, see section 14. ### 5. If you are accused or summoned, get a lawyer first. Do not settle privately and do not delete records
<!-- tags: money=high time=mid will=some gain=high scope=freedom -->
- Cost: Lawyer fees start at a few thousand yuan. If you have financial difficulty, you can apply to a legal aid organization for a free lawyer. When questioned, answer only questions relevant to the case and leave the rest to your lawyer. The hard part is staying calm on the spot instead of rushing to explain yourself.
- Plain language: You can get a lawyer from your very first questioning. When your lawyer asks to meet you, the detention center must arrange it within 48 hours at the latest, and the conversation will not be monitored. You may decline to answer questions unrelated to this case.
- Benefit: From the first questioning, or from the time you are subjected to a compulsory measure (restriction of personal liberty), you have the right to appoint a lawyer. While the case is still under investigation by the police, you may appoint only a lawyer, not anyone else, as your defender. When a lawyer requests a meeting, the detention center must arrange it within 48 hours at the latest, and the meeting will not be monitored. No one may be forced to confirm their own guilt. You have the right to refuse questions unrelated to the case. A summons or a forced escort may last at most 12 hours each time, and up to 24 hours for especially serious or complex cases. A summons notifies you to attend questioning; a forced escort brings you in by force. Consecutive summonses may not be used to hold someone in disguise. Anyone who fabricates facts to falsely accuse another person, aiming to have them criminally prosecuted, faces up to 3 years; if serious consequences result, 3 to 10 years. If someone is detained or arrested and the case is later withdrawn, not prosecuted, or ends in acquittal, they may apply for state compensation. Compensation is calculated per day, based on the previous year's national average daily wage for employees (nationwide).
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2018). 刑事诉讼法（2018 年修正，第三十四、三十五、三十九、五十二、一百一十九、一百二十条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201810/t20181027_396818.shtml>；全国人大 (1997). 刑法（第二百四十三条）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>；全国人大常委会 (2012). 国家赔偿法（2012 年修正，第十七、三十三条）. <https://www.stats.gov.cn/gk/tjfg/xgfxfg/202503/t20250306_1958899.html>（国家统计局转载）
- Notes: China only: Do not look for the other person privately, or their family. Any "private settlement" may be taken as an admission. Chat logs, call logs and travel records are the material that proves your innocence, and deleting them means deleting your own evidence. If you misremembered, or the report turns out to be unfounded, that is not the crime of false accusation. This applies equally to accused men and women. For the three expanded points, see item 33 (pursuing the party who fabricated facts), item 34 (acquittal when evidence is insufficient, and appeals and retrial) and item 35 (state compensation for detention, calculated per day).

### 6. If something happens, go and explain it yourself. Self-surrender can cut your sentence by up to 40%, and minor offenders may be exempt. Hiding until the limitation period runs out does not work
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=freedom -->
- Cost: No money. Turn yourself in to a public security organ, or stay where you are and call the police, then tell the whole story truthfully. The hard part is resisting the thought "let's wait and see, maybe no one will investigate."
- Plain language: Self-surrender can cut your sentence by up to 40%, and minor offenders may not be punished at all. Confessing only after being tracked down earns up to 20%. Running away or denying everything earns nothing. Hiding does not work either, because once a case is filed, the time you spend hiding does not count toward the limitation period.
- Benefit: Criminal Law Article 67: a person who voluntarily surrenders after committing a crime and truthfully confesses it is a self-surrenderer. For self-surrender, the penalty may be lighter or reduced, and for minor crimes it may be waived. Someone who does not surrender but truthfully confesses their crime (confession) may receive a lighter penalty; if the confession prevents a particularly serious consequence, the penalty may be reduced. The sentencing guidelines of the Supreme People's Court and the Supreme People's Procuratorate set out explicit ranges. The base sentence is the term first calculated from the facts of the case, and the percentages below are reductions from it. Self-surrender may reduce the base sentence by up to 40%; for minor crimes, by more than 40%, or the penalty may be waived by law. For confession, a reduction of up to 20% applies when the person confesses their own crime. If they confess a more serious crime of the same kind that judicial organs have not yet discovered, the reduction is 10% to 30%. If the confession prevents a particularly serious consequence, the reduction is 30% to 50%. Admitting guilt and accepting punishment: reduction of up to 30%. Where there are several circumstances, such as self-surrender, a major confession, return of stolen goods and compensation, or obtaining forgiveness, the reduction is up to 60%; for minor crimes, more than 60%, or the penalty may be waived by law. The limitation period falls into four tiers based on the maximum legal penalty: 5, 10, 15 and 20 years (Criminal Law Article 87). But Article 88 sets out an exception: once a procuratorate, public security organ or state security organ has filed a case, or a court has accepted one, the limitation period does not apply to someone who evades investigation or trial (nationwide; sentencing ranges in effect since 1 July 2021).

- Notes: China only: This entry is about your own benefit. Self-surrender is only a mitigating factor. The crime is still convicted, and the court decides how much is reduced. The sentencing guidance requires stricter discretion for reductions in serious violent crimes and drug crimes. Traffic accidents have their own calculation. Staying at the scene, rescuing people and reporting to the public security organ count as voluntary surrender, but since these are already legal duties, the reduction must be applied strictly. Surrendering after running away still counts as self-surrender, but the base penalty is set at the heavier legal tier, and then it is decided whether and how much to reduce. For what to do at the accident scene, see item 1 in this section (after a traffic accident, stop, help and call the police). ### 11. You may fight back against harm you cannot avoid, but only strike the person who is attacking, and stop when they stop

- Plain language: If someone is attacking you or others, you may stop them by force, and injuring them is not a crime if you do not clearly go too far. But you may only strike the attacker, and you must stop once they stop. Even if you are cleared, you may first be charged and held for days.
- Benefit: The Criminal Law sets out what counts as legitimate defense. When the state, public interests, or your own or others' person, property or other rights are being unlawfully infringed, stopping the attacker by force, including injuring them, is legitimate defense and not a crime. The same article says: "Where legitimate defense clearly exceeds the necessary limit and causes serious damage, criminal liability shall be borne, but the penalty shall be reduced or waived." Another paragraph states: "Defensive acts against violent crimes in progress, such as serious assault, murder, robbery, rape or kidnapping, or other violent crimes that seriously endanger personal safety, which result in the death or injury of the unlawful attacker, do not constitute excessive defense and do not entail criminal liability." An attack ends only when the attacker has actually lost the ability to attack or has actually given up. Defense must be directed at the attacker personally. If the attack is clearly minor and you can see that, but you stop it with means that could cause serious injury or death, it is not recognized as defense. Official cases show the costs. ### 18. When lending money, write a clear IOU; before guaranteeing someone else's debt, think carefully about whether you would be willing to repay it
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: Costs no money. Use one sheet of paper to write down the lender, borrower, amount, interest rate, term and repayment method, and have both sign. Pay by bank transfer so there is a record. Don't pay in cash.
- Plain language: Write the IOU in full: lender, borrower, amount, interest rate, term, repayment method, and both signatures. Pay by bank transfer, not cash, so the record is complete enough to sue on. When you act as a guarantor, check whether the words "joint and several" appear. Under an ordinary guarantee, the creditor must first pursue the borrower's assets before it reaches you. A joint and several guarantee lets the creditor come to you directly.
- Benefit: A loan contract should be in writing, and generally covers the type of loan, currency, purpose, amount, interest rate, term and repayment method. A complete contract is easier to sue on. If a guarantee contract does not state how the guarantee works, or is unclear, it is treated as an ordinary guarantee. An ordinary guarantor can wait: the creditor must first sue or arbitrate, then apply to enforce against the borrower's property. Only if enforcement still fails to recover the debt does the guarantor have to repay. Conversely, if you sign a "joint and several liability guarantee," you lose this protection, and the creditor can come to you directly (national).

### 19. Rights have deadlines: 3 years for a civil lawsuit and 1 year for labor arbitration, and one sentence from the other side saying "time-barred" is enough to defeat you
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: Costs no money. Don't just remind the debtor in your head. Leave a trace by WeChat, text message, email or registered mail.
- Plain language: The civil limitation period is 3 years, counted from the day you know your rights were violated. After it expires, the court will not act on its own, but the other side only needs to say "time-barred" in court. The money is still owed, but it can no longer be recovered. Within the 3 years, one traceable payment reminder restarts the 3-year clock. Labor disputes follow a separate rule: arbitration has a 1-year limit.
- Benefit: The Civil Code provides that "the limitation period for requesting the people's court to protect civil rights is three years." It runs from "the day the right holder knows or should know that their rights have been infringed and who the obligor is." That means from the day you know you were wronged and know whom to pursue. The period can run no longer than 20 years from the day the right was infringed. Once the period expires, "the obligor may raise a defense of non-performance." The court does not check limitation on its own, but if the other side raises it in court, it takes effect. The money remains, but it can no longer be recovered. Four things interrupt the limitation period, and once interrupted it restarts at 3 years: first, you ask the person who owes you to perform; second, they agree to perform; third, you sue or apply for arbitration; fourth, other circumstances with the same effect. The interruption must happen before the 3 years end, because reminders after expiry do not restart the clock. For debts repaid in instalments, the period runs from the due date of the last instalment. Four types of claims are not subject to limitation: first, requests to stop infringement, remove obstruction, or eliminate danger; second, return of your real property and registered movable property; third, child support, parental support and maintenance; fourth, other cases provided by law. Labor disputes are different: the arbitration limit is 1 year, counted from the day you know or should know your rights were infringed. But "during the continuance of the employment relationship, if a dispute arises over unpaid wages, the worker may apply for arbitration without being subject to the limitation period in paragraph 1 of this Article; however, if the employment relationship ends, the application must be made within one year from the date of termination" (national).

### 34. Insufficient evidence should result in acquittal, and confessions obtained by coercion must be excluded; there are appeals and retrials after judgment
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=some gain=high scope=freedom -->
- Cost: Going through the process costs nothing, but appeals and retrials take a lot of time. You usually need a lawyer, and fees start at several thousand yuan (RMB). If money is tight, you can apply for legal aid; see item 5 of this section (get a lawyer first). The hard part is waiting through each round without giving up.
- Plain language: The threshold for conviction is written into the Criminal Procedure Law. Evidence used to decide the case must be verified through legal procedure, and taken together, it must exclude reasonable doubt. If that cannot be done, the defendant must be acquitted. Confessions obtained by torture, and testimony obtained by violence or threats, must be excluded and cannot be the basis of a judgment. - Benefit: Article 55 of the Criminal Procedure Law: evidence must be sufficient, meaning that the evidence used to decide the case has been verified through legal procedure, and that, taken together, the evidence excludes reasonable doubt about the facts found. "Excluding reasonable doubt" means that, looking at all the evidence together, the facts found cannot still allow another plausible explanation. Article 56: confessions of suspects or defendants obtained by torture or other illegal methods, and testimony of witnesses or statements of victims obtained by violence, threats or other illegal methods, must be excluded. When evidence that must be excluded is found during investigation, review for prosecution, or trial, it shall be excluded in accordance with the law and may not be used as the basis for an indictment, a prosecution decision or a judgment. Article 200(3): where evidence is insufficient to find the defendant guilty, the court must issue an acquittal stating that the evidence is insufficient and the charged crime does not hold. Article 252 provides the appeal route: parties, their legal representatives and close relatives may petition the court or the procuratorate about judgments and rulings that have taken effect. But an appeal does not suspend enforcement. Article 253 lists situations in which the court must retry the case (nationwide).

### 38. The "insure them first, then act" route is blocked in law from the start: you get no money, and the crimes are charged as intentional homicide plus insurance fraud, with combined sentencing
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=high scope=freedom -->

If the beneficiary deliberately causes the insured's death, disability or illness, or attempts to kill the insured, the beneficiary loses the right to benefits. Article 27, paragraph 2: if the policyholder or the insured deliberately causes the insured event, the insurer may terminate the contract and is not liable to pay compensation or benefits; except as provided in Article 43, premiums are not refunded. Article 1125 of the Civil Code: a person who intentionally kills the decedent loses inheritance rights. Article 198, paragraph 1, item 5 of the Criminal Law lists as insurance fraud "the policyholder or beneficiary deliberately causing the death, disability or illness of the insured in order to fraudulently obtain insurance benefits." If the amount is relatively large, the penalty is up to 5 years and a fine of 10,000 to 100,000 yuan. If the amount is huge, 5 to 10 years. If it is particularly huge, 10 years or more. Early on July 18, 2016, he drove into a pond, and his wife drowned. Less than half a month before, he had insured her under four policies of 1 million yuan each. He was arrested on July 25. The court convicted him of intentional homicide and insurance fraud, applied aggregate punishment, and sentenced him to death with a two-year reprieve, plus a fine of 50,000 yuan.

### 40. Do not give money or cards to people who handle cases or enforce the law: giving a bribe is itself a crime, and bribing supervisory, enforcement or judicial staff is punished more heavily

### 45. If someone you know asks you to "help with face verification and be a witness" for a loan, or to read out a verification code, do not agree: this is an "AB loan," and you are the borrower, not a guarantor
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. The hard part is refusing a relative or friend face to face. If they say "it's only being a witness" or "you bear no responsibility," still refuse. Do not hand your phone, ID card or bank card to an intermediary, and do not complete face verification or signing on the intermediary's instructions. Verification codes are for your own use only; do not read them out, even to someone you know.
- Plain language: Being asked to "be a witness" for a loan usually means borrowing from a bank in your name. You are the borrower, not a guarantor, and the bank will come to you for repayment. If the bank did not know about the fraud, you cannot cancel the contract. A pledge from the acquaintance that "I'll repay it" does not bind the bank.
- Benefit: The Fujian regulator of the National Financial Regulatory Administration issued a 2022 risk alert describing this scheme. Customer A's credit was not enough to get a loan. The intermediary said a guarantor was needed, and asked A to find B, who had better credit. In practice, the intermediary used B's identity information to apply for the loan, and the money went to A. The alert says "the borrower is in substance B," and B "only learned they were the borrower rather than the guarantor when the bank began collection." The money went into B's account, B transferred it to A, and A paid more than 80,000 yuan in fees. ### 46. Fit good locks on doors and windows, a motion-sensor light outside the door, and actually lock up when you leave: together these protect against burglary, while an alarm on its own shows no benefit
<!-- tags: money=low time=low will=some gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: Replacing the door lock with one that needs a key to lock from inside, adding window locks, and installing a motion-sensor light outside the door generally cost tens to hundreds of yuan. Replacing the whole security door costs over 1,000 yuan. The hard part is actually locking the doors and windows every time you leave, including on summer days when windows are open.
- Plain language: In England and Wales, homes with a door lock and window locks were about 12.5 times less likely to be burgled than homes with no security devices. Adding an outdoor motion or timer light brought that to about 34 times. An alarm on its own did no good and may even raise the risk. This covers theft, not robbery when someone is at home.
- Benefit: A crime survey in England and Wales asked about security devices in 37,416 homes between 2008 and 2012, then compared them with homes that were burgled. Homes with no devices made up only 4.9% of all homes, but 36.6% of the homes that were burgled. The authors calculated a "protection factor": how many times less likely a home is to be burgled than a home with no devices. A double lock or deadlock on the door (a bolt that needs a key to retract) plus window locks gave 12.54 times. Here "door lock" means double locks or deadlocks. Door and window locks plus an outdoor motion-sensor or timer light gave 34.41 times. Adding an indoor timer light gave 49 times. With a single item, an outdoor light gave about 3 times and a door lock about 2.8 times. Window locks alone showed no difference. Homes with all five devices were no better than homes with the right three. Alarm only: the burglary rate was actually higher than in homes with nothing. Homes with a fake alarm box also had a higher chance of a break-in attempt that failed. Locks must actually be locked to count. Burglaries involving picked locks, smashed windows or removed door panels fell from 31 per thousand households in 1993 to 7 per thousand in 2006/07, a drop of 77%. Burglaries where the thief simply walked in through an unlocked door or window stayed at 7 per thousand and did not fall (England and Wales).
