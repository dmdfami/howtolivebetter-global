# 20. Caring for a Newborn

Scope: infant mortality and money. This section covers only matters that affect whether a child survives or how much you have to spend, and only where the difference is large and the hard evidence is solid. Everyday childcare details, such as how to soothe a baby, are not covered in this book. Tasks before birth and on the day of discharge from hospital are in section 27.

### 1. Put the baby to sleep on their back, on a firm flat surface, in the same room but not the same bed, with no soft objects in the bed
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=high gain=high scope=health who=infant,pregnancy -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. A firm mattress costs a few hundred yuan. The hard part is holding firm against elders' advice and keeping to this every time.
- Plain language: While a baby is still an infant, lay the baby face up on a firm, level surface. A sloped cradle or baby chair is not a safe place to sleep. Adults and the baby can share a room, but not a bed. Keep pillows, quilts, bed bumpers and plush toys out of the bed. In the United States, about 3,500 babies die each year from sleep-related causes.
- Benefit: About 3,500 babies in the United States die each year from sleep-related causes. These include sudden infant death syndrome (SIDS), deaths of unknown cause, and accidental suffocation in bed. The American Academy of Pediatrics recommends this safe sleep environment: "supine position; a firm, non-inclined sleep surface; room-sharing without bed-sharing; avoid soft bedding and overheating." A few other steps also lower the risk: breastfeeding, keeping the baby away from nicotine and alcohol, vaccinating on schedule, and using a pacifier.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: Moon RY, Carlin RF, Hand I, Task Force on Sudden Infant Death Syndrome and the Committee on Fetus and Newborn (2022). Sleep-Related Infant Deaths: Updated 2022 Recommendations for Reducing Infant Deaths in the Sleep Environment. Pediatrics, 150(1), e2022057990. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2022-057990>
- Notes: "No soft objects in the bed" means no pillows, quilts, bed bumpers, plush toys or shaped pillows. Keep out even nursing pillows, which look safe. A sloped cradle or baby chair is not a place to sleep. Of all the items in this section, this one makes the biggest difference, and it is the one you most need to hold firm on despite pressure from elders.

### 2. Give the first hepatitis B vaccine dose within 24 hours of birth
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=high scope=health who=infant,pregnancy -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. The hepatitis B vaccine is part of the national immunization program, so it is free. It is given in hospital on the day the baby is born, so no extra trip is needed.
- Plain language: The first hepatitis B shot should be given within 24 hours of birth. About 95% of people infected as babies or toddlers develop chronic hepatitis. Most adult infections clear on their own, so early vaccination matters most for infants. If the mother tests positive for hepatitis B surface antigen, the baby also needs immunoglobulin within 12 hours of birth.
- Benefit: The WHO says: "All infants should receive the hepatitis B vaccine as early as possible, within 24 hours of birth." The reason given is that "infection in infancy and early childhood develops into chronic hepatitis in about 95% of cases." Most infections acquired in adulthood resolve on their own, without treatment. The WHO's wording is that fewer than "5%" of adult infections become chronic. The National Health Commission's guidelines on preventing mother-to-child transmission require that babies born to mothers who are hepatitis B surface antigen positive receive "the first dose of hepatitis B vaccine as early as possible within 12 hours of birth, together with an injection of 100 international units of hepatitis B immunoglobulin."
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: World Health Organization. Hepatitis B fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/hepatitis-b>；国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2020). 预防艾滋病、梅毒和乙肝母婴传播工作规范（2020 年版）. <https://wjw.xinjiang.gov.cn/hfpc/zhgl6/202012/08b7de496f0b40e586d4b99d00cf79c4.shtml>（新疆维吾尔自治区卫生健康委员会转载）
- Notes: If the mother's hepatitis B surface antigen is positive, the baby also needs an immunoglobulin shot after birth, alongside the vaccine. Check this during prenatal care, not after delivery. For the mother's own screening, see section 1.

### 3. Get every vaccine in the national immunization program on schedule. They are free, and if one is missed, catch up only the doses not yet given
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=no gain=high scope=health who=infant,child,pregnancy -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. The law says vaccination units may not charge any fee for vaccines in the national immunization program. You still need several trips to the clinic, by age. Keep the immunization certificate safe so it is not lost.
- Plain language: Vaccines in the national immunization program are free, and clinics may not charge for them. Nurseries and schools check vaccination records, so missed doses are usually caught then. If a dose is missed, catch up only the missed doses. You do not start over, and switching manufacturer is fine.
- Benefit: When a child enters nursery or school, the institution must check the vaccination certificate. If a child has not been vaccinated as the national immunization program requires, the institution must report it to the vaccination unit and cooperate in urging the guardian to catch up. So a missed vaccine will generally be found when the certificate is checked for nursery or school entry. The schedule in force is the 2026 edition. The human papillomavirus (HPV) vaccine has been added to the national immunization program. The DTaP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis) vaccine's starting age has changed from 3 months to 2 months. There are 5 doses, given at 2 months, 4 months, 6 months, 18 months and 6 years. Eligible children in Tibet, Qinghai, Xinjiang and the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps have also been routinely given the Japanese encephalitis (JE) vaccine since March 2026. Several vaccines can be given on the same day: all vaccines currently in the national immunization program may be given together under the schedule or the catch-up rules. Two or more injected vaccines must be given in different sites, and "mixing two or more vaccines in the same syringe is strictly forbidden." If two or more injected live attenuated vaccines are not given on the same day, there must be an interval of at least 28 days between them. There is no interval requirement between inactivated vaccines, oral live attenuated vaccines and other vaccines. For children under 18, the catch-up principle is to catch up as early as possible: "only the unfinished doses need to be given; the full course does not need to be restarted." The same vaccine can be continued with a different manufacturer (national, 2026 edition schedule).
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2019). 疫苗管理法（第四十八、四十九条）. <http://ynswsjkw.yn.gov.cn/html/2019/faguiguizhangxin_0731/6509.html>（云南省卫生健康委员会转载）；国家疾病预防控制局、国家卫生健康委 (2026). 《国家免疫规划疫苗儿童免疫程序及说明（2026 年版）》解读问答. <https://www.ndcpa.gov.cn/jbkzzx/c100013/common/content/content_2073002930292035584.html>
- Notes: China only: The most common misunderstanding is "the child's constitution is weak, so don't vaccinate yet." Official guidance is that a so-called "allergic constitution," a family history of allergic disease, or a past food or drug allergy are not reasons to skip vaccination. Atopic dermatitis (eczema), urticaria, allergic rhinitis and bronchial asthma can all be vaccinated once the condition is stable. Children taking antiallergy drugs or inhaled corticosteroids can also be vaccinated, as can babies with physiological neonatal jaundice or breast-milk jaundice. Only two things are real contraindications: a severe acute allergic reaction to the same vaccine before, or a severe allergy to a known ingredient in the vaccine. Children with immunodeficiency, and children undergoing chemotherapy or radiotherapy or taking immunosuppressants, need individual assessment. Discuss their case at a vaccination clinic before deciding. For the hepatitis B first dose, see item 2. Self-paid vaccines outside the national program are charged a vaccine fee plus a service fee. The "free" in this item does not apply to them.

### 4. Breastfeed only for the first 6 months, with no water either. From 6 months, add solid food and keep breastfeeding
<!-- tags: money=0 time=high will=high gain=mid scope=health who=infant,pregnancy -->
- Cost: Costs nothing, and saves the cost of formula. The cost is time, since feeding takes up a fair part of every day. The hard part is keeping it up without a break in the first 6 months.
- Plain language: Start breastfeeding within an hour of birth. Give only breast milk for the first 6 months, no water either. From 6 months, add solid food every day, including iron-rich animal foods, and keep breastfeeding to age 2 or longer. If breast milk is insufficient or not possible, use formula.
- Benefit: The WHO recommends "starting breastfeeding within the first hour after birth." For the first 6 months, babies should have "exclusive breastfeeding, meaning no other food or liquids, including water." Then, "from 6 months of age, start adding safe and adequate complementary foods, while continuing breastfeeding to 2 years or beyond." The National Health Commission's service guidelines also promote exclusive breastfeeding from 0 to 6 months. This guideline requires that from 6 months, "daily complementary food must include iron-rich animal foods."
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: World Health Organization. Breastfeeding. <https://www.who.int/health-topics/breastfeeding>；国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2024). 婴幼儿营养喂养评估服务指南（试行）（国卫办妇幼函〔2024〕452 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202502/content_7002872.htm>
- Notes: If breast milk is not enough, or breastfeeding is not possible for medical reasons, use formula. There is no need to feel guilty. Breastfeeding versus formula makes a much smaller difference than the safe sleep item; this is the author's judgment. For how often to add complementary feeds, follow the health commission guideline: 1 to 2 times a day at 6 to 8 months, and 2 to 3 times a day at 9 to 12 months. Choose daily complementary foods from seven food groups, using at least four of them. If the child has severe eczema or an egg allergy, whether to avoid peanuts is covered in item 12 of this section (do not avoid peanuts). If the mother is vegetarian, or wants the child to be, see item 14 of this section (on a vegetarian diet, take vitamin B12 daily).

### 5. Mix formula with water at 70°C or above, let it cool before feeding, and pour away any leftovers
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=health who=infant,pregnancy -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. Each feed takes a few extra minutes while the milk cools. The hard part is waiting for the water to cool before mixing when the baby is crying with hunger.
- Plain language: Formula powder is not sterile and can carry a bacterium. Pour in hot water, let it cool to about 73.8°C, then add the powder. This usually brings the bacteria down to under one hundred-thousandth of the original level. Hot water lowers some vitamin C, but tested formulas still meet the standard. Let the milk cool before feeding, and throw away leftovers.
- Benefit: Powdered formula is not sterile and may contain Cronobacter sakazakii. The reported fatality rate for newborns infected with it is 40% to 80%. Experimental studies show that if hot water is poured into the bottle first, and the powder is added once the water has cooled to about 73.8°C, the milk is still above 70°C in most cases. This reduces the bacteria by more than 5 log units, to under one hundred-thousandth of the original level. The cost of hot water is vitamin C. WHO and the UN Food and Agriculture Organization held an expert meeting in 2006. The data seen there showed that when formula was reconstituted with boiling water, only vitamin C was significantly reduced, by 5.6% to 65.6% across four formulas. Formula is manufactured with extra vitamin C added to begin with. The formula with the largest loss still had 9.0 mg per 100 kcal after reconstitution, above the Codex Alimentarius minimum of 8 mg. The meeting saw only this one study, so it drew no conclusion on this point.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: Beary MA, Daly SE, Baker J, Snyder AB (2025). Assessing Hot Water Reconstitution Instructions and Labeling of Powdered Infant Formula to Ensure Cronobacter spp. Reduction. Journal of Food Protection, 88(9), 100571. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfp.2025.100571>；Sima WG, Legesse T, Girma S, et al. (2025). Emerging microbial risks: Cronobacter sakazakii in powdered infant formula for infants under six months of age in Ethiopia. BMC Microbiology, 26, 307. <https://doi.org/10.1186/s12866-025-04380-y>；World Health Organization & FAO (2007). Safe preparation, storage and handling of powdered infant formula: guidelines. <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241595414>；Bundesinstitut für Risikobewertung (2022). Recommendations for the hygienic preparation of powdered infant formula. Updated BfR Opinion No. 009/2022. <https://doi.org/10.17590/20220510-104706>；Agostoni C, Axelsson I, Goulet O, et al. (2004). Preparation and handling of powdered infant formula: a commentary by the ESPGHAN Committee on Nutrition. Journal of Pediatric Gastroenterology and Nutrition, 39(4), 320-322. <https://doi.org/10.1097/00005176-200410000-00002>；Silano M, Paganin P, Davanzo R (2016). Time for the 70 °C water precautionary option in the home dilution of powdered infant formula. Italian Journal of Pediatrics, 42, 17. <https://doi.org/10.1186/s13052-016-0228-9>；Losio MN, Pavoni E, Finazzi G, et al. (2018). Preparation of Powdered Infant Formula: Could Product's Safety Be Improved? Journal of Pediatric Gastroenterology and Nutrition, 67(4), 543-546. <https://doi.org/10.1097/MPG.0000000000002100>
- Notes: Disputed: The German Federal Institute for Risk Assessment's 2022 opinion is that healthy full-term babies can be fed formula mixed with water at 20 to 50°C. It notes that most manufacturers' tins also say 40 to 50°C. Its reasoning is that 70°C water cools quickly once poured into the bottle, so the bacteria actually killed are fewer than calculated. Hot water also loses some nutrients, and feeding before the milk has cooled can scald the baby. The European Society for Paediatric Gastroenterology, Hepatology and Nutrition (ESPGHAN) in 2004 also argued against mixing with boiling water, worrying that it would damage vitamins and the probiotics added to some formulas. WHO and FAO still recommend 70°C or above. Preterm babies, babies under 2,500 g at birth, babies under 2 months old and babies with weak immunity are at highest risk. For them, mix at 70°C, or ask a doctor. For healthy full-term babies, follow the temperature on the tin. The German opinion judges the infection risk to be very low in this case. The 70°C threshold comes from public health guidelines. Beary et al. wrote: "Public health guidelines for caregivers of high-risk infants recommend reconstituting infant formula with water heated to at least 70°C (158°F) to inactivate microorganisms." Do not use water left in a thermos for a day, and do not leave mixed milk at room temperature to be fed at the next feed.

### 6. Do not give honey to babies under 1 year old
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=health who=infant,pregnancy -->
- Cost: Costs nothing.
- Plain language: Do not give honey to a child under 1 year old. This includes honey water, honey mixed into solid food, and cakes or pastries containing honey. Honey can contain bacteria that cause botulism. An adult's gut bacteria can keep these bacteria in check, but a baby's cannot.
- Benefit: The US CDC says: "Do not give honey to children under 1 year of age," because "honey may contain bacteria that can cause botulism." Adults' intestinal flora can suppress these spores (the dormant form of the bacterium), but infants cannot.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: US CDC. Preventing Botulism. <https://www.cdc.gov/botulism/prevention/index.html>
- Notes: Not allowed: honey water, solid food mixed with honey, and honey-containing cakes.

### 7. The vitamin K injection at birth must be given
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=high scope=health who=infant,pregnancy -->
- Cost: 0 to a few dozen yuan. This cost is usually already included in the hospital fee for giving birth, so no separate payment is needed.
- Plain language: Newborns store very little vitamin K, so without a shot they can bleed. The late-onset form often causes bleeding in the brain. In Tashkent, where this prevention was not routine, 478 in every 100,000 newborns had this bleeding. Prevention could cut the rate by more than 78.5%. If your baby was born at home or in an informal facility, ask whether the shot was given.
- Benefit: Newborns have very little vitamin K stored in the body. Without supplementation, vitamin K deficiency bleeding can occur. The late-onset form often presents as intracranial bleeding (bleeding in the brain). The Belgian consensus recommends "a single intramuscular injection of 1 or 2 mg of vitamin K at birth." In areas without a system for prevention, the rate of vitamin K deficiency bleeding is 478 cases per 100,000 newborns. This figure comes from 42,000 newborns in Tashkent. The authors estimate that prevention can reduce the rate by "more than 78.5%."
- Evidence level: B
- Sources: Fiesack S, Smits A, Rayyan M, et al. (2021). Nutrients, 13(11), 4109. <https://doi.org/10.3390/nu13114109>；Tursunov D, Yoshida Y, Yrysov K, et al. (2018). Nagoya Journal of Medical Science, 80(1), 11. <https://doi.org/10.18999/nagjms.80.1.11>
- Notes: China only: At reputable hospitals in China, this shot is usually given routinely. This is the author's experience; no nationwide official rule has been found. Check the discharge summary before leaving hospital, or ask. If the baby was born at home or in an informal facility, confirm it proactively. The injection here means into the muscle. The Belgian consensus calls oral dosing "a slightly less effective alternative," to be used only when parents refuse the injection: 2 mg at birth, then 1 to 2 mg weekly for breastfed babies, for 3 months.

### 8. A baby under 3 months old with a temperature of 38°C goes straight to hospital, not home observation
<!-- tags: money=low time=low will=no gain=high scope=health who=infant,pregnancy -->
- Cost: A few hundred yuan for registration and tests.
- Plain language: A baby under 3 months with a temperature of 38°C should go straight to hospital. Do not watch at home, and do not give fever medicine and wait. Even a baby who seems well can have a serious infection. UK guidance counts such babies as high risk.
- Benefit: The American Academy of Pediatrics has a dedicated guideline for well-appearing full-term babies aged 8 to 60 days. It defines fever as "temperature ≥38.0°C." It sorts babies into three bands, 8 to 21 days, 22 to 28 days and 29 to 60 days, each with its own management pathway. Babies under 8 days old fall outside this guideline. The UK NICE fever guideline covers children under 5. It states that babies under 3 months with a temperature of 38°C or above are in the "high-risk group for serious illness."
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: Pantell RH, Roberts KB, Adams WG, et al. (2021). Evaluation and Management of Well-Appearing Febrile Infants 8 to 60 Days Old. Pediatrics, 148(2), e2021052228. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2021-052228>；NICE (2019, 2021 更新). Fever in under 5s: assessment and initial management (NG143), 1.2.12. <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng143/chapter/Recommendations>
- Notes: At this age, a serious bacterial infection can show up only as fever. So "looks fine" is not a reason to observe at home. Also do not give fever medicine yourself and wait to see.

### 9. Never shake a baby, no matter how tired or angry you are
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=high gain=high scope=health who=infant,pregnancy -->
- Cost: Costs nothing. The hard part is stopping yourself when you are exhausted and furious.
- Plain language: A baby's head is heavy and its neck is weak. Violent shaking can cause bleeding in the brain and eyes, which can kill a baby or leave lifelong disability. Gentle rocking to settle a baby is not the danger. If you feel you are about to lose control, put the baby down in the cot, walk away for a few minutes, and let them cry. That is safer than holding and shaking.
- Benefit: A baby's head is large and the neck muscles are weak. Severe shaking causes bleeding in the brain and in the retina (the back of the eye). This can be fatal or leave lifelong disability. Gentle rocking to settle a baby is not included. The danger is uncontrolled, violent shaking. Medicine calls injuries caused by shaking or hitting "abusive head trauma". It is the leading cause of death from head injury in children under 2. In the US from 1999 to 2014, it killed nearly 2,250 children under 5.
- Evidence level: B
- Sources: Spies EL, Klevens J (2016). Fatal Abusive Head Trauma Among Children Aged <5 Years — United States, 1999–2014. MMWR 65(20):505-509. <https://doi.org/10.15585/mmwr.mm6520a1>；Choudhary AK 等 (2018). Consensus statement on abusive head trauma in infants and young children. Pediatric Radiology 48(8):1048-1065. <https://doi.org/10.1007/s00247-018-4149-1>
- Notes: If you are really about to lose control, put the baby back in the cot and walk away for a few minutes. Letting them cry for a while is much safer than holding them and shaking. Anyone caring for a baby alone who is getting little sleep should remember this above all. For how to handle your own emotions, see section 3.

### 10. Choose diapers by fit, how often you change them, and whether they have been named in a safety spot check, not by brand
<!-- tags: money=low time=low will=no gain=mid scope=money who=infant,pregnancy -->
- Cost: RMB 200 to over 1,000 a month, depending on the grade you buy.
- Plain language: No official body ranks diaper brands. You can only check the State Administration for Market Regulation's product quality spot-check bulletins and regulators' investigations of specific incidents. Expensive does not mean safe, and imported does not mean compliant. Buy a small pack, try it for a week, and check for nappy rash and waistband marks before stocking up.
- Benefit: No official body has published a ranking of "the best brand", so this book does not recommend any brand. Only two kinds of information can be checked. One is the State Administration for Market Regulation's (China's market regulator) product quality spot-check bulletins. The other is regulators' investigations into specific incidents. After the "formamide problem in infant diapers" drew attention in June 2026, the State Administration for Market Regulation led a joint investigation team to look into it.
- Evidence level: C
- Sources: 市场监管总局 (2026-06-22). 市场监管总局牵头成立联合调查组核查「婴幼儿纸尿裤甲酰胺问题」：「针对媒体反映的"婴幼儿纸尿裤甲酰胺问题"，市场监管总局、工业和信息化部、国家卫生健康委、国家疾控局高度重视，成立联合调查组，核查婴幼儿纸尿裤甲酰胺有关问题，并依法依规处理。有关情况将及时公布。」. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/xw/zj/art/2026/art_78c0425db7604b27bd7a3f36ba319975.html>；中国政府网转载. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202606/content_7072931.htm>；NHS. Nappy rash（2026-08-28 审阅）. <https://www.nhs.uk/baby/caring-for-a-newborn/nappy-rash/>
- Notes: China only: When the joint investigation team was set up, it said only that "relevant information will be published in good time". As of 30 September 2026, no conclusion of this investigation had been found on the State Administration for Market Regulation's website or in recent national spot-check bulletins. Practical advice: expensive does not mean safe, and imported does not mean compliant. Buy a small pack, try it for a week, and look for red bottoms and elastic marks before you stock up. These points are the author's own experience. The UK NHS says nappy rash is most often caused by urine and stool sitting on the skin too long, changing too infrequently, and friction. Some nappies that are not absorbent or breathable can also cause it. The NHS also advises that nappies should fit well and not be too tight. For how to check spot-check bulletins, see section 5.

### 11. Buy big items in this order: borrow, then second-hand, then new. Don't buy the whole set at once
<!-- tags: money=low time=low will=some gain=mid scope=money who=infant,pregnancy -->
- Cost: No extra cost. The money you save is usually in the thousands of RMB. The hard part is resisting the urge to buy everything at once.
- Plain language: Prams, cots, blankets and toys are used only briefly, so borrow first, then buy second-hand, and buy new as a last resort. Do not use a second-hand car seat unless you can confirm it has not been in a moderate or serious crash, was not recalled, and has all its parts. Buy a new mattress. A used mattress from another child's home has been linked to a higher risk of sudden death, though the cause is unclear.
- Benefit: Prams, cots, blankets and toys are used for only a short time, and the second-hand market has far more supply than demand. Second-hand goods must meet three safety limits. The first is the car seat. The US National Highway Traffic Safety Administration advises replacing a seat after a moderate or severe crash, and never using it again. A crash counts as minor, and the seat can still be used, only if all five of these conditions are met. The car can be driven away on its own. The door nearest the seat is not damaged. No one in the car was injured. The airbags did not deploy. There is no visible damage to the seat. Before using a second-hand seat, check five things. It has not been in a moderate or worse crash. Its label shows a manufacturing date and model number. It has not been recalled. All its parts are present. The instruction manual is still there. The manufacturing date and model number are used to check for recalls and to see whether the seat is too old. The second limit is the mattress. A Scottish study compared babies who died of sudden infant death syndrome with healthy babies of the same age, and looked back at which mattress each one slept on. Babies who usually slept on another child's used mattress had a higher risk (OR 3.07, about 3 times, 95% CI 1.51–6.22). The figure was 4.78 for mattresses from other people's homes and 1.64 for mattresses from their own home, and the latter is not a meaningful difference. The author says current evidence is not enough to judge whether the mattress itself is the cause. The third limit is the cot. From 28 June 2011, a new mandatory cot standard applies in the US, and second-hand cots must also comply. Traditional cots with a drop-side rail were banned. The US Consumer Product Safety Commission says an old cot can only be judged by breaking it apart, so it cannot offer a self-check method. From November 2007 to April 2010, the US reported 147 deaths linked to standard-size cots. Of these, 35 were caused by structural faults in the cot, and 18 were linked to failure of the drop-side rail. Loose screws caused at least 10 deaths. In the US, inclined sleepers (sleeping baskets that tilt) are banned for babies, regardless of when they were made. This was written into regulation in 2023 (US, UK).
- Evidence level: C
- Sources: 省钱部分为作者经验，无直接文献；US NHTSA. Car Seat Use After a Crash. <https://www.nhtsa.gov/car-seats-and-booster-seats/car-seat-use-after-crash>；US NHTSA. Used Car Seat Safety Checklist. <https://www.nhtsa.gov/car-seats-and-booster-seats/used-car-seat-safety-checklist>；Tappin D, Brooke H, Ecob R, Gibson A (2002). Used infant mattresses and sudden infant death syndrome in Scotland: case-control study. BMJ, 325(7371), 1007. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.325.7371.1007>；Blair P, Fleming P, Smith I (1997). Case-control study of sudden infant death syndrome in Scotland. Income level or bed sharing would confound any effect of previous use of mattress. BMJ, 315(7111), 812. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.315.7111.812a>；US Consumer Product Safety Commission (2010). Safety Standards for Full-Size Baby Cribs and Non-Full-Size Baby Cribs; Final Rule. Federal Register 2010-32178. <https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2010/12/28/2010-32178/safety-standards-for-full-size-baby-cribs-and-non-full-size-baby-cribs-final-rule>；US Consumer Product Safety Commission (2023). Ban of Inclined Sleepers for Infants. Federal Register 2023-17350. <https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2023/08/16/2023-17350/ban-of-inclined-sleepers-for-infants>；冲动消费见第 5 节
- Notes: The evidence level stays C, because the money-saving half has no literature. The three safety limits each have sources, but all of them are from the US and UK. No official Chinese guidance specific to second-hand cots or car seats was found. The mattress study has been challenged: in 1997 a researcher wrote that household income and bed-sharing would confound the effect of a previously used mattress. For why car seats are needed, see section 1 item 10 (car seats). For how babies should sleep and what to keep in the cot, see item 1 of this section (sleeping on the back). This book neither recommends nor rules out postnatal care centres, early-learning classes or baby swimming centres. But they are all large, optional expenses, and the 24-hour cooling-off rule in section 5 applies to them.

### 12. If your baby has severe eczema or egg allergy, don't avoid peanuts. Introduce them early on a doctor's advice, but never give whole peanuts
<!-- tags: money=low time=low will=no gain=high scope=health who=infant,pregnancy -->
- Cost: A jar of peanut butter costs dozens of RMB. Feeding it a few times a week is easy to fold into meals. You need one doctor's visit for an assessment first.
- Plain language: Babies with severe eczema or egg allergy should eat peanut products regularly from 4 to 11 months. Among babies with a negative skin test, 1.9% of those who ate peanuts were allergic at age 5, versus 13.7% of those who avoided them. See a doctor before starting. Never give whole peanuts, which can be inhaled into the windpipe.
- Benefit: The UK LEAP randomised trial enrolled 640 babies with severe eczema, egg allergy, or both. They were aged 4 to 11 months at enrolment. They were randomly split into two groups. One group ate peanut products regularly, and the other avoided peanuts completely. Peanut allergy was checked at 60 months of age. Among the 530 babies with a negative skin test at enrolment, 13.7% in the avoidance group became allergic, versus 1.9% in the eating group (P<0.001). Among the 98 babies whose skin test was already weakly positive, 35.3% in the avoidance group became allergic, versus 10.6% in the eating group (P=0.004). Serious adverse events did not differ between the groups.

### 13. Watch for these newborn jaundice signs: yellow within 24 hours of birth, or hard to wake and not feeding, means going to hospital the same day. If it is getting yellower or the stool is pale, get checked soon
<!-- tags: money=low time=low will=no gain=high scope=health who=infant,pregnancy -->
- Cost: Registration and a bilirubin test cost a few hundred RMB or less. Phototherapy usually takes about two days in hospital. The hard part is making the trip even when the baby seems fine.
- Plain language: More than 80% of newborns look a little yellow. Jaundice within 24 hours of birth, or jaundice with unusual sleepiness, being hard to wake, refusing feeds, or a floppy or stiff body, needs immediate hospital care. Jaundice that gets worse after 24 hours, or pale stools, should be checked soon, since pale stools can mean biliary atresia. Very high bilirubin can damage the brain and cause lifelong disability.
- Benefit: The American Academy of Pediatrics' 2022 guideline says "more than 80% of newborns develop some degree of jaundice." High bilirubin can cause acute bilirubin encephalopathy and kernicterus. The guideline calls kernicterus a "permanent, disabling neurological condition". Its signs include cerebral palsy with involuntary limb movements, inability to look upward, enamel defects in baby teeth, and hearing loss. The guideline lists "jaundice within the first 24 hours after birth" as a risk factor for developing severe jaundice. The UK NHS gives parents two levels of advice. The first is to call an ambulance or go straight to A&E. This applies when the baby is jaundiced and also sleepier than usual or hard to wake, not feeding, floppy or stiff, or twitching. A baby who is jaundiced within the first 24 hours also falls into this level. The second is to book a doctor soon. This applies when a baby is older than 24 hours and the jaundice is not improving or is getting worse, when urine is dark yellow or brown, or when stools are pale cream. Globally, an estimated 480,000 newborns in 2010 had Rh haemolytic disease (caused by incompatible blood types between mother and baby) or severe hyperbilirubinaemia. Of these, 24% died and 13% developed kernicterus. Among survivors with kernicterus, more than 83% were left with one or more impairments. Pale stools may indicate biliary atresia. This condition needs a Kasai procedure within 60 days for clearly better outcomes. From 2004, Taiwan printed a stool colour card in every newborn's child health handbook. The share of babies operated on within 60 days rose from 60% in 2004 to 74.3% in 2005. In another nationwide Taiwanese study, after the colour card was introduced, mortality among babies with biliary atresia fell from 26.2% to 15.9%. This is a before-and-after comparison, not a controlled trial.

<!-- tags: money=low time=low will=some gain=mid scope=health who=pregnancy,infant -->

- Plain language: Vitamin B12 is found almost only in meat, eggs and dairy, so people on fully vegan diets need fortified foods or supplements. - Benefit: In 2003 the US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention reported two children. If your child is scalded, cool the burn with cold water first; see section 13 item 14 (cool a burn with cold water).
