# 24. Seeing a doctor: how to spend less money and avoid detours

Scope: money and time. This section covers how a medical visit works: which hospital to go to first, how to move up to a bigger one, and where you can save money. It also covers how emergency triage works, and which appraisals and certificates you may need after a serious injury has been treated. For benefits you can claim, see section 7. For managing a chronic illness over time, see section 16. For what to do first at the scene of an acute emergency, see section 13.

This section is mainly based on the Several Measures on Speeding Up the Building of a Tiered Medical Care System, issued by the General Office of the State Council in April 2026. Tiered care means deciding which level of hospital to visit first, based on how serious the illness is. The document directly links going to primary care first with saving money. But provinces and cities are rolling it out at different speeds, so the text describes national rules. For your own pooling area, follow what your local medical insurance and health commission say. The pooling area is the area where you are enrolled for medical insurance, usually a city.

### 1. Treat common illnesses at the community clinic first; get referred up step by step, and the inpatient deductible still carries over
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: Free. You have to go to the community clinic first. The hard part is resisting the urge to book a specialist directly.
- Plain language: Go to the community clinic first. If you are referred to a big hospital for admission, the money you already paid still counts toward the deductible. Local rules decide how this works; in Sichuan, you only top up the gap between the two hospitals. If you are sent back to the clinic for the same illness, the deductible is not recalculated.
- Benefit: Insured patients referred step by step from primary care can have their inpatient deductible counted continuously at higher-level hospitals. The deductible is the amount you pay yourself on each admission before insurance starts paying. The State Council document does not say how continuous counting works; local areas decide. Sichuan's method only tops up the difference between two hospitals' deductibles. The Yilong County Traditional Chinese Medicine Hospital deductible is 600 yuan, and the Yilong County People's Hospital deductible is 800 yuan, so moving from one to the other needs only 200 yuan more. For inpatients sent back down from a higher-level hospital to primary care, no new deductible is set within the same disease cycle.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「统筹地区内经基层医疗卫生机构逐级转诊的参保患者，在上级医院的住院起付线可连续计算；由上级医院下转至基层医疗卫生机构的住院患者，同一疾病周期内不再另设住院起付线。」；四川省医疗保障局 (2025). 关于省第十四届人大第四次会议第14041026号建议办理答复的函. <https://ylbzj.sc.gov.cn/scsybj/nc010414/2025/3/19/fea6f0a5f8ba416abf7ed17541afdc7a.shtml>：「仪陇县人民医院起付线是800元，仪陇县中医院起付线是600元，仪陇县中医院转仪陇县人民医院起付线只需要补200元差额即可。」
- Notes: China only: Referral rules are set separately by each province, city and medical group, so ask your community clinic before you go. For stable chronic illness, avoid going to a big hospital just for prescriptions, because routine outpatient prescribing there will be gradually cut back. A community clinic can prescribe up to 12 weeks of medicine at a time; see item 5 of section 16.

### 2. Within the same pooling area (your insurance area), the lower the level, the higher the reimbursement rate; the gap is about 10 percentage points
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part is resisting the urge to go to big hospitals.
- Plain language: Under the document, for the same inpatient bill, the lower the hospital level, the higher the reimbursement rate. Each area should widen the gap by about 10 percentage points per level. For example, on a 10,000 yuan bill, reimbursement drops from 80% to 70%, so what you pay goes from 2,000 yuan to 3,000 yuan. New medical insurance funds also tilt toward primary care.
- Benefit: The document asks local areas to widen the reimbursement gap between hospital levels, in principle by about 10 percentage points per level within a pooling area. On that gap, each step up costs you roughly 10% of the reimbursable bill in extra out-of-pocket payment. For example, on 10,000 yuan of reimbursable inpatient costs, reimbursement falling from 80% to 70% raises your own payment from 2,000 yuan to 3,000 yuan. The extra 1,000 yuan is 10% of the total bill, but it is half as much again as your original 2,000 yuan. Actual rates differ by area, and the deductible and cap also change the gap, so this is only an illustration based on the document's 10-point difference. New annual medical insurance funds also tilt toward primary care.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「因地制宜适当拉开参保人员在不同等级医疗卫生机构的住院报销水平，原则上统筹地区内医疗卫生机构住院报销比例逐级拉开10个百分点左右的差距。」「具备条件的地方，可按分级诊疗导向酌情拉开参保人员在不同等级医疗卫生机构的门诊报销水平。」
- Notes: China only: For the same inpatient bill, what you pay yourself at a community clinic and at a tertiary hospital can differ by several thousand yuan. Don't go up to a bigger hospital for something that can be done at primary level, but not every illness has to be seen at a community clinic.

### 3. To get into a big hospital, go through primary-care referral or the hospital's referral center; don't use ticket scalpers
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=no gain=mid scope=time -->
- Cost: Free. There is one extra referral step.
- Plain language: The lead hospital in a closely integrated medical group must keep some appointment slots and beds for people referred from primary care. Hospitals must also set up a referral center or name a fixed department to handle referrals, with full coverage by 2027. If you can't get an appointment, that doesn't mean there's no way in, so you don't need a ticket scalper.
- Benefit: The lead hospital in a closely integrated medical group must reserve a set share of appointment slots and beds for patients from primary care, and admit referred patients promptly. A closely integrated medical group is a set of hospitals and community health centers working together as one. So a referral from a community clinic gets its own slots and beds, not the same slots fought over by people in the hospital lobby. Medical institutions must set up a referral center or name a fixed department, with full coverage by 2027.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「牵头医院要为基层医疗卫生机构预留一定比例的号源和床位，并及时接诊经基层转诊患者。」「医疗机构要强化转诊服务统一管理，设立转诊中心或指定固定部门承担患者转诊服务工作，到2027年实现全覆盖。」
- Notes: China only: The reserved slots are kept for referred patients, so if you can't get a slot, a referral may still get you in. If you can't find the referral center, call the hospital's service line and ask.

### 4. Before going out of province for care, check locally first; in principle, a senior doctor (deputy chief physician or above) must assess the need
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: Free. Ask one more question before you leave.
- Plain language: Whether you should go out of area for care is, in principle, assessed by a deputy chief physician or higher at a secondary or tertiary hospital. Reimbursement for temporary out-of-area care can differ from what you would get at the same level of hospital at home, depending on local rules. Ask locally before you set off, rather than finding out after you arrive that it won't be reimbursed.
- Benefit: Whether a patient needs to seek care across pooling areas or across provinces is, in principle, assessed by a deputy chief physician or higher at a secondary or tertiary hospital. A pooling area is the area outside your insurance area. Whether a trip is necessary should, in principle, be stated by a doctor of sufficient seniority. Reimbursement for temporary out-of-province care is kept at a reasonable difference from what same-level institutions in the home insurance area pay. So the same illness treated elsewhere brings back somewhat less money than it would at home.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2026). 关于加快建设分级诊疗体系的若干措施. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>：「原则上由二、三级医院副主任医师及以上职称人员评估患者跨统筹地区、跨省异地就医的必要性。」「进一步强化异地就医直接结算管理服务，跨省临时外出就医人员报销水平与参保地同级别医疗机构报销水平保持合理差异。」
- Notes: China only: "Temporary out-of-area care" and "long-term residence elsewhere" are two different registrations, with different reimbursement levels. For how to register and which conditions can be settled directly, see section 16. Don't go to another area first and only then think about reimbursement.

### 5. After every visit, keep your own copies of your medical records, test reports and imaging
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: Free. Hospitals may charge a cost-only fee for photocopies, and the fee schedule must be public. The hard part is remembering to make a copy every time you visit.
- Plain language: The law gives you the right to read and copy your own medical records. You can copy outpatient records, inpatient notes, lab reports, imaging, surgery and anesthesia records, pathology reports and bills. Hospitals must assign a department or staff member to handle requests, and may only charge a cost-based fee. Changing hospitals, getting a second opinion, filing a commercial insurance claim, or dealing with a real dispute all depend on this stack of documents.
- Benefit: Regulations explicitly list reading and copying medical records as patient rights. The scope covers all records: outpatient records, inpatient notes, lab tests, medical imaging, surgery and anesthesia records, pathology, and medical costs. Changing hospitals, getting a second opinion, filing a commercial insurance claim, and dealing with a real dispute all depend on these records.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2018). 医疗纠纷预防和处理条例（国务院令第 701 号）第十六条：「患者有权查阅、复制其门诊病历、住院志、体温单、医嘱单、化验单（检验报告）、医学影像检查资料、特殊检查同意书、手术同意书、手术及麻醉记录、病理资料、护理记录、医疗费用以及国务院卫生主管部门规定的其他属于病历的全部资料。」「患者要求复制病历资料的，医疗机构应当提供复制服务，并在复制的病历资料上加盖证明印记……可以收取工本费，收费标准应当公开。」「患者死亡的，其近亲属可以依照本条例的规定，查阅、复制病历资料。」第十五条「任何单位和个人不得篡改、伪造、隐匿、毁灭或者抢夺病历资料。」<https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm>；国家卫生计生委、国家中医药管理局 (2013). 医疗机构病历管理规定（2013 年版）：医疗机构「应当指定部门或者专（兼）职人员负责受理复制病历资料的申请」，复制在申请人在场的情况下进行并加盖证明印记. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2014/content_2600084.htm>
- Notes: China only: Photocopies are handled by the department the hospital designates, so you don't need to ask your doctor. Use Article 16 of the Regulations on the Prevention and Handling of Medical Disputes as your checklist. First come outpatient records, inpatient notes, temperature charts, doctor's orders, lab reports and imaging. Then surgery and anesthesia records, pathology reports, nursing records and the itemized bill. Keep them in one folder in date order. The most important items are the discharge summary, surgery notes, pathology report and key imaging. For chronic illness, keep the results of each test in one notebook; see section 16.

### 6. If you have questions about treatment, ask on the spot to seal the medical records; both sides must be present, make a list, and each keeps a copy
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: Free. You ask the hospital to seal the records, then wait for both sides to be present to complete the paperwork. The hard part is saying this out loud to the doctor.
- Plain language: If you doubt your treatment, you can ask for the records to be sealed, with both you and the hospital present. You make a list, both sides sign or stamp it, and each keeps a copy. Seal the completed part first if the records are not finished. If you suspect an IV drip, transfusion, injection or medicine caused the problem, seal the bag, the medicine and the drip set together with the records.
- Benefit: The regulation provides that when sealing or unsealing medical records is needed because of a medical dispute, it must be done with both the doctor and the patient present. The sealed material can be originals or copies, kept by the medical institution. If records are not yet complete, the completed part is sealed first and the rest is sealed once finished. The institution must draw up a sealing list, signed or stamped by both sides, with each side keeping a copy. If an adverse outcome is suspected from an IV infusion, blood transfusion, injection or medication, both sides must jointly seal the physical items on site, such as the bag of fluid, the medicine and the drip set, and unseal them jointly. Any testing must be jointly commissioned from a qualified testing body. If both sides cannot agree, the county-level health authority where the institution is located designates one. If blood needs sealing because a transfusion may have caused harm, the institution must notify the blood center to send staff. A firm rule also applies: "No unit or individual may alter, forge, conceal, destroy or seize medical records." Once the dispute is resolved, or three years pass with no request to resolve it, the institution may unseal the records on its own. (Applies nationwide, in force from 1 October 2018.)
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2018). 医疗纠纷预防和处理条例（国务院令第 701 号，第十五、二十四、二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm>
- Notes: China only: Sealing and copying are different things. Copying is routine; see item 5 of this section (keep your own copies of records and imaging). Sealing fixes the evidence when a dispute arises, to prevent records being added to or changed later. If you suspect a problem with an IV drip, a transfusion or medicine, seal the bag, the medicine and the drip set along with the records, and don't let anyone take them away and discard them. Hospitals must publish the dispute resolution routes, procedures and contact details in a prominent place, and set up a single complaints office. Take issues there first, not to the doctor on duty.

### 7. For serious or critical illness or injury, go straight to the emergency pre-triage desk; don't queue at the registration window
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=time -->
- Cost: Free. Walk a few extra steps to find the pre-triage desk. State the most dangerous symptom in one sentence.
- Plain language: The emergency room does not work first come, first served. It sorts patients into four levels by how serious their condition is. The level is set at the pre-triage desk at the emergency entrance, not at the registration window. When you get there, state the most dangerous symptom first: heavy bleeding, shortness of breath, loss of consciousness, chest pain like a heart attack, or pain you can't bear.
- Benefit: The 2024 national quality indicators for emergency medicine require pre-triage to classify patients into four levels by how critically ill they are. Level I patients are in critical condition and need immediate treatment. Level II patients are seriously ill, and assessment and treatment often happen at the same time. Level III patients are acutely ill and need diagnosis and treatment within a short time. Level IV patients are non-acute or sub-acute, and are seen in order in the general treatment area. Pre-triage means a trained staff member first assesses how serious your condition is and sets the order in which you are seen. Beijing's implementing standard gives response requirements for each level. Level 1: assess and treat immediately, and move the patient to a resuscitation room or rescue room. Level 2: monitor vital signs immediately, treat within 10 minutes, and move the patient to a rescue room. Level 3: treat before Level 4 patients, with the patient waiting in the general treatment area; if the wait exceeds 30 minutes, reassess. Level 4: seen in order, and the wait may be long unless the condition changes; if the wait exceeds 4 hours, reassess. Beijing's manual criteria for Level 1 include acute impaired consciousness. Level 2 criteria include active or severe bleeding, severe difficulty breathing, chest pain resembling a cardiac cause, and severe pain from any cause (7 to 10 out of 10). Triage should uphold the principle of giving priority to critically ill patients. Emergency departments should set up a pre-triage area in a prominent place at the entrance. (The four levels are national; the response times come from the Beijing standard.)
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2024). 关于印发急诊医学等 6 个专业医疗质量控制指标（2024 年版）的通知（国卫办医政函〔2024〕150 号）附件 1，指标四「急诊分级分诊执行率」. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202405/content_6951260.htm>；北京市卫生健康委员会 (2019). 北京市加强急诊预检分诊分级工作方案及附件《北京市医院急诊预检分诊分级标准（试行）》：「坚持急危重症优先就诊原则。按照患者病情的严重程度，遵循从重到轻、从病情迅速变化到相对稳定的原则，合理安排患者就诊顺序。」「在急诊科入口的显著位置，设置急诊预检分诊区域」「院前医疗急救医生应按照相关标准对患者进行初步分级，对1、2级患者需及时通知所送医院急诊科做好接诊准备」. <http://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/zwgk_20040/ylws/201912/t20191216_1242338.html>
- Notes: China only: The four levels are national; how fast each is handled is set by each province and city. Beijing's version is the one that can be checked word by word. Triage looks at heart rate, blood pressure, blood oxygen and symptoms. Pain counts too, and a pain score of 7 to 10 can place you at Level 2. Level 1 hard criteria include these. First, heart rate above 180 or below 40 beats per minute. Second, systolic pressure below 70 mmHg; systolic is the top number in a blood pressure reading. Third, blood oxygen below 80%. Fourth, shock, a confirmed heart attack, or acute impaired consciousness. If you think triage rated you too low, state the most dangerous symptom clearly. If your condition changes while you wait, go straight back to the triage desk for a new assessment. If it is truly critical, call 120, China's ambulance number (see section 13). Beijing's plan requires pre-hospital emergency doctors to give an initial grade using the standard, and to notify the receiving emergency department early for Level 1 and 2 patients so it can prepare.

### 8. Without money, ID or a clear identity, emergency care still must come first
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: Free.
- Plain language: A state fund covers emergency costs for people who cannot be identified or cannot pay. Hospitals cannot refuse or delay emergency care, and ambulances cannot skip calls over cost. Do not stay home for lack of money, or fear unpaid bills when calling an ambulance. The fund covers only emergency care; later bills go through insurance and medical aid.
- Benefit: The national emergency aid system covers patients in China who have an acute, critical or life-threatening illness or injury, need emergency care, but whose identity is unclear or who cannot afford the costs. The fund pays two kinds of cost: "emergency costs for patients whose identity cannot be determined", and "emergency costs owed by patients whose identity is clear but who cannot pay". The document also says: "All levels and types of medical institutions and their staff must provide timely and effective emergency treatment to critically ill or injured patients, and must not refuse, pass on or delay treatment for any reason." For prehospital emergency care, the national emergency number is 120. Emergency centers (stations) "must not refuse, pass on or delay prehospital emergency care because of dispatch arrangements", and "must not refuse or delay prehospital emergency care because of cost". Transfers follow "the principles of nearest, most urgent, meeting professional needs, and taking patient wishes into account" (national, from 2013).
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国务院办公厅 (2013). 关于建立疾病应急救助制度的指导意见（国办发〔2013〕15 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2013-03/01/content_6069.htm>；国家卫生和计划生育委员会 (2014). 院前医疗急救管理办法（委令第 3 号，第十三、二十二、二十三、二十五条）. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2014/content_2580977.htm>
- Notes: China only: The fund pays only the emergency-care part of the bill. The hospital applies to the fund after treatment; you do not collect money at a window. If you have medical insurance, use it first as usual. Application procedures and limits differ by province. The transfer rule "nearest, most urgent" ranks ahead of "patient wishes", so an ambulance may not take you to your chosen hospital. In a critical case, the nearest facility that can treat you is the one that counts. Costs you still face afterwards that remain too heavy go to medical aid; see section 7, item 10 (medical aid).

### 9. A disability assessment should wait until treatment has ended or your condition has stabilized
<!-- tags: money=low time=mid will=some gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: Assessment fees range from several hundred to 2,000 yuan (RMB), usually advanced by the party who applies. The assessment must wait until treatment has ended or your clinical condition is stable. How long that takes depends on the injury, and the doctor decides. The hard part is resisting the urge to settle the matter while the injury has not healed.
- Plain language: The amount of compensation depends mainly on the disability grade, and the grade depends on how much functional impairment remains after treatment. The standard says the assessment must be done after treatment has ended or the clinical effect is stable. A bone that has not finished healing does not meet this rule. A common fracture that has healed and does not affect function usually does not qualify for a grade.
- Benefit: The assessment principle in the Classification of Degrees of Disability from Human Injury is that it "should be based on the outcome or result of the injury treatment, objectively assessing the degree of loss of tissue or organ and/or functional impairment". The standard says the timing "should be after the primary injury, and any complications closely linked to it, have finished treatment, or after clinical effect has stabilized". It "divides human injury disability into 10 grades, from grade 1 (100% disability rate) to grade 10 (10% disability rate), each grade differing by 10%". The basis is "the destruction of tissue or organ structure, functional impairment, and the degree of dependence on medical care and nursing, with appropriate consideration of social interaction and psychological effects of disability". Among grade 10 items related to fractures are these. First, "a compression fracture of one vertebral body (compression of 1/3) or a comminuted fracture". Second, "fractures at two or more pelvic sites, or comminuted fractures, with malunion". Third, "loss of function of any one major limb joint (except the ankle) of 25% or more". Disability of the same site and nature may not be counted twice. For disabilities at two or more sites, each must be graded separately (national, in force from 1 January 2017).
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 最高人民法院、最高人民检察院、公安部、国家安全部、司法部 (2016). 关于发布《人体损伤致残程度分级》的公告（2016 年 4 月 18 日发布，2017 年 1 月 1 日施行），标准第 4.1、4.2、4.3、4.4、4.5、5.10.6、6.2 条. <https://www.moj.gov.cn/pub/sfbgw/zwxxgk/fdzdgknr/fdzdgknrtzwj/201908/P020210316701681025917.pdf>
- Notes: China only: Three separate disability assessment systems are not interchangeable, so keep them apart. Injuries from assault, traffic accidents and other personal injury are assessed under the judicial Classification of Degrees of Disability from Human Injury, which sets disability compensation (see section 9). Work injuries go through a labor capacity assessment, which uses a different set of ten disability grades and governs work injury benefits (see section 19). If you want disabled-person policy benefits, you need a separate Disabled Persons' Card (see item 10). Before assessment, gather your medical records, surgery notes and follow-up imaging (see item 5, keep your own copies of records and imaging). The standard does not say whether an early assessment gives a grade that is too low or too high. This follows from the principle that the assessment is based on the treatment outcome. An early assessment records how you are at that time: functions not yet recovered may improve later, or worsen. When this injury is mixed with a pre-existing old condition, the assessment opinion must also state how much of the disability outcome this injury accounts for. This entry directly affects how much compensation you receive.

### 10. If lasting impairment remains after treatment, apply for a Disabled Persons' Card at your county disability federation
<!-- tags: money=low time=mid will=some gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: The card itself has no processing fee. Assessment and photo fees are normally paid by you, and people in hardship can apply for a reduction. Bring your ID, household register booklet and three 2-inch white-background photos to the county-level China Disabled Persons' Federation (CDPF) where your household is registered. After a qualifying assessment, the result is posted publicly in your village or community for 5 working days, and the federation completes its review within 10 working days. The cost is mainly the trips and the waiting.
- Plain language: The card is the gateway to disability benefits, not proof of injury. It covers seven types: vision, hearing, speech, physical, intellectual, mental and multiple disabilities, with grades one to four. The steps are application, acceptance, assessment at a designated institution, public notice, approval and issue. The card is valid for ten years and is free to renew when it expires. If you disagree with the assessment, you can ask for a reassessment.
- Benefit: The card is "a legal certificate identifying a disabled person and their disability type and grade, and an important basis for enjoying the preferential policies of the state and local governments under the law". The assessment standard is the national standard Classification of Disabled Persons and Disability Grading (GB/T 26341-2010). Applications follow the principles of voluntary application and local jurisdiction. People with any of the seven types of disability (vision, hearing, speech, physical, intellectual, mental and multiple) may apply, with grades one to four. The county-level federation handles acceptance and issue, based on the assessment conclusion of a hospital or specialist institution designated by the province. If the assessment meets the standard, it is posted publicly for five working days in the village (community), and the county-level federation "completes its review within ten working days". "No fee is charged for the Disabled Persons' Card." Assessment and photo fees "are in principle paid by the applicant personally; where conditions allow, local finance may subsidize them, and applicants in special hardship should be coordinated with relevant departments for exemption or reduction". The card is valid for ten years and is renewed free at expiry. If the disability type or grade changes, the holder may apply for reassessment. If the holder disagrees with the assessment conclusion, they may apply to the prefecture-level federation for reassessment within ten working days. If the dispute continues, the provincial disability assessment expert committee makes the final decision (national, in force from 1 January 2018).
