# 27. Pregnancy and childbirth: from finding out you are pregnant to getting the paperwork at discharge

# Pregnancy and childbirth: from finding out you are pregnant to getting the paperwork at discharge

The first half of this section works out the risk of death for the pregnant woman and the fetus. The second half works out the money and the time spent running around. The two ledgers are not converted into each other. The content only covers what to do and when. Things like prenatal nutrition and fetal education, which have no hard evidence or only very small effects, are not covered here. Maternity benefits, maternity leave and the money side of raising a child are in section 18. How to look after a baby after birth is in section 20. Emergencies are in section 13.

The World Health Organization figures: in 2023, about 260,000 women worldwide died from pregnancy and childbirth. About 75% of those deaths came from five causes: severe bleeding after birth, infection, high blood pressure disorders of pregnancy (a group of conditions in which blood pressure rises during pregnancy, including preeclampsia and eclampsia), complications during labor, and unsafe abortion. The WHO also says that "most maternal deaths are preventable, because the medical means to prevent and manage complications are known." The most effective items in this section are the ones that stop these problems early.

The entries are grouped by theme below. The numbers in brackets are item numbers.

**Things you can do yourself before and during pregnancy**: start folic acid before you conceive (item 1), no smoking and no drinking (item 4), don't rehome your cat (item 17), inactivated flu vaccine (item 18), sleep on your side after 28 weeks (item 19).

**Prenatal checks and screening**: set up your Maternal and Child Health Handbook (item 2), HIV, syphilis and hepatitis B tests (item 3), low-dose aspirin (item 5), gestational diabetes screening (item 6).

**If something goes wrong, go to the hospital right away**: the "go to the hospital now" list (item 7), and if your water breaks, lie down where you are (item 8).

**During labor**: if you want a painless delivery, just ask for it (item 9). Don't ask for a cesarean without a medical reason (item 10).

**After birth**: the 42-day postpartum checkup (item 16), and breastfeeding is not contraception (item 20).

**Money and documents**: confirm your maternity insurance (item 11), the birth medical certificate (item 12), heel-prick blood screening and hearing screening (item 13), enroll the baby in resident medical insurance (item 14), and register the birth (item 15).

### 1. Start taking 0.4 mg of folic acid every day before you conceive, and keep it up until the first three months of pregnancy are over
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=health who=pregnancy -->
- Cost: A few dozen yuan a bottle. Rural women pay nothing, because the national free folic acid supplement program distributes it. One tablet a day. The hard part is remembering it every day.
- Plain language: Taking one 0.4 mg folic acid tablet a day before and early in pregnancy can cut a baby's risk of neural tube defects, such as spina bifida, by about seven in ten. For a woman who has already had a baby with one, the risk of another affected baby falls to about a third of what it was.
- Benefit: A Cochrane systematic review pooled 5 trials covering 6,708 births and rated the quality of the evidence as high. Taking folic acid every day around conception lowers the risk of neural tube defects in the fetus, RR 0.31 (95% CI 0.17 to 0.58, the credible range), a reduction of about 70%. For women who have already had a baby with a neural tube defect, the risk of another affected baby is RR 0.34 (95% CI 0.18 to 0.64), about a third of the original risk. Across population subgroups, 0.4 mg and higher doses showed no difference in effect. Taking folic acid alone and taking a multivitamin showed no difference in effect either. The 2010 Ministry of Health management plan for the folic acid supplement program states that free folic acid tablets of 0.4 mg are given to women planning pregnancy in rural areas, to be "taken from three months before pregnancy through the first three months of pregnancy." The US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention says neural tube defects develop in the first weeks of pregnancy, often before you know you are pregnant.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: De-Regil LM, Peña-Rosas JP, Fernández-Gaxiola AC, Rayco-Solon P (2015). Effects and safety of periconceptional oral folate supplementation for preventing birth defects. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (12), CD007950. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD007950.pub3>；卫生部办公厅 (2010). 2010 年增补叶酸预防神经管缺陷项目管理方案. <https://www.nhc.gov.cn/wjw/gfxwj/201006/22a0b4d4651e4b6d896453d443d9f79d/files/1733384846754_72260.doc>；Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. About Folic Acid. <https://www.cdc.gov/folic-acid/about/index.html>
- Notes: The key is starting before you get pregnant. Neural tube defects form in the first weeks of pregnancy, so starting only after a positive test may be too late. If you are planning a baby, start three months ahead, following the approach of the national program. In the same pooled review, folic acid had no clear effect on cleft lip and palate, congenital heart defects or miscarriage, so don't expect it to treat those. Pregnant women on a fully plant-based diet also need daily vitamin B12; see item 14 of section 20 (if you eat no animal products, take vitamin B12 every day). If you are still deciding when to have a baby, the age trade-off is in item 7 of section 18 (having a baby a few years later).

### 2. Before 13 weeks of pregnancy, go to your community health center to set up a Maternal and Child Health Handbook, and use up your free prenatal checks
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=no gain=mid scope=health who=pregnancy -->
- Cost: Free. This is part of the national basic public health service program. You will need to make several trips, timed by weeks of pregnancy.
- Plain language: The state pays for at least five prenatal checkups, plus a home visit after birth and a checkup at 42 days postpartum. To claim them, set up a Maternal and Child Health Handbook at a community health center before 13 weeks of pregnancy. If you don't, you will miss these free checks.
- Benefit: The National Basic Public Health Service Standards (third edition) include maternal health management services. Resident pregnant women in the area can use these: one health check in early pregnancy, two in mid-pregnancy, two in late pregnancy, one home visit after birth, and one health check at 42 days postpartum. Register before 13 weeks, and have the first prenatal checkup at the same time. The two mid-pregnancy visits fall at 16 to 20 weeks and 21 to 24 weeks. The two late-pregnancy visits fall at 28 to 36 weeks and 37 to 40 weeks. Local published service lists also include a full first set of lab tests and a postpartum depression screening.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国家卫生计生委 (2017). 国家基本公共卫生服务规范（第三版）. <https://www.nhc.gov.cn/ewebeditor/uploadfile/2017/04/20170417104506514.pdf>；靖江市人民政府. 国家基本公共卫生服务项目——孕产妇健康管理服务（地方服务清单公开）. <https://www.jingjiang.gov.cn/xxgk/zdlyxxgk/wsjk/art/2023/art_383017a2e7794892bc772bfbe042f379.html>
- Notes: China only: These five visits are the free minimum, not all the prenatal care you will get. The hospital may add ultrasounds, blood sugar tests and fetal heart monitoring, which you pay for yourself. The earlier you register, the earlier the pregnancy risk grading can be done. Pregnancy risk grading sorts pregnant women into risk levels. If you are classed as high risk, you can be referred early to a hospital that can handle it. If you live outside your household registration (hukou) area, register where you currently live. You do not need to go back to your registered area.

### 3. Get tested for HIV, syphilis and hepatitis B at your first prenatal checkup. If HIV is found, free treatment to block transmission is available.
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=high scope=health who=pregnancy -->
- Cost: HIV counseling and initial screening are free under the regulations. Syphilis and hepatitis B tests are not listed as free in national documents, so ask your hospital whether it charges.
- Plain language: Get tested for HIV, syphilis and hepatitis B at your first prenatal checkup. A positive result is not the end. HIV treatment and counseling are free. Syphilis is treated with penicillin, started early. For hepatitis B, the baby gets a vaccine and immunoglobulin at birth, which stops the virus passing from mother to child.
- Benefit: Mother-to-child transmission means the disease passes from mother to child. The Standards for Preventing Mother-to-Child Transmission of HIV, Syphilis and Hepatitis B (2020 edition) set out requirements. At the first prenatal checkup, the hospital must inform you of the relevant information and "provide appropriate and standardized testing, and provide follow-up counseling based on the results." If testing is not finished before delivery, the woman is generally treated as infected. The standards require that pregnant women with syphilis "receive standardized penicillin treatment as early as possible," and that the baby receive preventive penicillin right after birth. For pregnant women who test positive for hepatitis B surface antigen, those who qualify receive antiviral treatment after giving informed consent. A positive result is not the end. Article 23 of the AIDS Prevention and Control Regulations provides that people who voluntarily seek HIV counseling and testing are "provided with counseling and initial screening free of charge." Article 44 provides that pregnant women infected with HIV are "provided with free treatment and counseling to prevent mother-to-child transmission of AIDS." Article 43 requires services including blocking transmission, treatment, postpartum visits, infant follow-up and testing. If the mother is hepatitis B surface antigen positive, the newborn receives the hepatitis B vaccine and hepatitis B immunoglobulin at the same time, at birth (see item 2 of section 20).
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国家卫生健康委办公厅 (2020). 预防艾滋病、梅毒和乙肝母婴传播工作规范（2020 年版）. <https://wjw.xinjiang.gov.cn/hfpc/zhgl6/202012/08b7de496f0b40e586d4b99d00cf79c4.shtml>（新疆维吾尔自治区卫生健康委员会转载）；国务院 (2006). 艾滋病防治条例（第二十三、四十三、四十四条）. <https://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/zwgk_20040/zcwj2022/flfg/202304/t20230408_2992986.html>（北京市卫生健康委员会转载）
- Notes: China only: The earlier these three tests are done, the more useful they are, because blocking and treatment take time. Article 3 of the AIDS Prevention and Control Regulations also states that "no unit or individual may discriminate against people infected with HIV, AIDS patients or their families." Marriage, employment, medical care and schooling are protected by law. For routine prevention and testing, see section 1. For post-exposure blocking after a high-risk event, see section 13.

### 4. Don't smoke or drink a drop for the whole pregnancy, and don't let anyone smoke indoors around you
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=high gain=high scope=health who=pregnancy -->
- Cost: Free, and you save the money you would have spent on cigarettes and alcohol. The hard part is getting the people you live with to quit with you.
- Plain language: Smoking during pregnancy raises the chances of preterm birth, babies born too small, and cleft lip and palate. It doubles the risk of abnormal bleeding during pregnancy and delivery. A baby whose mother smoked during pregnancy, or who breathes smoke after birth, has a higher risk of sudden infant death. It is never too late to quit.
- Benefit: The US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention says that smoking in pregnancy can lead to a baby being "small even when born at full term" and can "cause preterm birth." It "doubles the risk of abnormal bleeding during pregnancy and childbirth." It raises the risk of birth defects such as cleft lip and cleft palate. It "damages the developing lungs and brain of the fetus, with effects that can last into childhood." Babies born to mothers who smoked during pregnancy, and babies exposed to smoke after birth, have a higher risk of sudden infant death syndrome. The official advice is: "It is best to quit before pregnancy, but if you are already pregnant, quitting still helps," and "it is never too late to quit." On alcohol, the CDC says there is "no known safe amount of alcohol" during pregnancy, and no period of pregnancy in which drinking is known to be safe. The baby's brain develops throughout pregnancy, so exposure at any point may affect it.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Smoking During Pregnancy. <https://www.cdc.gov/tobacco/campaign/tips/diseases/pregnancy.html>；Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. About Alcohol Use During Pregnancy. <https://www.cdc.gov/alcohol-pregnancy/about/index.html>
- Notes: Smoke that a pregnant woman breathes in from someone else smoking indoors counts for the fetus too, so family members should not smoke indoors either. The same goes for alcohol: no amount of drinking is known to be safe, so the simplest approach in pregnancy is not to drink at all. For the adult's own cost-benefit of smoking and drinking, see section 2.

### 5. If you have risk factors for preeclampsia, take one low-dose aspirin a day as prescribed, starting after 12 weeks of pregnancy
<!-- tags: money=low time=low will=some gain=mid scope=health who=pregnancy -->
- Cost: A few dozen yuan. Take it every day as prescribed, until delivery. The hard part is not forgetting.
- Plain language: Preeclampsia is a high blood pressure disorder of pregnancy. For people at high risk, taking low-dose aspirin every day from 12 weeks, as prescribed, lowers the risk of preeclampsia by about 15% and the risk of preterm birth by about 20%. Others should not take it on their own.
- Benefit: The US Preventive Services Task Force gives a B recommendation: for people at high risk of preeclampsia, start low-dose aspirin (81 mg a day) after 12 weeks of pregnancy to prevent it. Preeclampsia is a high blood pressure disorder of pregnancy. Pooling several studies gives the following results; the 95% CI is the credible range. Preeclampsia RR 0.85 (95% CI 0.75 to 0.95, 16 studies), about 15% lower. Preterm birth RR 0.80 (95% CI 0.67 to 0.95, 13 studies), about 20% lower. Perinatal death RR 0.79 (95% CI 0.66 to 0.96, 11 studies), about 21% lower; perinatal death means death in the period around delivery. Small for gestational age, or fetal growth restriction, RR 0.82 (95% CI 0.68 to 0.99, 16 studies), about 18% lower; this means the fetus is smaller than expected for its gestational age.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Aspirin Use to Prevent Preeclampsia and Related Morbidity and Mortality: Preventive Medication. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/low-dose-aspirin-use-for-the-prevention-of-morbidity-and-mortality-from-preeclampsia-preventive-medication>
- Notes: The high-risk factors are: having had preeclampsia in a previous pregnancy, carrying twins or more, and chronic high blood pressure. Also type 1 or type 2 diabetes before pregnancy, kidney disease, and autoimmune diseases such as systemic lupus erythematosus and antiphospholipid syndrome. If you are not in these groups, do not take it on your own. This medicine requires a prescription, so give your full medical history at your prenatal checkups and let the doctor decide. In China, most pharmacies sell 100 mg enteric-coated tablets; take them as prescribed.

### 6. From 24 weeks of pregnancy onward, get one gestational diabetes screening. Don't skip it because the sugar drink is a hassle.
<!-- tags: money=low time=low will=no gain=mid scope=health who=pregnancy -->
- Cost: A few dozen to over a hundred yuan. You go on an empty stomach and have blood drawn several times, which takes up half a morning.
- Plain language: Gestational diabetes is high blood sugar that first appears during pregnancy. After 24 weeks, spend half a morning drinking one sugar drink and having blood drawn a few times, and it can be detected. If it goes untreated, the risks rise for a very large baby, a difficult labor, a cesarean delivery, preeclampsia and low blood sugar in the newborn.
- Benefit: The US Preventive Services Task Force gives a B recommendation: for pregnant women with no symptoms (they do not feel anything wrong), screen for gestational diabetes at 24 weeks or later. Gestational diabetes is high blood sugar that first appears during pregnancy. For women who are screened only once, this one test should be at 24 weeks or later. The Task Force's conclusion on screening before 24 weeks is that current evidence is insufficient to judge the benefits and harms (I statement). Untreated gestational diabetes raises the risk of the following: macrosomia (a baby that grows too large), difficult delivery, cesarean delivery, preeclampsia and neonatal hypoglycemia (low blood sugar in the newborn).
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Gestational Diabetes: Screening. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/gestational-diabetes-screening>；Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Gestational Diabetes. <https://www.cdc.gov/diabetes/about/gestational-diabetes.html>
- Notes: According to the US Preventive Services Task Force, if the result is positive, the usual first steps are exercise, dietary changes and blood sugar monitoring. Insulin or oral medication is used only if blood sugar cannot be controlled. Don't skip the test because you fear needing injections. Blood sugar should be rechecked after birth: the CDC says about half of people who had gestational diabetes later develop type 2 diabetes.

### 7. Memorize this "go to the hospital now" list. It applies during pregnancy and for one year after birth.
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=high scope=health who=pregnancy -->
- Cost: Free. It takes five minutes to memorize, and ideally the people you live with should know it too.
- Plain language: If any of these signs appear, go to the hospital that same day, not the next morning. The list still applies for a year after giving birth. Signs include a headache that won't go away, vision changes, a fever of 38 °C or higher, and severe swelling of the hands or face. Go at once for trouble breathing, chest pain, a racing heart, abdominal pain that won't ease, or reduced fetal movement.
- Benefit: The US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention lists these urgent warning signs in pregnancy and after birth: a headache that won't go away or keeps getting worse, dizziness or fainting, vision changes, and a body temperature of 38 °C or higher. Severe swelling of the hands or face, thoughts of harming yourself or your baby, trouble breathing, chest pain, or a racing heart. Severe nausea and vomiting, abdominal pain that does not go away, and fetal movement that stops or clearly decreases during pregnancy. Vaginal bleeding or leaking fluid during pregnancy, or abnormal vaginal bleeding or discharge after birth. Severe swelling, redness or pain in one arm or one leg. These problems can occur within a year after birth, so the idea that you are fine once the postpartum month is over does not hold.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Hear Her — Urgent Maternal Warning Signs. <https://www.cdc.gov/hearher/maternal-warning-signs/index.html>；World Health Organization. Maternal mortality fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/maternal-mortality>
- Notes: The purpose of this list is to tell "wait a bit" apart from "leave right now." A headache with blurred vision and upper abdominal pain points to preeclampsia (a high blood pressure disorder of pregnancy). A swollen, painful calf points to deep vein thrombosis (see section 13). According to the WHO, severe bleeding after birth can take a healthy woman's life within hours. If any sign appears, go straight to the hospital and do not wait at home until the next day.

### 8. If your water breaks, lie down where you are, raise your hips, and call 120. Don't walk around or shower.
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=high scope=health who=pregnancy -->
- Cost: Free.
- Plain language: Your water breaking is not a reason to slowly gather your things and set off. Lie down where you are, put a pillow under your hips, and call 120 (China's ambulance number). Standing and walking make the fluid leak faster, and the umbilical cord can slip out with it. This is an emergency that can cost the baby's life.
- Benefit: The Beijing Municipal Health Commission's guidance says that once the water breaks, you should "lie down right away somewhere to keep the fluid from leaking out," "not move around casually," and "call the 120 emergency ambulance number immediately." While waiting for the ambulance, tell family to bring things and meet you at the hospital. Medical health-education material explains why: when you are standing, fluid is lost faster, and the baby's umbilical cord may slip out with it. This is called cord prolapse, an emergency that endangers the fetus. So lie down and raise your hips. Lying on your left side is an option.
- Evidence level: B
- Sources: 北京市卫生健康委员会. 每周急救话题：准妈妈羊水早破怎样办？ <https://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/bmfw_20143/jkzs/jzjj/202001/t20200115_1575632.html>；北京市顺义区妇幼保健院. 孕晚期在家破水怎么办？ <https://www.bch-syfy.cn/Html/News/Articles/9453.html>
- Notes: China only: 120 is China's national ambulance number. The three signs of imminent labor are regular contractions, a bloody show, and water breaking. Water breaking is the one where you "lie down on the spot and call for a car." This is the author's own summary based on the two sources above. The Shunyi District Maternal and Child Health Hospital's guidance says that standing, walking, sitting and squatting all make fluid leak faster and raise the risk of cord prolapse. So after your water breaks, don't shower, don't squat on the toilet, and don't drive yourself. Note the time the water broke and the color of the fluid, and tell the doctor. Light green, yellow-green or brownish-yellow fluid suggests meconium (the baby's first stool) may be mixed in. If you feel a cord-like thing hanging down in the vagina, it may be cord prolapse: call 120 immediately and lie down with your hips raised. Fetal movement that is clearly different from usual should also be reported to the doctor.

### 9. If you want a painless delivery, just say so. It does not raise the risk of a cesarean.
<!-- tags: money=low time=low will=some gain=mid scope=time who=pregnancy -->
- Cost: A few hundred to over a thousand yuan, depending on the area. Ask the hospital and its anesthesia department in advance whether they offer it. The hard part is that you have to bring it up yourself.
- Plain language: Painless delivery means a numbing injection in the lower back that dulls the pain of labor. It works better than other pain relief options, and it does not make a cesarean more likely. The idea that it causes back pain for life has no evidence behind it. Since 2024, it has its own billing item, so hospitals have more reason to offer it.
- Benefit: The Cochrane systematic review concludes that epidural analgesia is more effective than non-epidural analgesia or no analgesia at reducing labor pain and at increasing mothers' satisfaction with pain relief. Painless delivery uses epidural analgesia. The same review states that "epidural analgesia has no effect on cesarean risk or long-term back pain." The increase in instrumental delivery seen in earlier studies is no longer seen in studies after 2005. From 2024, the National Healthcare Security Administration's guidance on obstetric service price items lists "labor analgesia," "doula-assisted delivery" and "family companion during labor" as separate items. The stated reason is that, without separate price items, hospitals had less incentive to provide labor analgesia.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: Anim-Somuah M, Smyth RMD, Cyna AM, Cuthbert A (2018). Epidural versus non-epidural or no analgesia for pain management in labour. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (5), CD000331. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD000331.pub4>；国家医疗保障局 (2024). 重构产科服务价格项目 助力构建生育友好型社会. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202406/content_6957553.htm>
- Notes: The two most widespread claims, "a painless delivery will leave you with back pain for life" and "a painless delivery leads to a difficult labor and surgery," have no evidence behind them. Cochrane pooled epidural analgesia with other pain relief approaches and found no effect on cesarean risk or long-term back pain. The real side effects include low blood pressure, fever, weak legs, difficulty urinating, longer first and second stages of labor, and a higher chance of needing oxytocin. You decide whether to have it, but don't give it up because of rumors. Whether it is available also depends on whether the hospital has an anesthesiologist on site 24 hours a day, so ask during your prenatal checkups.

### 10. Don't ask for a cesarean without a medical reason, and don't schedule surgery to pick an auspicious date
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=mid scope=health who=pregnancy -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part is holding firm against family pressure.
- Plain language: The World Health Organization says that once a region's cesarean rate goes above 10%, there is no evidence that deaths among mothers and newborns keep falling. A cesarean that is medically needed must be done. Having surgery to pick a date, or to avoid pain, means taking on an extra major abdominal operation.
- Benefit: In its 2015 statement, the WHO said that when the cesarean rate in a population rises to about 10%, maternal and newborn deaths decline. Once the rate exceeds 10%, there is no evidence that mortality improves any further. The WHO also stresses that every effort should be made to provide cesareans to mothers who need them, rather than aiming for a particular rate.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: World Health Organization (2015). WHO Statement on Caesarean Section Rates (WHO/RHR/15.02). <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-RHR-15.02>；Keag OE, Norman JE, Stock SJ (2018). Long-term risks and benefits associated with cesarean delivery for mother, baby, and subsequent pregnancies: Systematic review and meta-analysis. PLoS Medicine, 15(1), e1002494. <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1002494>
- Notes: When a cesarean is medically needed, it is a life-saving operation. Have it, and don't tough it out. What to avoid is surgery that is not medically needed, such as to avoid pain, to pick an auspicious time, or to fit around when a child starts school. A cesarean is major abdominal surgery. A pooled analysis comparing cesarean with vaginal birth found that in pregnancies after a cesarean, placenta previa had an OR of 1.74, about 70% higher. Placenta accreta had an OR of 2.95, about three times higher. Placental abruption had an OR of 1.38, and stillbirth an OR of 1.27. This directly affects whether you want a second child. In the other direction, women who had cesareans had fewer cases of urinary leakage and pelvic organ prolapse later on. These findings come from studies that followed people over time, and the authors say they cannot conclude that the cesarean caused them.

### 11. Check your maternity insurance status before pregnancy; a spouse who does not work can also claim childbirth medical costs
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=money who=pregnancy -->
- Cost: Free. Make one call to the social insurance or medical insurance enquiry line.
- Plain language: If your employer pays maternity insurance, the childbirth medical bills and your maternity-leave allowance come from that fund. If your spouse does not work, the childbirth medical costs can still be claimed. Ask how many months of contributions you need, and whether you must register first if you give birth away from home.
- Benefit: Article 54 of the Social Insurance Law: where the employer has paid maternity insurance premiums, its employees enjoy maternity insurance benefits. The non-working spouse of an employee enjoys childbirth medical expense benefits under national rules, and the money comes from the maternity insurance fund. So if your employer has paid in, you get the benefits, and a non-working spouse's childbirth medical costs can also be paid from that fund. Maternity benefits have two parts. One is childbirth medical costs, the medical costs of the birth itself. The other is the maternity allowance, the money paid to you during maternity leave. Article 56 lists three cases in which you can receive the allowance: a female employee on maternity leave, leave for a family-planning procedure, and other cases set by laws and regulations. The allowance is "calculated on the average monthly wage of employees at your employer in the previous year." That means your employer's average monthly wage for employees last year, not your own pay.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 全国人大常委会 (2010). 中华人民共和国社会保险法（第五十四、五十六条）. <https://guangdong.chinatax.gov.cn/gdsw/qysw_gkwj/2020-02/12/content_8e8c485d0db34b989531f3ba57cec5a9.shtml>（国家税务总局广东省税务局转载）
- Notes: China only: Ask your local medical insurance office two things in advance. First, whether you have paid in for enough consecutive months, and whether there is a minimum. Second, if you will give birth outside your insured area, whether you must first file an out-of-area childbirth registration, and how costs are claimed if you do not. These rules differ between places, and they have not been checked place by place here. Asking ahead is the author's own advice. For how maternity leave days and the allowance are calculated, see section 18, item 2.

### 12. Get the Birth Medical Certificate before you leave hospital; decide the name in advance and check every character
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=time who=pregnancy,infant -->
- Cost: Free. Collect it while you do the discharge paperwork.
- Plain language: This certificate is needed before you can register the hukou, enrol in insurance or start school. If the baby was born at a licensed birth facility, collect it there. If not, you must first get a paternity test before you can apply. Check that every character in the name can be typed into the household registration system.
- Benefit: Article 23 of the Maternal and Infant Health Law: medical institutions, and people who deliver babies at home, must issue the uniform newborn Birth Medical Certificate under the rules of the State Council health authority. This certificate is the precondition for everything that follows. Hukou registration, insurance, a medical insurance card, and kindergarten and school enrolment all require it. The 2015 General Office of the State Council opinion on registering people without hukou states: if the baby was born in a birth facility, apply for the certificate from that facility. If the baby was born outside one, first get a paternity test certificate from a qualified testing institution, then apply to the institution entrusted by the county-level health authority.
- Evidence level: A

### 17. You do not need to rehome the cat when pregnant: have someone else change the litter, cook meat thoroughly, and wear gloves when touching soil
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=mid scope=health who=pregnancy -->
- Cost: Free. Have a family member scoop the litter every day. If you must do it yourself, buy a box of disposable gloves. The hard part is that meat must be fully cooked, so rare steak and lamb are off the menu.
- Plain language: Toxoplasma is a parasite. If you first catch it while pregnant, it can cause miscarriage, and the baby's eyes and brain can be harmed. Keeping a cat does not make infection more likely, so the cat does not need to go. The bigger risks are undercooked meat and touching soil without washing your hands. Let someone else scoop the litter each day.
- Benefit: The US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) says that if you first catch toxoplasmosis shortly before or during pregnancy, it can pass to the fetus. Most infected babies have no symptoms at birth but may later go blind or have intellectual disability. A small number have serious eye or brain damage at birth. The UK NHS says infection during pregnancy can cause miscarriage. A toxoplasmosis centre in Lyon, France, tracked 603 pregnant women diagnosed with infection during pregnancy; most took anti-parasitic drugs. The overall rate of transmission to the fetus was 29% (95% CI 25 to 33; credible interval). About 6% of infections at 13 weeks were passed to the fetus, and about 72% of infections at 36 weeks. The earlier the infection, the more likely the baby is to show symptoms. For infections at 24 to 30 weeks, the risk of the baby showing symptoms early is highest, at about 10%. A European study compared pregnant women in six cities who had a new infection during pregnancy with those who did not. Owning a cat: OR 1.0 (95% CI 0.7 to 1.5), no difference. Touching soil: OR 1.81 (95% CI 1.2 to 2.7), about 80% higher. In each city, 30% to 63% of infections could be traced to undercooked or cured meat, and 6% to 17% to soil. Change the litter daily, because Toxoplasma in cat faeces takes 1 to 5 days to become infectious to people. During pregnancy, do not get a new cat, and do not adopt or handle stray cats, especially kittens. Keep the cat indoors, and feed it only cat food or cooked food, never raw meat. A China CDC public science article also says the litter tray should preferably be cleaned by the expectant father, and that with protection during pregnancy you can keep the cat. The NHS states that touching or owning a cat does not give you toxoplasmosis.

### 18. In flu season during pregnancy, get an inactivated flu shot at any stage of pregnancy; avoid the nasal spray
<!-- tags: money=low time=low will=no gain=high scope=health who=pregnancy -->
- Cost: Self-paid, tens to over a hundred yuan (RMB) per shot. Go to a community vaccination clinic. The hard part is choosing a product whose leaflet does not list pregnant women as a contraindication.
- Plain language: Pregnant women who catch flu are more likely to be hospitalised than women who are not pregnant. A flu shot during pregnancy cuts your own flu hospital admissions by about 40%. In the baby's first 6 months, confirmed flu falls by about half, and flu hospital admissions fall by about 72%. No rise in miscarriage, stillbirth or birth defects has been seen from the shot.
- Benefit: The WHO's 2022 position paper summarised existing studies. Pregnant women with flu have about 7 times the risk of hospitalisation compared with women who are not pregnant (OR 6.8, 95% CI 6–7.7; credible interval). A trial in Bangladesh randomly assigned 340 pregnant women to two groups. One group had an inactivated flu vaccine and the other, as a control, a pneumococcal vaccine; they were followed until 24 weeks after the baby's birth. Among babies whose mothers had the flu vaccine there were 6 confirmed flu cases, against 16 in the control group; vaccine efficacy was 63% (95% CI 5–85). Maternal fever plus respiratory illness was 36% lower (95% CI 4–57). Two trials in South Africa used a placebo, and neither the doctors nor the pregnant women knew which shot was given. Among HIV-negative pregnant women, confirmed flu fell from 3.6% to 1.8%, efficacy 50.4% (95% CI 14.5–71.2). For their babies, the rate fell from 3.6% to 1.9%, efficacy 48.8% (95% CI 11.6–70.4). Pooling 4 trials and 3 observational studies, babies had about 48% lower risk of confirmed flu within 6 months (95% CI 33–59). Four observational studies looking at babies hospitalised for confirmed flu found about 72% lower risk (95% CI 39–87). Another review that pooled only the two trials found 34% lower confirmed flu (95% CI 15–50). But there was no difference in babies' "flu-like illness", meaning illness that looks like flu but was not tested. For the mothers' own hospitalisation, data from four countries from 2010 to 2016 found that vaccinated women had about 40% lower risk of hospitalisation for confirmed flu (95% CI 12–59). China CDC's 2026–2027 guide lists pregnant women as a priority group. It says vaccination at any stage of pregnancy protects infants under 6 months, and that it works better in late pregnancy. ### 19. After 28 weeks of pregnancy, sleep on your side, left or right; avoid lying flat on your back
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=health who=pregnancy -->

### 20. Use contraception once sex resumes after birth, since breastfeeding is not contraception; if you want another baby, wait at least 18 months
<!-- tags: money=low time=low will=some gain=mid scope=health who=pregnancy -->
- Cost: A box of condoms costs tens of yuan (RMB). IUDs and implants require a hospital visit, and prices vary by area. The hard part is that postpartum life is chaotic, so this is easy to put off.
- Plain language: You can get pregnant again as early as 3 weeks after birth, even while breastfeeding and without a period. Breastfeeding prevents pregnancy only if three things all apply: you feed only breast milk, you have no period, and the baby is under 6 months. If you conceive less than six months after a birth, the next baby's risk of preterm birth is about 40% higher. The WHO advises waiting at least two years between births.
- Benefit: The UK NHS says you can get pregnant again as early as 3 weeks after birth, even while breastfeeding and without a period. Unless you want to conceive straight away, use contraception every time you have sex. Using breastfeeding as contraception is called the lactational amenorrhoea method (LAM), and it needs three conditions at once: only breast milk, no period, and the baby under 6 months. Done strictly, it prevents more than 99% of pregnancies. If the rules are not kept that strictly, about 2 in 100 women become pregnant within 6 months. Switch to another method if any of these happen: the baby is over 6 months, you start formula, solid food or a dummy (pacifier), you get a period (even light bleeding counts), you stop feeding at night, or you feed less often. A Canadian study looked at more than 148,000 pregnancies and compared 6-month and 18-month intervals. For mothers aged 35 and over, death or serious illness occurred in 0.62% against 0.26%, aRR 2.39 (2.03–2.80), about 2.4 times. For mothers aged 20 to 34 there was no difference (0.92, 0.83–1.02). - Notes: Disputed. Opinions differ on how long is long enough. Among WHO experts, one camp favoured 18 months and another 27 months, and 24 months was the compromise. The NHS says at least 12 months. An Australian study compared 40,441 mothers' own two intervals with each other and reached different conclusions. For intervals of 0 to 5 months, preterm OR 1.07 (0.86–1.34) and low birth weight 1.03 (0.79–1.34) showed no difference. When comparing different mothers with each other, preterm birth was 1.41. So part of the risk from short intervals may come from the mothers' own circumstances. Contraception itself is not disputed; only the interval is. According to the NHS, condoms and implants can be used straight after birth, and breastfeeding mothers can use them too. Those who breastfeed and use combined contraceptive pills generally need to wait at least 6 weeks after birth. Discuss which method to use at the 42-day postpartum check; see item 16 of this section.
