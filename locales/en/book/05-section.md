# 5. Don't Waste Money

This section counts only money: how much you can save, the yearly difference in returns, and how much a sum returns on average over the long run. These are not converted into lifespan or time. Entries that involve investing only explain how to do the math, using verified historical data. None of them is investment advice.

Entries in this section are grouped by theme below. Item numbers are in parentheses.

**Everyday spending**: turn off auto-renewal (item 1), recalculate your phone and broadband plans (item 4), refuse extended warranties on electronics (item 11), choose drugs selected through national volume-based procurement (item 12), drink boiled tap water (item 14), pay per session at gyms (item 21), set a cooling-off period for big purchases (item 23), and do not stockpile just because an item has a crossed-out "original price" (item 24).

**Impulse spending and children's spending**: do not buy lottery tickets (item 5), do not tip livestreamers (item 8), a child's top-ups and tips can be refunded (item 9), anyone who asks a child for a verification code is a scammer (item 10), count bead bracelets and designer toys by the money you have already spent (item 33), accept jewelry and gemstones only with a CMA report (item 34), and treat blind boxes and gacha pulls as random sales (item 35).

**Online shopping and consumer rights**: sign a written contract for prepayments (item 22), online shopping follows the platform's rules (item 29), if something goes wrong with a livestream purchase (item 30), demand ten times the price for unsafe food (item 31), check spot-check and certification results before buying big items (item 32), buy prepackaged goods if you want to read the ingredient list (item 36), and if a group tour takes you to shopping stores (item 50).

**Tax, housing provident fund and medical insurance**: annual personal income tax reconciliation (item 2), the housing provident fund can be used for rent and renovation (item 3), family pooling tied to medical insurance (item 13), personal pension account (item 20), and when you pay employee pension insurance yourself, pay at the lowest tier (item 46).

**Saving, borrowing and investing**: walk away from high returns (item 6), convert loan costs to an annual rate before borrowing (item 7), do not trade stocks frequently (item 15), do not borrow to invest (item 16), use a broad-market index fund as your core holding (item 17), choose funds with low fees (item 18), do not put all your money in one place (item 19), save an emergency fund first (item 27), compare options before prepaying a mortgage (item 28), sell losing stocks by a set rule (item 37), buy US stocks through QDII funds or Hong Kong Stock Connect (item 38), do not buy virtual currency (item 45), and check bank-recommended wealth products before you buy them (item 51).

**Insurance**: buy pure-protection insurance first (item 25), buy enough third-party liability car insurance (item 26), insure only losses you cannot afford (item 39), buy term life insurance for the main earner first (item 40), cancel within the cooling-off period (item 41), tell deposits and insurance apart at the bank counter (item 42), do not use an agent to surrender a policy (item 43), and name specific people as beneficiaries (item 44).

**Buying a car**: inspect the car on the spot when you pick it up (item 47), have a used car contract state that the car is not a crash-damaged car (item 48), and do not pay the financial service fee at 4S dealerships (item 49).

### 1. Turn off all auto-renewals and renew manually when they expire
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. Go through the auto-deduction lists in Alipay, WeChat, Apple and Android. The full check takes 10 to 20 minutes, once. After that, each renewal takes one extra tap on your part.
- Plain language: What you save is the subscriptions you no longer use but still get charged for every month. Go through the auto-deduction lists in Alipay (a payment app), WeChat, Apple and Android. It takes only a few minutes. By rule, a merchant must clearly remind you before a charge and give you a simple way to cancel at any time.
- Benefit: What you save equals the total of the subscriptions you no longer use but still pay for. Each person is different, so there is no standard figure. Regulators require merchants to clearly remind you before renewal. They must also offer a simple option to cancel at any time, and cancelling costs nothing (China, in force since 2021).
- Evidence level: C
- Sources: 国家市场监督管理总局 (2021, 2025 修正). 网络交易监督管理办法（总局令第 37 号，第十八条）. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/fgs/art/2025/art_4b47c79b8d994a42bba4835997688faa.html>
- Notes: China only: Services you really use often are usually cheaper paid yearly than monthly, so keeping auto-renewal can be worthwhile for them. Turn off the ones you forgot about but still pay for. For how a business selling memberships on its website should send reminders, see section 26, item 13 (selling auto-renewing memberships).

### 2. File your annual personal income tax reconciliation every March to June, and fill in every special deduction you qualify for
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. It takes about 10 to 20 minutes in the Individual Income Tax app.
- Plain language: People who worked only part of the year, changed jobs mid-year, or missed deductions such as child education, mortgage interest, rent or elderly support often get money back. You can file on your phone. You can skip it if your comprehensive income is 120,000 yuan or less, or the tax you would owe is 400 yuan or less.
- Benefit: Filing combines four types of income into one annual total: wages and salaries, labour remuneration, manuscript fees and royalties. Overpaid tax is refunded, and underpaid tax must be paid. There are two cases where you can skip it: your annual comprehensive income is no more than 120,000 yuan, or the tax still owed is no more than 400 yuan. These two thresholds come from a 2023 announcement by the State Taxation Administration, valid until December 31, 2027. Conversely, people in these situations usually get money back: those who had income for only a few months of the year, those who changed jobs mid-year, and those who did not claim deductions such as child education, mortgage interest, rent or elderly support.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国家税务总局 (2025). 个人所得税综合所得汇算清缴管理办法（第六条）及其解读. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202502/content_7006465.htm>；财政部、税务总局 (2023). 关于延续实施个人所得税综合所得汇算清缴有关政策的公告. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202308/content_6900594.htm>
- Notes: China only: Anyone who owes tax must also file, and not filing is illegal. There are seven special additional deductions: child education, continuing education, serious illness medical costs, housing loan interest, housing rent, elderly support, and childcare for children under 3. Housing loan interest and housing rent: you can pick only one of the two, not both.

### 3. The housing provident fund is not only for buying a home: rent, renovation and property fees can also be withdrawn, under the new rules from September 20, 2026
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. You only need to go through one withdrawal application.
- Plain language: The housing provident fund (a mandatory housing savings account for employees in China) is not only for buying a home. The new rules list nine situations where you can withdraw, including paying rent, renovating the home you live in, and paying property fees for that home. After you apply, the fund center must decide within 3 days whether to approve it.
- Benefit: The revised regulation lists nine situations in which you can withdraw. These include paying rent, renovating a home you live in, and paying property management fees for a home you live in. The management center must decide to approve or reject the withdrawal within 3 days, counting from the day it accepts your application.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 国务院 (2026). 国务院关于修改《住房公积金管理条例》的决定（国务院令第 844 号，2026 年 7 月 31 日国务院第 93 次常务会议通过，「自2026年9月20日起施行」）：修改后第二十四条「职工有下列情形之一的，可以提取职工住房公积金账户内的存储余额：（一）支付房租的；（二）购买、建造、翻建、大修自住住房的；（三）偿还购房贷款本息的；（四）装修自住住房的；（五）支付自住住房物业费的；（六）离休、退休的；（七）完全丧失劳动能力，并与单位终止劳动关系的；（八）出境定居的；（九）国务院批准的其他住房消费情形。」；第二十五条「住房公积金管理中心应当自受理申请之日起3日内作出准予提取或者不准提取的决定」；第十八条缴存比例「均不得低于职工上一年度月平均工资的5％」. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12946/202608/content_7079363.html>
- Notes: China only: The money in the account is your own wages plus the part your employer adds. If you leave it untouched, it earns only a very low interest rate. The rules also say that when an employee dies or is declared dead, heirs and legatees can withdraw the account balance. The documents you need and the exact steps are set by each city's fund management center, so check your local center's guide before you go. The fund can also be used for a home loan at a lower rate than commercial loans; see section 15, item 16 (use the housing provident fund for a loan first). For property companies that cut water and power to push for property fees, see section 15, item 17 (property companies cutting off water and power).

### 4. Recalculate your phone and broadband plans every year, downgrade what you do not need, and complain if your operator will not let you switch
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. Checking one bill takes about 10 to 20 minutes. The hard part is holding firm when customer service refuses to downgrade you.
- Plain language: Your old plan is often tens of yuan more expensive than plans sold now, and that adds up to hundreds a year. By rule, an operator cannot refuse or delay a switch without a valid reason. It also cannot set special prices just for people who switch, and if it blocks you, you can complain.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT) rules say an operator may not "refuse, block or delay providing number portability services to users without a valid reason". It may not "use interception, throttling or other technical means to affect the service quality of users who port their numbers". It may not "set special tariffs or marketing plans for number-porting users". Old plans are often tens of yuan more expensive than plans on sale now. Staying on one wastes hundreds of yuan a year.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 工业和信息化部 (2019). 携号转网服务管理规定（工信部信管〔2019〕242 号）. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2019-11/13/content_5451483.htm>
- Notes: China only: First check in the carrier's business hall app how much data and how many call minutes you actually used in the last six months, then choose a plan based on that usage. If customer service says downgrading must be done at a carrier shop, or that you cannot change during the contract period, ask them for a written basis. If they cannot provide one, call 12300 (the national telecom complaints line) to complain.

### 5. Do not buy lottery tickets
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. Not buying is saving.
- Plain language: By rule, a Double Color Ball ticket pays out 51% of its sales as prizes. Over time, you get back about 0.51 yuan for every 1 yuan you spend. The rest is lost.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Finance sets minimum prize shares of sales: 50% for lotto and number games, 65% for scratch-off (instant) tickets, and 73% for guessing games. Double Color Ball pays out 51% of sales as prizes, so for every 100 yuan sold, 51 yuan goes to prizes. For you, that means that for each 1 yuan you spend, you get back 0.51 yuan on average, a 49% loss. In 2024, 623.486 billion yuan in lottery tickets was sold nationwide, and 161.031 billion yuan went to the public welfare fund. That money did not return to people who bought tickets (China, 2024).
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 财政部 (2015). 关于规范和加强彩票资金构成比例管理的通知（财综〔2015〕94 号）. <http://m.mof.gov.cn/czxw/201511/t20151113_1560814.htm>；财政部 (2025). 财政部公告 2025 年第 8 号（2024 年彩票公益金筹集分配情况）. <http://zhs.mof.gov.cn/zhengcefabu/202508/t20250828_3970977.htm>；中国福利彩票双色球游戏规则（第十四条）. <https://mzj.nc.gov.cn/ncsmzj/flcp/202111/5dccc1a5da89434e94a62f8ea0e5887e.shtml>
- Notes: China only: Over the long run, you always lose, because the system is set up that way, not because of luck. You may buy if you want, but treat it as a 2-yuan-per-purchase entertainment expense, never as a way to make money.

### 6. If you see "high returns", "capital guaranteed" or "sure profit", walk away
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. Just pause when you hear these words.
- Plain language: A former regulator chief said in 2018 that a return above 6% is a warning sign, and a return of 10% or more means you should expect to lose all your money. Legitimate wealth products may no longer promise to protect your capital or guarantee returns. If you lose money in an illegal fundraising scheme, no one will pay you back.
- Benefit: In 2018 at the Lujiazui Forum, the former chairman of the China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission said: "A return above 6% should raise a question mark. Above 8% is dangerous. At 10% or more, be prepared to lose all your principal." The 2018 asset management guidelines require financial institutions not to promise principal protection or guaranteed returns, and not to advance funds to cover redemptions in any form when they face difficulty paying out. So legitimate wealth products no longer offer capital protection. Losses from illegal fundraising are borne by the participants themselves, and no one compensates them afterwards (China, 2018 and 2021).
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 郭树清 (2018). 防范化解金融风险 奋力跨越重大关口（第十届陆家嘴论坛讲话，国家金融监督管理总局官网）：「在打击非法集资过程中，努力通过多种方式让人民群众认识到，高收益意味着高风险，收益率超过6%的就要打问号，超过8%的就很危险，10%以上就要准备损失全部本金」. <https://www.nfra.gov.cn/cn/view/pages/ItemDetail.html?docId=182418>；天津市地方金融管理局转载. <https://jrgz.tj.gov.cn/ztlm/ztzl/jrjfwphjr/202006/t20200625_2744709.html>；中国人民银行等 (2018). 关于规范金融机构资产管理业务的指导意见（银发〔2018〕106 号，第二条、第六条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2018/content_5323101.htm>；国务院 (2021). 防范和处置非法集资条例（国务院令第 737 号，第二十五条）. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5588815.htm>
- Notes: China only: The 6%, 8% and 10% lines are rules of thumb set by regulators, not legal provisions. The quote was made while cracking down on illegal fundraising, and it targets products and investment companies that promise high returns. This is not investment advice. If you run a business and want to borrow from people or bring people in as shareholders, see section 12, item 26 (raising money from people to start a business) for the red lines.

### 7. Do not pay only the minimum on your credit card, and do not take installment plans or consumer loans to fund spending. If you must borrow, convert the interest into an annual rate before comparing.
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. Keep enough cash to pay the full balance before the statement date. The hard part is resisting installment plans that spread out large purchases. If you must borrow, spend a few minutes asking each lender for the annual rate.
- Plain language: Paying only the minimum is a loan that charges daily interest, and it cancels your interest-free period. A plan that says "0.6% fee per month" really costs about 13.8% a year, not 7.2%, because the fee stays on the original amount as you repay. To compare loans, look only at the annual rate.
- Benefit: Before 2020, credit card overdraft interest had a ceiling and a floor. The ceiling was 0.05% per day, and the floor was 0.7 times the ceiling. 0.05% a day equals 18.25% a year. This is simple interest: interest does not roll into the principal. From 2021, card issuers and cardholders set the price themselves. If you use minimum repayment, the unpaid principal is charged full interest every day, and the interest-free period ends at the same time. The central bank has required issuers to show the annual rate clearly, and not to display only daily rates or daily repayment amounts. From 2021, every lender must state the annual rate clearly in its ads and contracts. Daily and monthly rates may be shown too, but they may not look more prominent than the annual rate. The annual rate must include interest and all fees directly tied to the loan. For a loan repaid in installments, the money you actually use is based on the principal remaining after each payment. The central bank gave an example: borrow 100,000 yuan, repay over 12 months, with a 0.5% installment fee per month on the original principal, and a 1,000-yuan service fee paid when you borrow. Under simple interest, the annual rate is about 12.80%. Under compound interest (the internal rate of return method), it is about 13.58%. Looking only at the fee rate, you would think the cost is 0.5% × 12 + 1% = 7% a year. The real cost is almost double, because the principal falls every month but the installment fee stays on 100,000 yuan. Using the same method, a credit card with 12 installments, a 0.6% monthly fee and no other charges looks like 7.2% a year on paper. With compound interest it is about 13.8% a year, roughly 1.9 times the nominal figure. For 6 to 36 installments, the multiple is between 1.8 and 2. If the lender is an individual or an ordinary company, that is private lending. If the agreed rate exceeds 4 times the one-year loan prime rate (LPR) at the time of the contract, courts will not support the excess. If interest was deducted from the loan upfront, the court calculates the principal based on the money you actually received. The one-year LPR on August 20, 2026 was 3.0%, so 4 times that is 12%. This line does not reach banks, consumer finance companies and other licensed financial institutions. It also does not apply to seven types of local financial organizations, including small loan companies, pawn shops and finance leasing companies. So do not apply the 12% limit to their loans; check the annual rate written in the contract (China, 2020 and 2021).
- Evidence level: C
- Sources: 中国人民银行 (2020). 关于推进信用卡透支利率市场化改革的通知（银发〔2020〕327 号）. <https://xining.pbc.gov.cn/zhengwugongkai/4081330/4406346/4693549/4159909/index.html>；中国人民银行 (2021). 中国人民银行公告〔2021〕第 3 号（第一、三、四条，附件《采用内部收益率法计算贷款年化利率示例》第三例）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-03/31/content_5597156.htm>；最高人民法院 (2020 年第二次修正). 关于审理民间借贷案件适用法律若干问题的规定（第一、二十五、二十六条）. <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/94b6623974526df7d2430a3c73f050.html>（最高人民法院公报）；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于新民间借贷司法解释适用范围问题的批复（法释〔2020〕27 号）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/285171.html>；全国银行间同业拆借中心受中国人民银行授权 (2026). 2026 年 8 月 20 日贷款市场报价利率（LPR）公告. <https://www.chinamoney.com.cn/chinese/rdgz/20260820/3399885.html>
- Notes: China only: The 18.25% is calculated directly as 0.05% × 365, without compounding. With compounding it would be higher. Some banks offer discounts, so the annual rate printed on your own bill is the one that counts. When you truly run short of cash, first try legitimate channels with lower rates, rather than letting interest roll up while you pay only the minimum. When comparing two loans, ask the lender for the annual rate calculated with compound interest. If they give you simple interest, the rule requires the statement to say "simple interest", and simple interest comes out lower than compound interest. The 13.8% figure and the 1.8 to 2 times multiple are this book's own calculations using the central bank's method; the method is in the verification notes. Do not touch high-interest loans from private lenders; see section 7, item 15 (do not borrow from loan sharks). For fees that a car dealer charges on a car loan, see item 49 of this section (financial service fee at 4S car dealerships). If you already cannot repay, see section 7, item 23 (credit card or online loans you cannot repay).

### 8. Do not tip livestreamers, do not top up games, and do not place impulse orders
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. Spend a few minutes on three things: turn off password-free payment, set per-transaction and daily limits, and remove saved payment methods from the platform. The hard part is holding back in the moment.
- Plain language: Gamers in China spend about 480 yuan a year each on games on average, and that money is almost never recovered. The law lets minors get back money spent without parental consent. Adults have no such right. The easiest defense is to set limits in advance: turn off password-free payment, set per-transaction and daily limits, and unlink your payment methods.
- Benefit: This is a large expense. In 2024, China's domestic game market had actual sales revenue of 325.783 billion yuan, with 674 million users, an average of about 480 yuan per person (this figure comes from dividing the first two numbers). Money spent is almost never recovered. There is one exception: when a person with limited civil capacity (here, a minor) tips or tops up without the consent of a guardian, and the guardian asks for a refund, "the people's court shall support" the request. There is no such rule for an adult's own tips and top-ups. Also, minors may play online games only on Friday, Saturday, Sunday and public holidays, and then only one hour a day, from 20:00 to 21:00 (China, 2020 and 2021).
- Evidence level: B
- Sources: 国家新闻出版署 (2024). 今年中国游戏市场收入超 3257 亿元创新高（中国音数协游戏工委《2024 年中国游戏产业报告》）. <https://www.nppa.gov.cn/xxfb/ywdt/202412/t20241216_877445.html>；最高人民法院 (2020). 关于依法妥善审理涉新冠肺炎疫情民事案件若干问题的指导意见（二）（法发〔2020〕17 号，第 9 条）. <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/230181.html>；国家新闻出版署 (2021). 关于进一步严格管理 切实防止未成年人沉迷网络游戏的通知（国新出发〔2021〕14 号）. <https://www.nppa.gov.cn/xxfb/tzgs/202108/t20210830_666285.html>
- Notes: China only: Rated B because no one has directly measured "how much you save by not tipping"; there are only industry-wide totals. The two practical steps, turning off password-free payment and setting limits, are rules of thumb, so on their own they would be C. The original text of Civil Code articles 19 and 145 is quoted word for word in item 9 (children using phones to top up or tip). Not investment advice. If a spouse spends a large amount of shared money on tips behind your back, see section 10, item 22 (tipping livestreamers).

### 9. A child who tops up or tips using a phone: for large spending over age 8 that a parent has not ratified, you can claim a refund
<!-- tags: money=0 time=mid will=some gain=mid scope=money who=child -->
- Cost: No money. It takes time to gather evidence and deal with the platform, which usually refuses at first and refunds later. If talks fail, you must sue, which takes at least several months, and you pay your own lawyer's fees. So prepare your materials first, and go through the platform and the 12315 consumer complaint hotline before anything else. The hard part is that your child may not want to talk, so you need to ask patiently.
- Plain language: Spending by a child over 8 that does not fit the child's age and understanding needs a parent's consent or later approval. If the parent gives neither, the deal is void and you can ask for a refund. If the parent never responds, the law treats it as a refusal.
- Benefit: A minor over eight is a person with limited civil capacity, meaning they can act alone only in ways that match their age and understanding. Civil acts that do not match their age and understanding need the consent or ratification of their legal representative: the parent must agree in advance, or approve afterwards. A child under eight has no civil capacity and cannot act alone, so a legal representative acts for them. If the legal representative does not respond, it is deemed a refusal to ratify: if the parent stays silent, the deal is treated as not approved.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 全国人大 (2020). 民法典. 最高人民检察院转载全文. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml> 第十九条：「八周岁以上的未成年人为限制民事行为能力人，实施民事法律行为由其法定代理人代理或者经其法定代理人同意、追认；但是，可以独立实施纯获利益的民事法律行为或者与其年龄、智力相适应的民事法律行为。」第二十条：「不满八周岁的未成年人为无民事行为能力人，由其法定代理人代理实施民事法律行为。」第一百四十五条：「限制民事行为能力人实施的纯获利益的民事法律行为或者与其年龄、智力、精神健康状况相适应的民事法律行为有效；实施的其他民事法律行为经法定代理人同意或者追认后有效。」「法定代理人未作表示的，视为拒绝追认。」
- Notes: China only: The key is evidence: you must show that the child, not you, operated the phone. Useful materials include: top-ups concentrated during class time or when parents are at work; device usage records; the child's chat and game records; and the child's own statement. "Matches age and understanding" has no fixed amount threshold: a few dozen yuan for a game skin is not the same as tens of thousands for a tip. Prevention is easier: set payment limits without a password, turn on teen mode, and do not tell your child your payment password. For your own impulsive tips or top-ups, see the entry above titled "Do not tip livestreamers, do not top up games, and do not place impulse orders."

### 10. Teach your child one rule: anyone online who asks you to use a parent's phone or to read out a verification code is a scammer
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=money who=child -->
- Cost: No money. Go through the six types of scams below with your child, then agree on one phrase: "If you get scammed, tell me first, and I won't scold you." Also set payment limits, turn on teen mode, and do not tell your child the payment password. The hard part is not losing your temper if your child does get scammed.
- Plain language: The tricks differ, but the last step is always the same: getting the child to transfer money from a parent's phone. So one rule is enough: anyone who asks you to touch a parent's phone or give out a verification code is a scammer. Add: "If you get scammed, tell me first, and I won't scold you."

### 11. Decline the extended warranty on electronics
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: Nothing upfront. The trade-off: once the warranty runs out, any repair comes out of your own pocket. That is unlikely to happen, though.
- Plain language: An extended warranty usually costs 10% to 50% of the product's price, and the seller's margin on it is far higher than on ordinary goods. Major parts already have a 3-year warranty under national rules. For a cheap extended warranty, divide its price by the cost of one repair. The result shows how likely a breakdown must be for it to pay off.
- Benefit: The extended warranty is usually priced at 10% to 50% of the product's original price. Industry estimates cited in the study put the average gross margin on extended warranties at 50% to 60%: for every 100 yuan sold, 50 to 60 yuan is profit. That is roughly 18 times the gross margin on ordinary goods. In 2003, extended warranties made up only 3% to 4% of Best Buy's revenue but contributed over 50% of its profit (US, retail data from the 2000s). For extended warranties priced well below this range, you can do the math yourself: extended warranty price ÷ cost of one repair = breakdown probability needed to break even. If you estimate the chance of at least one breakdown during the warranty period is higher than that figure, the warranty is worth buying. For example, for a 25 yuan extended warranty, assuming a repair costs 300 yuan, the chance of at least one breakdown must exceed about 8% to break even. The 300 yuan is only an example, not a statistic. Before calculating, subtract the part already covered by national rules. The national three-guarantee period (repair, replace, return) starts from the date on the purchase invoice: 1 year for whole washing machines and fridges, and 3 years for their main parts. Main parts of a washing machine are the motor, timer, program controller and capacitor; for a fridge, the compressor, thermostat and so on. Any overlap between the extended warranty and the three-guarantee period means you are paying for coverage you already have (nationwide, since 1995).
- Evidence level: B
- Sources: Chen T, Kalra A, Sun B (2009). Why Do Consumers Buy Extended Service Contracts? Journal of Consumer Research 36(4):611-623. <https://doi.org/10.1086/605298>；国家经贸委等 (1995). 部分商品修理更换退货责任规定（国经贸〔1995〕458 号，第八条三包有效期自开具发票之日起计算；附件《实施三包的部分商品目录（第一批）》洗衣机、家用电冰箱整机 1 年、主要部件 3 年）. 国家市场监督管理总局行政规范性文件. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/zc/xzgfxwj/art/2019/art_f943e66de125437d92df701e1654c47c.html>
- Notes: China only: The gross margin figure is an industry estimate that the paper cites (Business Week 2004); the paper did not measure it itself. The method above suits ordinary people who can afford one repair bill themselves. People who depend on one device to earn a living and have no spare must work it out separately. Phone screen insurance and similar cover should be assessed separately too: estimate how often you drop the phone, then check whether the premium pays off. A very cheap extended warranty will not cost you more than a few dozen yuan. Before buying, check when coverage starts: if it starts on the purchase date, it overlaps the early years of the three-guarantee period. Also check whether the seller, the brand or a third-party company provides the cover, and whether home visit and part replacement fees are charged. Read the manual and warranty card too: the brand's own warranty may be longer than the three-guarantee period.

### 15. Trade stocks infrequently, and hold back especially when prices swing wildly
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: Nothing. The hard part is seeing news and wanting to act, or adding more when everyone else is making money. You have to resist that.
- Plain language: American households that traded most often in the 1990s earned 11.4% a year, while the whole market returned 17.9%. The gap comes mainly from trading costs. In China's boom and crash of 2014 to 2015, the smallest 85% of household accounts traded too much. Compared with holding still, they lost 250 billion yuan.
- Benefit: This study looked at 66,465 American discount brokerage household accounts from 1991 to 1996. The group that traded most had an annualized return of 11.4%, meaning on average 11.4% a year. The whole market returned 17.9% over the same period, and the average household 16.4%. The average household had a turnover of 75% a year, meaning it replaced three-quarters of its holdings within a year. The gross returns (before trading costs) of the frequent-trading group and the infrequent group differed little. So the gap came mainly from the trading costs paid when buying and selling (US, 1991 to 1996). For China, the study used day-by-day account data from the Shanghai Stock Exchange, covering all about 40 million accounts. The sample period ran from July 2014 to December 2015, 18 months in total. The two groups had roughly equal total starting stock value: 880 billion yuan and 808 billion yuan. The result: the smallest 85% lost 250 billion yuan through active trading, while the largest 0.5% earned 254 billion yuan. The "loss" here is measured against holding still and doing nothing during the same market swing. The two figures are each about 30% of the group's starting stock value: 28% for the first and 31% for the second. For comparison, the two and a half years before June 2014 were calmer. Over any 18-month window in that period, the similar gap was only 1% to 3% of starting market value. ### 16. Do not invest borrowed money, do not use leverage, and do not buy what you do not understand
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: Nothing. The hard part is when the market is going up: resisting the thought of "borrowing a bit to earn more."
- Plain language: If you buy stocks with borrowed money and prices fall below the line, you cannot top up the shortfall and will be forced to sell. A paper loss becomes a real loss on the spot, with no chance to wait for a recovery. Regulators rule that people who have traded securities for less than half a year, and whose average daily assets over the last 20 trading days are under 500,000 yuan, cannot open a margin account. That threshold alone shows it is not meant for ordinary people.
- Benefit: Leverage means borrowing to buy, which magnifies losses. After you buy on margin, the market value of your collateral can fall below the maintenance margin line. This line is set by the brokerage and covers what you have pledged with it. If the line is breached and you cannot top up the collateral to restore it, the broker forcibly closes your position and sells on your behalf. A paper loss becomes a real loss, with no chance to wait for a recovery. Regulators stipulate that clients who have traded securities for less than half a year, lack risk-bearing capacity, and whose average daily securities assets over the last 20 trading days are below 500,000 yuan may not open a credit account. That threshold alone shows it is not a tool for ordinary people (China, 2015).

But the high cost of active management passes through in full as lower net returns for investors (net return means the return after all fees). The study used bootstrap simulation, reshuffling performance again and again to see how far luck alone can go. ### 18. Among similar funds, pick the one with the lower fees
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: Nothing. Before buying, check how much the three fees add up to: management fee, custody fee and sales service fee.
- Plain language: Each extra 1 percentage point of annual fee cuts your money by about 18% after 20 years, and by about 26% after 30 years. Among funds in the same category, choose the lower-fee one, and less of your money gets eaten up.
- Benefit: Fee differences nibble at your principal through compounding, meaning the amount deducted each year piles up year after year. A fee that is 1 percentage point higher each year cuts the end value by about 18.2% after 20 years (1 − 0.99^20 ≈ 0.182), and by about 26% after 30 years. End value means the balance in your account at that point. From 2023, China began a fund fee reform that lowered the cap on fees for actively managed equity funds (mainly buying stocks, with a fund manager picking them). From 7 July 2023, newly registered funds of this type charge a management fee of no more than 1.2% and a custody fee of no more than 0.2%. 136 fund companies also cut these two fees on their existing products to below this line.
- Evidence level: C
- Sources: 中国证监会 (2023). 证监会有关部门负责人就公募基金费率改革答记者问. <http://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100028/c7418692/content.shtml>；厦门证监局 (2024). 「新注册的主动权益类基金统一执行"管理费率不超过1.2%、托管费率不超过0.2%"的上限标准」. <http://www.csrc.gov.cn/xiamen/c105636/c7511608/content.shtml>；Fama EF, French KR (2010). 见第 17 条（宽基指数基金）. <https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2010.01598.x>
- Notes: China only: The formula is a mechanical calculation, and it assumes the two funds earn the same return before fees. In practice you must also include subscription fees, redemption fees and, for Class C shares, the sales service fee. This is not investment advice.

### 19. Do not put your money into one stock, one platform or one house
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: Nothing. The hard part is accepting this: you will not always pick the one that does best.
- Plain language: As long as several assets do not all rise and fall together, spreading your money keeps the average return the same but makes your account swing less. Putting all your money into one stock, one platform or one house means that if it goes wrong, you go down with it entirely.
- Benefit: As long as several assets do not all rise and fall together, spreading money keeps the expected return (the average you can earn) unchanged, while volatility (how much the account swings up and down) is lower. This is the basic conclusion of portfolio selection that Markowitz set out in 1952, and it has been used for decades since. Put the other way, if all your money is in one stock or one platform, your variance is that holding's variance: you rise and fall exactly with it. When it goes wrong, there is no hedge, so no other asset can help you recover.
- Evidence level: B
- Sources: Markowitz, H. (1952). Portfolio Selection. The Journal of Finance, 7(1), 77–91. <https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1952.tb01525.x>
- Notes: Rated B because the classic paper is a pure mathematical derivation and does not compute how much diversification actually saves in losses. Diversification only makes the account swing less; it does not guarantee that you will not lose money. Buying a broad index fund (see item 17 of this section) is itself an easy way to diversify. This is not investment advice.

### 20. Before opening a personal pension account, work out the tax savings and check the withdrawal conditions. If you pay no income tax, or your rate is only 3%, do not pay in just to cut taxes
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: Opening the account is free, but money you pay in cannot be withdrawn at will. Apart from reaching the basic pension age, you can withdraw under qualifying conditions such as eligible medical expenses, unemployment or minimum living allowance; the thresholds are in the notes. If you do not meet the conditions, you may still have to wait many years.
- Plain language: If you pay in a full 12,000 yuan, all within one tax bracket, the 10%, 20% and 30% brackets save you a net 840, 2,040 and 3,240 yuan. This already subtracts the 3% tax you pay on withdrawal; if your contribution crosses brackets, recalculate. People in the 3% bracket save tax that they pay back on withdrawal, at best breaking even. Do not put money you need soon into it; early withdrawal is allowed only for specified cases such as medical expenses, unemployment or minimum living allowance.
- Benefit: Money you pay in, up to 12,000 yuan a year, can be honestly deducted from your comprehensive income or business income. Investment returns inside the account are not subject to personal income tax for now. On withdrawal, the money is not merged into comprehensive income; it is taxed separately at 3%. The tax saved this year is the full-year tax before the deduction minus the full-year tax after it. The deduction can push you into a lower bracket, so you cannot simply multiply the current marginal rate by the amount you pay in. The marginal rate is the rate you pay on each additional yuan you earn. For example, after all other deductions, your annual comprehensive taxable income before paying in is 40,000 yuan, with tax of 1,480 yuan. After paying in and deducting 12,000 yuan, taxable income becomes 28,000 yuan and tax is 840 yuan, so you pay 640 yuan less this year. Assuming no investment return and no time value of money, withdrawing the 12,000 yuan principal later costs 360 yuan in tax, leaving a net tax difference of 280 yuan. You can use a bracket's rate to calculate this year's saving only if you pay the full 12,000 yuan and the whole deduction stays within one bracket. Again counting only principal, with no investment return and no time value, the net tax differences in the 10%, 20% and 30% brackets are 840, 2,040 and 3,240 yuan. These are tax differences that occur at different times. They are not investment returns, and they cannot be divided by years to get an annualized return. Someone who previously paid no income tax gets no tax saving now, and still pays 3% on withdrawal. Someone who was only in the 3% bracket saves at most 3% of the amount paid in. If annual comprehensive income after all deductions is no more than 36,000 yuan, it falls in this bracket. Counting only principal and ignoring time value, this tax saving is no more than the tax on the principal you later withdraw, so it cannot be justified by tax savings alone.
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: 财政部、税务总局 (2024). 关于在全国范围实施个人养老金个人所得税优惠政策的公告（财政部 税务总局公告 2024 年第 21 号，2024 年 12 月 12 日）. <https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c102416/c5237110/content.html>；人力资源社会保障部等五部门 (2022). 个人养老金实施办法（第七、八、九、十二、十三条）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-11/05/content_5724783.htm>；全国人民代表大会常务委员会 (2018). 中华人民共和国个人所得税法（2018 年第七次修正，个人所得税税率表一）. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/zgrdw/npc/xinwen/2018-09/05/content_2060671.htm>；人力资源社会保障部等五部门 (2025). 关于领取个人养老金有关问题的通知（人社部发〔2025〕39 号，第一、二、三、七项）. <https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100013/c5242402/content.html>
- Notes: China only: The current withdrawal conditions are six: reaching the basic pension age, total loss of ability to work, emigrating abroad, and the three below. For medical expenses, the look-back period is the 12 months before application: out-of-pocket costs within the reimbursement catalog, after insurance, for you, your spouse or your minor children. The cumulative total must exceed the provincial per capita disposable income of last year. For unemployment, unemployment insurance benefits must have accumulated to 12 months within the 2 years before application. For minimum living allowance, you must be currently receiving urban or rural minimum living security. If any one condition is met, you can apply for verification through your account bank, the National Social Security Service Platform (the national social security online service) or other channels. Funds already invested in products must also be handled under the rules of each product. Do not put emergency money into it (see item 27 for emergency funds). Whether it is worth paying in also depends on how long the money will be used, product returns, fees and risks; do not decide on the tax saving alone. The money in the account must be invested in products you choose, and some products lose money. For choosing products, see items 17 and 18 of this section (broad index funds, and lower-fee funds first). The deduction certificate is issued by the personal pension information management service platform. Income subject to cumulative withholding, such as salary, can be deducted in the current year's withholding or in the next year's annual tax reconciliation. Other income is deducted in next year's annual reconciliation (see item 2).

### 21. Pay per visit or on short-term plans at the gym, unless you have a stable record of more than a year
<!-- tags: money=low time=low will=no gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: Pay-per-visit costs more per session than an annual pass spread over the year. You need to estimate honestly how many times a month you will actually go.
- Plain language: In a three-year US study, members on contracts over 70 dollars a month went an average of only 4.3 times a month, which is over 17 dollars per visit. A 10-visit card costs only 10 dollars per visit. These members paid about 600 dollars extra over their membership. Unless you already have a stable record of going for over a year, pay-per-visit or short contracts are the better deal.
- Benefit: This study followed 7,752 members at 3 US fitness clubs over 3 years. Members on contracts over 70 dollars a month went an average of only 4.3 times a month, over 17 dollars per visit. A 10-visit card costs only 10 dollars per visit. These members paid about 600 dollars more on average during their membership. Also, the share of monthly-fee members who had not cancelled after more than a year was 17% higher than for annual-fee members (US, three-year panel data).

- Notes: This is one study, using US data, but the underlying logic is widespread: people overestimate their future self-control. If you have a record of going more than twice a week, continuously for a full year, then the annual pass really is cheaper.

### 24. Do not stock up because of crossed-out prices and big sales events
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. The cost is that you sometimes pass up real bargains too. You need to know the usual prices of the few things you buy often. The hard part is resisting the urge to stock up on the day of a big sale.
- Plain language: People get stuck on the first price they see, and later adjustments rarely go far enough. So "original price 999, now 499" makes a bargain look bigger than it is. Under the rules, the reference price must be the store's lowest price in the seven days before the sale. The crossed-out price may never have been charged.
- Benefit: People's judgments are anchored by the first number they see, and later adjustments usually do not go far enough. So "original price 999, now 499" systematically inflates your sense of how much you are saving. Regulations say the reference price for a discount must be the same store's lowest transaction price in the seven days before the promotion. A discount should therefore be measured against the lowest price the store actually sold at in that window. The crossed-out price may never have been real (China, in force since 2020).
- Evidence level: C
- Sources: Tversky A, Kahneman D (1974). Judgment under Uncertainty: Heuristics and Biases. Science 185(4157):1124-1131. <https://doi.org/10.1126/science.185.4157.1124>；国家市场监督管理总局 (2020). 规范促销行为暂行规定（总局令第 32 号，第二十一条）. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/fgs/art/2023/art_cae53a080be2401e8f91c6d6291539f8.html>
- Notes: China only: The anchoring finding comes from laboratory studies, and no one has calculated how much stockpiling actually loses money. Stockpiling also has costs you will not record: items expire, they take up space, and you start thinking "we already have it, so use more." Stock up only on items that meet all three conditions: a long shelf life, you are sure to use them up, and the unit price really is lower than before.

### 25. Prefer pure-protection insurance, and treat refunds and dividends as not guaranteed
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: Pure-protection policies pay nothing back when they end, which can feel like money wasted. You have to arrange your own savings separately. The hard part is accepting that this money will not come back.
- Plain language: The insurer does not guarantee the dividends and refunds on these products. The policy document must state in bold that future policy dividends are not guaranteed. ### 37. Sell losing stocks by a rule you set in advance, not by averaging down to win back your losses
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=some gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is admitting that this position is a loss and then selling it.
- Plain language: People tend to sell winners and hold losers. In records from 10,000 US accounts over seven years, winning stocks were sold more than 50% more often than losing ones. Those same investors also added to losers more readily. The losers they kept trailed the winners they sold by 3.4 percentage points over the next year.
- Benefit: This study covers all trading records from 1987 to 1993 for 10,000 accounts at a US discount brokerage. The proportion of gains realized (PGR) is 0.148. The proportion of losses realized (PLR) is 0.098. The difference is 0.050, with a t-value above 35. Measured per day, winning stocks were more than 50% more likely to be sold than losing ones. The same accounts also added to losing stocks more actively. The rate of buying a losing stock again is 0.135, versus 0.094 for winners, with a t-value of 19. The study also tested the idea that "the ones you hold will come back." The result was the opposite: over the following year, winners that were sold beat losers that were held without selling by 3.4 percentage points on average. Excess return means the portion above the market index. The author also worked through an example using averages. Selling $1,000 of stock: selling a loser rather than a winner yields about 4.4% higher returns over the next year. That 4.4% includes savings from the tax deduction gained by selling losers early. The tax calculation follows US tax rules (United States, 1987 to 1993).
- Evidence level: A
- Sources: Odean T (1998). Are Investors Reluctant to Realize Their Losses? The Journal of Finance 53(5):1775-1798. <https://doi.org/10.1111/0022-1082.00072>
- Notes: This item covers whether to sell; item 15 (don't trade stocks frequently) covers not trading too often. The two do not conflict. Setting rules in advance and following them is the author's experience that you end up trading less, which the study did not test. Averaging down is arithmetically correct: buying more lowers your average cost. The mistake is treating it as a way to recover. Its actual effect is to increase the money you have tied up in that one stock; see item 19 (don't put your money into one stock). The tax part is based on US tax rules and does not carry over to China, but the rest still holds. This is US account data from the 1980s and 1990s; A-share trading costs and tax rules are different. Not investment advice.

### 39. Buy insurance only for losses your household cannot absorb, and cover losses you can afford with an emergency fund
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. What you need to do is list the big losses your household might face and see which ones your savings cannot cover. Takes about half an hour.
- Plain language: Only part of your premium goes out as claims; the rest covers the insurer's costs and profit. So insurance is worth buying for losses that are rare but that your household cannot pay for, such as serious illness, injuring someone while driving, or losing the household's main earner. Small losses your savings can cover belong in your emergency fund.
- Benefit: Only part of the premiums an insurer collects is paid out as claims. The rest covers sales and operating costs, plus the company's profit. Regulators require companies selling individual short-term health insurance to publish a "comprehensive claims ratio" on their websites every six months. It roughly measures the share of premiums paid out in claims, so you can check on a company's website how much it pays out. So for any one loss, buying insurance is, on average over the long run, a small money loss. What you get is to spread a single large expense that could crush the household into small annual payments. The smaller the loss and the more you can afford it, the less worthwhile this trade is. By this standard, the items already covered in this book are worth buying. For one-year medical insurance, see item 20 of section 7 (one-year medical insurance). For people who drive, see item 26 of this section (third-party liability insurance). For people going abroad, see item 4 of section 21 (overseas medical care). For a family whose members rely on your income, see item 40 of this section (term life insurance) (nationwide, since 2021).

### 40. If others depend on your income, buy term life insurance for the earner first, not for the children
<!-- tags: money=high time=low will=no gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: Several hundred to several thousand yuan a year, depending on age, coverage amount, and policy term (author's rough estimate, no official figures). Before buying, you must disclose your health honestly; this takes half an hour.
- Plain language: Term life insurance covers a fixed number of years; if you die during that period, your family receives a lump sum. It is for people who depend on your income, such as when a mortgage is unpaid or children are young. If nobody relies on your income, you don't need it. Death coverage on a child who doesn't earn is also capped by law.
- Benefit: The regulator defines life insurance as personal insurance whose subject is the insured's lifespan, divided into term life, whole life, endowment and other types. ### 41. If you regret signing a personal insurance policy longer than one year, cancel within the cooling-off period to get nearly all of your premium back
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=mid scope=money -->
- Cost: No money. Call the insurer's customer service or go to a branch; it takes about 15 minutes. The hard part is reading the contract calmly within 15 days of signing.
- Plain language: Personal insurance policies longer than one year have a cooling-off period. For policies bought at a bank, it is 15 days after you receive the policy, and cancelling costs at most 10 yuan in fees. After that, cancelling returns only the surrender value, often less than you paid. If a follow-up call asks whether you understand, don't say yes unless you do.
- Benefit: Regulators require that personal insurance policies longer than one year state when the cooling-off period starts, how many days it lasts, and what rights the policyholder has during it. The cooling-off period for long-term health insurance may not be less than 15 days. For policies sold through banks with a term over one year, the contract always specifies a 15-day cooling-off period. It starts from the day you receive the policy and sign for it in writing, counted in calendar days. The cover of such a policy must print this notice: "You have the right to a full refund (minus a fee of up to 10 yuan) within 15 days of receiving the insurance contract. Cancelling after 15 days incurs a loss." After the cooling-off period you can still cancel the contract at any time. The insurer must refund the policy's cash value (the refundable part of the policy) within 30 days, per the contract. Financial regulators' risk warnings say that cancelling long-term personal insurance after the cooling-off period usually returns less than the total premiums paid. ### 42. At the bank counter, check whether you are signing for a deposit or an insurance policy before you sign

- Plain language: Banks may sell insurance but may not describe it as a deposit. ### 44. Name a specific person as the beneficiary on your policy, and claim the money in time: 5 years for life insurance, 2 years for other insurance
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: Free. You fill it in once when you buy the policy. To change the beneficiary later, you must notify the insurer in writing.
- Plain language: If no beneficiary is named, or the named beneficiary dies first, the payout counts as part of the estate. It must first be used to repay the debts and taxes the deceased owed. If you named "my wife, [name]" and then divorced, it counts as if no one was named. Family members need to know the policy exists. For life insurance, claim within 5 years of learning of the event. For other insurance, the limit is 2 years.
- Benefit: Under insurance law, the insured person or the policyholder names the beneficiary. If the policyholder names one, the insured must consent. The payout counts as the insured's estate, and is shared under inheritance rules, in three cases. First, no beneficiary is named or the naming is unclear. Second, the beneficiary died before the insured and there is no other beneficiary. Third, the beneficiary has legally lost or waived the right and there is no other beneficiary. If the beneficiary and the insured die in the same event and the order cannot be established, the beneficiary is presumed to have died first. Estate money must first pay off the deceased's unpaid taxes and debts, and only the rest is divided. A Supreme People's Court interpretation states that if the beneficiary is written as a "name plus family relationship" and that relationship has changed when the event happens, it counts as no beneficiary named. A beneficiary change that was not notified to the insurer does not take effect against the insurer. The time limit to claim is 5 years for life insurance and 2 years for other insurance, counted from the day you knew or should have known of the event. Life insurance sums are often in the hundreds of thousands of yuan, so the benefit is rated large (nationwide, amended 2015, in effect from 2021).

### 51. Check any product a bank employee recommends in the bank's own app or on its website before buying it; if you cannot find it there, it may be a private "rogue" sale by the employee
<!-- tags: money=0 time=low will=no gain=high scope=money -->
- Cost: Free. Search the product name in the bank's app or on its website before buying; it takes a few minutes. The hard part is telling a familiar account manager, "let me check first."
- Plain language: When a bank employee privately sells a product that the bank did not issue and did not authorize, it is called a "rogue sale." Bank-agency products may only be sold at the bank's branches, on its website and in its app, and you can look them up there. If you cannot find it there, do not buy it yet. Some courts have ordered banks to bear half the loss from rogue sales. In a securities-branch case, the first trial dismissed the claim, and only on appeal was partial compensation awarded.
- Benefit: The Beijing Third Intermediate People's Court explains that a wealth-management "rogue sale" is when staff of a financial institution, using their job position, privately sell a third-party product that their institution did not issue and is not authorized to distribute. The National Financial Regulatory Administration's 2025 rules say that when a bank sells another institution's product as an agent, it may only set up a sales section in its own branches, official website and app. The bank must provide a search function in its own channels showing that the product is agency-sold, its type and its issuer. Banks may not mix agency products with deposits or with their own products. The first page of marketing material for an agency product must state: "This product is issued and managed by [partner institution]. [Bank], as the agent, does not bear investment or redemption responsibility." The bank must authorize which staff may sell which products and publish this at its branches. Sales at branches must be audio- and video-recorded. Banks and their staff may not "privately recommend or sell unapproved products in the name of the institution they belong to" (nationwide, from 1 October 2025). In 2022, the Beijing Third Intermediate Court published a case. In 2013, a bank wealth manager told a client that a product was the bank's bestseller and that the bank was the custodian. The client signed a membership agreement with a fund company and transferred 1.2 million yuan to the company's account. The principal was never returned, and the fund company's controlling person went to prison. The court held that the employee's promotion during work hours and at the workplace, using job position, was an act in the course of duties. The client chased high interest and was also at fault. The first instance ordered the bank and the client to share the losses equally, and the appeal upheld this. In October 2026, the Supreme People's Court, the People's Bank of China, the National Financial Regulatory Administration and the China Securities Regulatory Commission jointly published a typical case. A securities brokerage branch manager privately sold private equity funds that the branch had not authorized, and a client invested 1 million yuan. The client sued in August 2023, and the first instance dismissed the claim. On appeal, the court found the branch at fault as well as the client, and changed the judgment to order the branch to pay about 577,000 yuan (exactly 576,716.97 yuan). The case number is from 2024 (nationwide, 2022 and 2026).
