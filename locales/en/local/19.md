### 1. Overtime is paid at 1.5x, 2x or 3x; if it is not paid, complain to labor inspection, and late payers owe 50% to 100% more
- In the United States: Under the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA), covered nonexempt employees must receive overtime pay for hours worked over 40 per workweek at a rate not less than time and one-half their regular rates of pay. If your employer fails to pay required overtime, you can file a complaint with the Wage and Hour Division (WHD).
- Official sources: https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/overtime (Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA), 29 U.S.C. § 207)

- Reliability: verified

### 2. Annual leave is 5, 10 or 15 days by cumulative years of service; unused days are paid at 300% of the daily wage

### 10. Many injuries from dust, noise and chemical poisons are irreversible (such as pneumoconiosis and noise-induced deafness): the employer must supply protective equipment, and you may refuse work that has no protective measures
- In the United States: The Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) requires employers to provide personal protective equipment (PPE) when necessary to protect against hazards, usually at no cost to the worker. You have a legal right to refuse to work if you believe you are in imminent danger and there is not enough time to go through regular channels.
- Official sources: https://www.osha.gov/personal-protective-equipment ; https://www.osha.gov/workers/right-to-refuse (Occupational Safety and Health Act of 1970, 29 U.S.C. § 654)
