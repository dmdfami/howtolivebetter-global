### 1. Before paying tuition, check whether the school is on CSCSE's list of recognised institutions
- In the United States: The US Department of Education does not evaluate or recognize foreign degrees, and there is no federal regulation of credential evaluation services. Foreign credentials must be evaluated by private, non-governmental services, usually for a fee. The requirements depend on whether you are seeking to study (contact the school), to work (contact the employer), or to practice a licensed profession (contact the state licensing board).
- Official sources: U.S. Department of Education. Recognition of Foreign Qualifications. <https://www.ed.gov/about/initiatives/international-affairs/recognition-of-foreign-qualifications>
