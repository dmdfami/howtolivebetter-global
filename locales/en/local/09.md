### 2. Don't post or forward images, memes, or comments that insult heroes and martyrs, and don't joke about the national flag or anthem
- In the United States: In the United States, insulting military heroes, the national flag, or the anthem is protected as free speech under the First Amendment. The Supreme Court ruled in Texas v. Johnson that burning or desecrating the American flag is protected expressive conduct. No criminal penalties apply to such actions.

### 5. Refuse "part-time jobs" that ask you to use your own card to receive, withdraw, or transfer money, however much they offer
- In the United States: Acting as a money mule, even unknowingly, can involve you in federal crimes such as money laundering, wire fraud, or bank fraud under 18 U.S.C. § 1956 and § 1343. Penalties include severe fines and up to 20 years in prison. The FBI advises the public to refuse any job requiring the transfer of funds through personal accounts.
