### 2. After someone clearly refuses you, stop: continuing to pursue them is a public-security offence, not "sincerity"
- In the United States: Continuing to pursue someone after a clear refusal can constitute stalking or harassment, which are state and federal crimes. ### 10. For money in marriage, check the legal default rules first, then decide whether to sign a written agreement
- In the United States: Nine states use community property rules where most assets acquired during marriage are owned equally. The other states use equitable distribution, where courts divide property fairly but not necessarily equally. Couples can alter these default rules by signing a prenuptial or postnuptial agreement.

### 12. Large debts one spouse borrows without your signature or later acceptance do not automatically become your debt
- In the United States: In community property states, most debts incurred by either spouse during the marriage are joint debts. In equitable distribution states, debts in one spouse's name are generally their separate responsibility unless the funds were used for family necessities.
