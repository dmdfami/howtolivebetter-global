### 1. Under 16 there is no "going to work" option: an employer that hires you faces a 5,000 yuan monthly fine, so any employer willing to hire you is running illegal child labor
- In the United States: The federal Fair Labor Standards Act regulates youth employment in non-agricultural work, setting a general minimum age and restricting both the hours and occupations for minors. Employers violating these provisions face civil money penalties per affected minor. Some states enact stricter laws, in which case the stricter standard applies.
- Official sources: Fair Labor Standards Act, https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/child-labor
