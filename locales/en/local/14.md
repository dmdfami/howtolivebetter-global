### 5. If unauthorized charges hit your card, freeze it first, then report to the police, then demand that the bank pay out. The bank must prove you made the charge yourself.
- In the United States: The Electronic Fund Transfer Act limits your liability for unauthorized debit card charges to $50 if you report the loss within two business days. If you wait more than two days but less than 60 days, your liability can reach $500. The burden of proof is on the financial institution to show that an electronic fund transfer was authorized by you.
- Official sources: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2022-title15/html/USCODE-2022-title15-chap41-subchapVI-sec1693g.htm (15 U.S.C. 1693g)
