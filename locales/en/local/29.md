### 3. After losing your job, set a routine for your medical insurance and job hunting, and do not stay at home all day
- In the United States: You can apply for unemployment benefits through your state's unemployment insurance program. You may qualify to keep your employer's health insurance for up to 18 to 36 months under COBRA.
- Official sources: https://www.dol.gov/general/topic/unemployment-insurance, https://www.dol.gov/general/topic/health-plans/cobra
- Checked on: 2026-11-02
- Reliability: verified

### 4. If a loved one died by suicide, in an accident or by homicide, do not try to cope alone; actively seek professional help
- In the United States: You can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline for free, confidential support 24 hours a day. Seek help from a licensed mental health professional if needed.
- Official sources: https://www.samhsa.gov/find-help/988
- Checked on: 2026-11-02
- Reliability: verified
