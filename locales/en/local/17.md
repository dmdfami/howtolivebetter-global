### 1. While the elderly person is still clear-headed, put in writing who will be their guardian
- In the United States: You can make advance directives to set out your wishes for health care and name a health care proxy. This lets you choose who will make medical decisions if you cannot. Medicare Part B covers advance care planning as part of your yearly wellness visit.
- Official sources: https://www.medicare.gov/coverage/advance-care-planning ; https://medlineplus.gov/advancedirectives.html

### 6. Apart from the reverse mortgage pension insurance sold by insurers, avoid all other "home-equity pension" schemes. Never mortgage your home to buy investment products
- In the United States: You can apply for a Home Equity Conversion Mortgage (HECM), which is a reverse mortgage insured by the Federal Housing Administration (FHA). You must be 62 or older, own your home outright or have a low balance, and live in the home. You receive money from your home's equity, and the loan is repaid when you move, sell, or pass away.
- Official sources: https://www.hud.gov/program_offices/housing/sfh/hecm/hecmhome (Home Equity Conversion Mortgages for Seniors)
- Checked on: 2026-11-02
- Reliability: verified

### 7. If an elderly relative at home is bedridden for long periods or severely disabled, apply for long-term care insurance at the medical insurance office where they are insured. It is not only for the elderly.
- In the United States: Medicare generally does not pay for long-term care or custodial care. It only pays for medically necessary care like skilled nursing facility care or home health care, usually for a short time after an illness.
- Official sources: https://www.medicare.gov/coverage/long-term-care (Medicare coverage for long-term care)
- Checked on: 2026-11-02
- Reliability: verified

### 9. If cataracts already affect an older person's ability to get around, arrange an eye exam and surgery. If both eyes are affected, do not delay the second eye too long.
- In the United States: Medicare Part B helps pay for cataract surgery when it is done using traditional surgical techniques or lasers. You pay 20% of the Medicare-approved amount after meeting the Part B deductible.
- Official sources: https://www.medicare.gov/coverage/cataract-surgery (Medicare coverage for cataract surgery)

### 13. If an older person's memory is getting worse and affecting daily life, take them to a memory clinic or neurology department to rule out treatable causes first, such as depression, medication, and poor hearing or vision.
- In the United States: Medicare Part B covers cognitive assessments if you show signs of cognitive impairment. Your doctor can perform this assessment during a routine visit or a separate appointment to check for conditions like depression, anxiety, or delirium, and to create a care plan.
- Official sources: https://www.medicare.gov/coverage/cognitive-assessment-care-plan-services (Medicare coverage for cognitive assessment)
